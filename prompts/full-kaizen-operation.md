# Social Media Full Kaizen Operation Prompt

Paste this prompt at the root of a campaign, content system, calendar, report, training, or digital-marketing project.

## Configuration

```text
Brand/client and product type: [DISCOVER]
Objective, audience, market, and languages: [DISCOVER]
Channels and campaign period: [DISCOVER]
Baseline data and analytics sources: [DISCOVER]
Content, asset, rights, approval, and policy locations: [DISCOVER]
Known feedback, incidents, or performance gaps: [NONE OR LIST]
Cycle ID: [YYYY-MM-DD-short-name]
Improvement authority: project-local reversible edits are authorised; publishing and ad spend are not
```

## Prompt

Run a complete Kaizen operation on this social-media product. Base decisions on audience value and measured outcomes, not trends or vanity metrics. Freeze a capped baseline, test the smallest safe improvement, validate content and measurement, standardise supported learning, and prepare the next cycle.

### Routes and boundaries

Read project instructions. Resolve Social Media Skills and read its `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, relevant strategy/content/analytics/policy skills, anti-slop routes, product-type adapter, and `skills/meta-utility/kaizen-improvement-system/SKILL.md`. Read the Digital Research portfolio standard and verify current platform, advertising, policy, market, legal, and benchmark claims. Route visual work to Design System Skills and website journeys to Website Skills.

This prompt authorises local draft, calendar, measurement-plan, template, and analysis edits. It does not authorise publishing, scheduling, messaging users, community moderation, account changes, influencer commitments, ad spend, pixel deployment, or canonical engine changes. Stop for missing account authority, rights, consent, safeguarding, privacy, regulated claims, approval, rollback, or crisis escalation. Mark unavailable platform data and reviews `NOT ASSESSED`.

### Evidence pack

Create `docs/kaizen/<cycle-id>/` with `00-scope-and-evidence.md`, `01-baseline-scorecard.md`, `02-improvement-backlog.md`, `03-experiment-log.md`, `04-validation-record.md`, `05-final-report.md`, and `06-next-cycle.md`. Inventory the brief, objective, audience evidence, funnel, channels, content pillars, assets, rights, AI provenance, language variants, calendars, CTAs, landing pages, approvals, policy constraints, moderation process, analytics definitions, raw exports, baselines, experiments, reports, incidents, and owners.

### Capped baseline

Score ten equal dimensions with evidence, confidence, deficiency, and status:

1. Business/mission objective, audience decision, funnel role, and success definition.
2. Audience insight, segmentation, local/cultural fit, language, and channel behaviour evidence.
3. Channel strategy, format fit, cadence, distribution, and platform-policy compliance.
4. Message, story, specificity, usefulness, voice, CTA, and non-generic quality.
5. Creative system, brand coherence, accessibility, captions, alt text, readability, and mobile rendering.
6. Rights, consent, disclosures, claims, safeguarding, privacy, AI provenance, and ethical persuasion.
7. Conversion path, landing-page continuity, response handling, community trust, and recovery.
8. Measurement design, event/metric definitions, denominators, attribution limits, baselines, and data quality.
9. Production workflow, approvals, version control, scheduling readiness, moderation, and crisis escalation.
10. Reporting, insight quality, decision rules, client handoff, reuse, and learning cadence.

Publish `min(raw_overall, 65)` and freeze the baseline. Rights, safety, legal, privacy, cultural harm, account, and approval failures remain blockers regardless of score.

### Improvement loop to 95

Create a P0/P1/P2 backlog. Each action states evidence, root cause, affected audience/channel, exact asset or process, hypothesis, owner, primary measure, denominator/time window, trust/accessibility counter-metric, smallest reversible variant, rights/approval needs, rollback/pause rule, acceptance evidence, target contribution, and re-audit date.

Run one controlled content, CTA, channel, workflow, or measurement experiment at a time. Use local variants or approved test infrastructure only. Preserve the control, record exposure assumptions, and distinguish `supports`, `weakens`, `mixed`, and `does not test`. Reach alone is not success. Reject a variant that harms qualified action, trust, safety, accessibility, cultural fit, or downstream capacity.

If live publishing or elapsed-time data is required, prepare the exact approved experiment pack but do not publish. Mark results pending rather than fabricating them.

### Strict anti-AI-slop gate

Apply anti-AI-slop rules during strategy and production, audit after every major content batch/iteration, and run the final gate before approval. Grade F blocks release. Reject fabricated audience insights, trends, quotes, testimonials, performance data, platform benchmarks, campaign results, or sources; generic motivational captions; interchangeable content pillars; emoji and hashtag stuffing; trend copying without audience fit; repeated hook-body-CTA patterns; empty engagement questions; vague calendars; fake urgency; synthetic community voice; and AI visuals with malformed text/anatomy, inconsistent identity, false documentary cues, or unclear rights.

Require each asset to have a specific audience need, funnel role, native channel format, factual basis, cultural/language review, accessibility treatment, rights record, approval owner, measurable outcome, counter-metric, and follow-up decision. High reach cannot rescue low trust, cultural harm, inaccessible creative, weak qualified action, or invented evidence.

### Validate, standardise, and re-measure

Run applicable brief-to-output, source, anti-slop, policy/legal, rights, cultural/language, accessibility, visual, link/landing-page, analytics-definition, approval, and report checks. Verify every statistic and performance claim against dated source data. Inspect representative rendered formats and truncation. Record commands, review evidence, calculations, samples, output paths, and unavailable checks.

Promote accepted learning into the project strategy, content matrix, template, style rule, approval checklist, metric dictionary, dashboard logic, moderation runbook, or experiment log. Re-score only with evidence and state the uncapped final result. The final report must show before/after results, accepted and rejected variants, outcome limitations, blockers, release/publish verdict, and next test.

Return the score summary, completed changes, validations, pending live tests, blockers, evidence-pack path, and re-audit date. Do not publish or communicate externally.
