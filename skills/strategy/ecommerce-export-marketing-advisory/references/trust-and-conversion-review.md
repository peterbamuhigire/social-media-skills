# Trust and Conversion Review

## Trust Layer

Check whether a cross-border buyer can quickly answer:

- Is this seller real?
- Is the product authentic?
- How do I pay safely?
- What happens if delivery fails?
- Can I return or exchange?
- Who supports me in my language/timezone?
- Are reviews or customer proof credible?
- Are prices, currency, duties, and delivery costs clear?

## Conversion Review

| Area | Check |
|---|---|
| Mobile UX | Pages load quickly and key actions are usable on a phone. |
| Product page | Clear photos, specs, price, proof, shipping, returns. |
| Checkout | Few steps, transparent fees, suitable payment options. |
| Payment | Local/cross-border rails fit the target buyer. |
| Delivery | Time, cost, tracking, and returns are clear. |
| Support | WhatsApp/email/chat route visible and staffed. |
| Analytics | Funnel events and campaign source tracking exist. |

## Evidence Rule

Do not cite channel penetration, cart-abandonment, or buyer-behaviour statistics without a current source. If evidence is weak, state the assumption and test it in the pilot.
