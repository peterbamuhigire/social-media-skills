# Export Marketing Plan Template

## 1. Market Choice

- Target country and customer segment.
- Why this market is viable.
- Unit-economics verdict and constraints.
- Assumptions and open questions.

## 2. Buyer Persona

- Buyer need.
- Trust barrier.
- Payment preference.
- Delivery expectation.
- Proof required.
- Language/currency/norms.
- Main objection and response.

## 3. Positioning and Messaging

- Value proposition.
- Proof points.
- Localised product/category copy.
- Offer and guarantee.
- Risk reducers.

## 4. Channel Plan

| Channel | Role | Asset | Budget | CAC guardrail | KPI | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Partnership Outreach

- Target partner type.
- Reason for partnership.
- Mutual value.
- Proof to include.
- Next step.

## 6. 90-Day Execution

- Weeks 1-2: fix trust/conversion blockers.
- Weeks 3-4: partner outreach and pilot assets.
- Weeks 5-8: campaign pilot.
- Weeks 9-12: review CAC, conversion, orders, fulfilment, and margin.
