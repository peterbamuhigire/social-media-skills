# Creator Monetisation Strategy — East Africa

Plans how an East African creator earns from an audience: which of five income routes to pursue, how to price brand work and how to pitch it, with every platform rule verified on the day.

<!-- dual-compat-start -->
## Use When

- A creator wants to know which income routes are open in East Africa and what each realistically pays.
- We must check YouTube Partner Programme, TikTok or Facebook payout eligibility before setting subscriber targets.
- A creator needs a rate card, a distribution fee plus talent fee price for brand work, and polite ways to decline poor offers.
- The audience could buy an ebook, course, template or paid community through Mobile Money.
- Affiliate programmes and disclosure wording are needed for product recommendations.

## Do Not Use When

- `strategy-personal-brand` for an individual's positioning, reputation and authority.
- `08-influencer-marketing-strategy` for brands hiring, vetting and paying creators.
- `playbook-social-selling` for relationship selling and high-value outreach.
- Stop before promising income figures or relying on platform payout rules not verified against current platform terms.

## Required Inputs

| Artefact | Source/provider | Required? | If absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creator name, niche (specific, e.g. "personal finance for Ugandan salaried workers") and monetisation goal (primary, supplemental or building toward full-time) | Creator | Yes | Ask the intake questions in the method reference; do not assess streams for a "lifestyle" niche. |
| Platforms, followers and engagement rate per platform | Creator's native analytics exports | Yes | Mark stream readiness `not assessed`; do not set subscriber or brand-deal targets. |
| Audience demographics and purchasing behaviour (age, city/region, income, whether they buy online) | Platform analytics or audience poll | Yes | Mark affiliate and digital-product streams `Not Yet` until buying behaviour is shown. |
| Payment capability (local bank, MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, Payoneer, USD card) | Creator | Yes | Assume Mobile Money only and exclude USD-paid programmes and platforms. |
| Current platform programme terms (YPP, TikTok, affiliate programmes) with date checked | Official help pages, opened on the day | Yes | Mark the programme `not assessed` and drop it as a goal. |
| Past brand quotes, deals and outcomes | Creator's records | If any exist | Build the first quote from scope only; start the dated rate history. |

## Workflow

1. Confirm the creator's goal and that the request is creator-side income, not brand-side hiring (`08-influencer-marketing-strategy`) or relationship selling (`playbook-social-selling`); run the intake questions in the [method reference](references/creator-monetisation-method.md).
2. Check payment capability first, then open each platform's official programme pages and record thresholds, country list, payout route and date checked; stop and drop any programme that is unavailable in the creator's country or cannot be verified.
3. Rate each of the five streams (YouTube Partner Programme, TikTok, affiliate, digital products, brand partnerships) as Priority, Secondary, Not Yet or Not Applicable against audience size, niche, engagement and payment route.
4. For YPP, build the month-by-month path to 1,000 subscribers from the default model, extended to 12–18 months for entertainment, music or vlog formats.
5. Price brand work with the distribution fee + talent fee method (Hennessy, 2018), with usage, exclusivity, season and rush priced separately; show affiliate and product earnings only as a formula on the creator's own numbers.
6. Draft the five-section pitch with no prices, plus a separate rate sheet shared only on request, and the disclosure wording for affiliate and sponsored posts.
7. Check every income, eligibility and payment claim for a source and date; correct any undated or guaranteed-income claim and rerun the check, then run the anti-slop gate before hand-off.

## Outputs

| Artefact | Consumer | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| Creator income plan: five streams rated Priority / Secondary / Not Yet / Not Applicable with the payment route for each | Creator | Every rating cites the creator's audience, engagement or payment evidence; no stream is left unrated. |
| YPP month-by-month growth timeline | Creator | Tailored to the niche, with the date the YPP terms were checked. |
| Brand-work quote built from distribution fee + talent fee, and a separate rate sheet | Creator; brand buyers on request | Usage, exclusivity, season and rush are line items; no follower-tier benchmark is presented. |
| Digital product and sales-channel plan in UGX | Creator | Each product has production effort, price and a Mobile Money or USD route that the creator can use. |
| Five-section brand partnership pitch and disclosure wording | Creator; prospective brands | All five sections populated; pitch and press kit carry no prices. |

## Evidence Produced

| Evidence | Format | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| Platform programme register (thresholds, countries, payout route, date checked) | Table in the plan | Each programme is dated from an official page or marked `not assessed`. |
| Affiliate programme terms record (rate, cookie window, country eligibility, payout, date) | Table | No commission rate appears without its source and date. |
| Rate history of past quotes and outcomes | Dated log | Each quote records scope, usage, exclusivity and result. |

## Capability and Permission Boundaries

Read and search only; analysis is read-only. Publishing, spend, live account changes, outreach and personal-data processing need explicit, action-specific client authority. Sending pitches to brands, joining affiliate programmes or opening payment accounts on the creator's behalf is out of scope.

## Degraded Mode

Without the creator's analytics and current platform programme terms, return the narrowest qualified result and mark the affected checks `not assessed`. A brand-work pricing method, the pitch structure and a Mobile Money digital-product route can still be delivered, with income routes marked provisional.

## Decision Rules

| Condition | Action | Failure or risk avoided |
|---|---|---|
| Audience proof and platform eligibility can be verified | Rank feasible revenue streams and mark platform facts for current verification. | Projected income relies on unavailable or stale monetisation programmes. |
| The creator's country is not on YPP's or TikTok's current programme list | Say so and drop the programme as a goal. | A growth plan aimed at income the creator cannot receive. |
| The audience pays mainly by Mobile Money | Start with WhatsApp direct sales and Selar; add Gumroad only once Payoneer or a USD account exists. | Products listed where the audience cannot pay. |
| The audience does not shop online | Do not recommend affiliate programmes such as Jumia; rate affiliate `Not Yet`. | Irrelevant links that erode audience trust. |
| An offer is gifted product with no talking points | Treat it as coverage and optional; price only campaigns with talking points, hashtags and a go-live window. | Unpaid campaign work displacing organic content. |
| A brand's budget is below the quote and the fee cannot move | Counter politely, or ask for a deposit, split payment, shorter terms or non-cash value; ask for the contract before accepting. | Underpricing and usage rights given away. |
| Evidence is contradictory or materially incomplete | Pause the affected recommendation and request the accountable source. | Confident advice built on an unresolved premise. |
| Authority is limited to analysis or planning | Deliver a read-only plan and approval checklist. | Unauthorised publication, spend, outreach, or data use. |

## Quality Standards

- All five monetisation streams are assessed with EA-specific eligibility criteria and payment method information relevant to the client's payment capability, each with a clear recommendation: Priority / Secondary / Not Yet / Not Applicable, based on the creator's current profile.
- The YPP path includes a month-by-month growth timeline tailored to the creator's niche.
- Brand-work pricing uses the distribution-fee + talent-fee method with usage, exclusivity and season priced explicitly; no unsourced follower-tier rate card is presented as a benchmark.
- Digital product pricing is in UGX with production effort estimates per product type, and the affiliate programme table includes EA-accessible options with Mobile Money payment noted where available.
- The brand partnership pitch template is complete with all five sections populated for the client, and the default pitch and press kit contain no prices.
- Affiliate and sponsorship disclosure follows the FTC/ASA standard as best practice with register AD-09/AD-10 cited; the data-protection law is invoked only for personal-data collection.
- Earnings, job access, eligibility and payment claims are sourced, dated and qualified; no guaranteed income or pay-to-access opportunity is presented.
- Uganda/East Africa, British English, EAT, UGX and WhatsApp-first assumptions are explicit; `ai-marketing/anti-ai-slop` is applied during drafting and release is blocked on an F from `ai-marketing/ai-slop-audit`.

## Anti-Patterns

- Stating YPP or TikTok thresholds from memory or an older guide (for example the original Creator Fund). Fix: open the official help pages and record the programme, thresholds and date checked.
- Publishing follower-tier price bands as local benchmarks. Fix: build each quote from scope with the distribution fee + talent fee method.
- Presenting the illustrative affiliate arithmetic as expected income. Fix: show the formula with the creator's own numbers, labelled an estimate.
- Putting prices in the pitch, press kit or one-sheet, or a wall of past-client logos. Fix: share the rate sheet on request after scope is known and link to a sponsored-work archive.
- Accepting product seeding or unpaid campaigns by default. Fix: accept only for a dream brand, travel that lifts content quality or real brand promotion; otherwise decline gracefully.
- Repeating guaranteed daily earnings or pay-to-access vacancy claims. Fix: record source, access date, market and payment condition, and never promise income.
- Recommending publication, outreach, spend, data collection or a regulated claim without authority. Fix: stop at an approval-ready draft.
