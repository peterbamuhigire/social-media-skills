# Kaizen Campaign Learning Loop

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md)

Use this reference to make campaign testing an operating loop rather than a one-off report.

## Source basis and limits

- *LEAN: Ultimate Collection* (2018) supports Build-Measure-Learn, validated learning, innovation accounting, value/waste thinking, and KPI cadence. It is an uneven compilation; verify exact platform, statistical, and legal claims elsewhere.
- *Applying the Kaizen in Africa* (2018) supports PDCA, participatory and incremental improvement, low-cost tests, 5S, muda removal, on-site observation, QC Story, standardisation, and learning that is taught into the organisation. Its cases are not guarantees for an individual client.
- *Digital Storytelling* and *Video Game Storytelling* support purposeful sequence, audience identification, tension, character motivation, choice, consequence, and cross-disciplinary handoff. Apply these as content and campaign design heuristics, not as evidence of performance.
- *Dynamic Characters* supports visual gesture, silhouette, framing, value separation, and readability. *Anatomy for Artists* is excluded because the supplied extraction is unreadable.
- *Paid for Your Perspective* supports evidence-bounded expert positioning, preparation, compliance screening, focused calls, follow-up, and a durable knowledge-product pipeline. It does not authorise claims about a client's expertise or legal position.

## Operating cycle

1. **Observe:** collect the brief, audience evidence, content history, comments, platform exports, creative, and approval boundary. Record dates and provenance.
2. **Baseline:** define the audience problem, content role, funnel stage, denominator, current metric, sample limit, and guardrails. Apply analytics 5S: remove duplicate names, unused events, ambiguous labels, and unowned dashboards.
3. **Select:** choose one high-leverage uncertainty. Prefer one variable and a reversible, low-cost organic or staged test when the account is small.
4. **Experiment:** write the hypothesis, variant, primary metric, guardrails, timebox, owner, minimum observation, stop rule, and rollback before publication or spend.
5. **Check:** verify delivery, reach/exposure, readability, accessibility, cultural fit, AI disclosure, factual claims, comments, sentiment, and metric integrity.
6. **Standardise:** retain a winning pattern only when it improves the primary outcome without violating trust, safety, accessibility, or brand guardrails. Update the content, campaign, reporting, or training skill.
7. **Teach:** add the learning to the calendar template, content brief, prompt, approval checklist, and client handoff.
8. **Re-measure:** set the next baseline and review date. Preserve failed and inconclusive tests as evidence, not as embarrassment to be deleted.

## Minimum campaign learning record

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Audience and problem | Named audience, context, need, barrier, and evidence status. |
| Narrative job | Hook, tension, proof, choice, consequence, and CTA; brand may guide but audience retains agency. |
| Variant | One intentional change with creative, caption, format, and platform named. |
| Primary metric and denominator | Exact metric, event definition, unit, period, and source. |
| Guardrails | Accuracy, permissions, cultural safety, sentiment, accessibility/readability, cost, and complaint or opt-out signals. |
| Decision | Standardise, reverse, iterate, or inconclusive with the reason. |
| Ownership | Person responsible for approval, analysis, standardisation, and next review. |
