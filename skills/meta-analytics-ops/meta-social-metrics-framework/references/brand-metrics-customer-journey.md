# Brand Metrics Across The Customer Journey

Self-contained synthesis prepared from supplied brand metrics and social media marketing source material. Use it to connect social reporting to brand efficacy and business outcomes.

## Journey Metric Map

| Journey stage | Business question | Useful metrics | Social evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Do relevant people recognise us? | aided/unaided awareness, reach, branded search, share of voice | reach, impressions, mentions, video views, profile visits |
| Consideration | Are prospects shortlisting us? | preference, consideration, website visits, enquiry intent | saves, clicks, DMs, comments asking price/location |
| Purchase | Does demand convert into revenue? | conversion rate, revenue, CAC, offer uptake | tracked leads, WhatsApp enquiries, promo-code use |
| Post-purchase | Are customers satisfied after buying? | satisfaction, reviews, complaints, repeat intent | review sentiment, support response time |
| Retention/loyalty | Do customers come back? | repeat purchase, churn, retention, CLV | retention campaigns, community participation |
| Advocacy | Do customers recommend us? | referrals, NPS, UGC, testimonials | shares, tags, UGC, reviews |
| Holistic health | Is the brand becoming stronger? | salience, distinctiveness, trust, price premium | brand search, positive share of voice, direct traffic |

## Measurement Rules

- Pick metrics from the journey stage the strategy is trying to move.
- Separate leading indicators from lagging outcomes.
- Use surveys for brand memory and preference where platform analytics cannot answer the question.
- Use UTM links, source fields, promo codes, and customer asking at point of sale to close attribution gaps.
- Report confidence level when attribution is partial.

## Social-To-Brand Dashboard

Build dashboards in three layers:

1. Business outcomes: leads, revenue, retention, referrals, footfall, list growth.
2. Brand journey indicators: awareness, consideration, post-purchase trust, loyalty, advocacy.
3. Platform diagnostics: reach, engagement rate, saves, clicks, completion rate, response rate.

The owner dashboard should show layers 1 and 2 first. Platform diagnostics explain movement; they do not replace business outcomes.

## Brand Health Interpretation

| Pattern | Likely meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| High reach, low consideration | People see the brand but do not care enough | Sharpen positioning, offer, proof, and CTA |
| High engagement, low leads | Content entertains but does not convert | Add lead magnet, landing page, DM path, or offer clarity |
| High leads, low sales | Sales process or offer mismatch | Fix qualification, pricing, follow-up, or proof |
| Good sales, weak retention | Delivery/service issue | Improve onboarding, support, post-purchase content |
| High satisfaction, low advocacy | Happy customers are not prompted to refer | Build review, referral, and UGC system |

## Common Metric Traps

- Treating follower growth as brand health.
- Reporting engagement without segment or journey context.
- Claiming ROI from impressions.
- Ignoring post-purchase and advocacy because they sit outside platform dashboards.
