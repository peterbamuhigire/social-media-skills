# Narrative, audience empathy, and content quality audit

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md)

Use this reference when auditing a content history, campaign, content calendar, or social product. It extends metric review with the audience's meaning-making, trust, readability, and next-action experience.

## Source basis and limits

- *Digital Storytelling* and *Video Game Storytelling* support audience fit, conflict/tension, character motivation, purposeful sequence, choice/consequence, dialogue, and cross-team narrative language. They do not provide current platform benchmarks.
- *Dynamic Characters* supports visual focal point, silhouette, gesture, framing, contrast, and readable intent. The supplied *Anatomy for Artists* file contains only `Part1` and is not used.
- *Paid for Your Perspective* supports specific expert positioning, preparation, compliance screening, evidence-bounded answers, follow-up, and a knowledge-product pipeline.
- *LEAN* and *Applying the Kaizen in Africa* support validated learning, PDCA, small reversible tests, observation, standardisation, and team teaching.

## Audit dimensions

For each representative content item, inspect and record:

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Audience empathy | Who is this for, what situation are they in, what do they already know, and what could make the message feel irrelevant, unsafe, inaccessible, or culturally false? |
| Narrative clarity | Is there a recognisable problem or desire, useful tension, credible proof, a meaningful choice, and one consequence or next action? |
| Information hierarchy | Can the audience identify the point in the first frame/lines, scan the structure, understand the CTA, and recover if they cannot act immediately? |
| Visual/readability quality | Is the focal point clear, text legible on mobile, contrast adequate, motion tolerable, and image meaning available through caption/alt or audio description where needed? |
| Evidence and expertise | Are facts, experience, quotes, results, permissions, and claims traceable? Does the content stay within the speaker's real expertise? |
| AI transparency and control | If AI assisted generation, targeting, recommendation, or response, is the contribution disclosed where required, reviewable, correctable, and human-escalated? |
| Learning value | What baseline, hypothesis, metric, guardrail, and decision does this item contribute to? |

## Scored audit contract

Score the applicable dimensions with evidence, then publish `min(raw score, 65)` as the audit score. The cap is a reporting ceiling, not a pass. The improvement plan must target 95/100 and name the gap, root cause, owner, experiment, acceptance evidence, risk, rollback, and re-audit date.

If a visual render, platform export, audience evidence, accessibility review, native-language review, or approval record is unavailable, mark the check `not assessed`; do not infer a pass from the absence of complaints.
