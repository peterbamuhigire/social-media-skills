# Evergreen Register and Rotation — Identify, Score, Refresh and Schedule Durable Content

Merged from skills/meta-analytics-ops/meta-evergreen-content-strategy on 2026-09-29 at 7c60138; preservation map: [meta-evergreen-content-strategy.md](../../../../docs/kaizen/consolidation-2026-09-29/preservation/meta-evergreen-content-strategy.md)

Parent skill: [`meta-content-repurposing`](../SKILL.md). The parent turns one source asset into derivatives; this reference manages the *library* over time — which existing posts are evergreen, how to refresh them, and how to rotate them through a 90-day calendar. Evergreen posts that are refreshed should also re-enter the parent's repurposing chain.

## 1. When to use this reference

- The client has an existing content archive and wants to identify, refresh and schedule durable content instead of producing everything new.
- The deliverable is an **evergreen content register and 90-day rotation**.
- Framework source: Macarthy (2022) *500 Social Media Marketing Tips*, 6th edn.

## 2. Inputs

Before generating the evergreen system, confirm:

- **Client business name** and trading name (if different).
- **Industry** and sub-sector.
- **Country / city** (default: Uganda / Kampala).
- **Platforms in scope** (all that apply: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, X/Twitter).
- **Existing content archive** — can the consultant share the last 12 months of posts, or is the audit from scratch? If from scratch, state this clearly; the audit steps below guide data collection.
- **Current posting frequency** per platform (for example Facebook 5×/week, Instagram 3×/week).
- **Number of evergreen candidates identified** in the initial review (or "unknown — running the full audit now").
- **Primary content goal** — one of: awareness / education / trust-building / conversion.
- **Content inventory, performance history and expiry constraints** from the client, approved systems or dated platform exports. If absent, stop the affected decision; request it or mark the field unknown and narrow the output.

Do not generate the rotation plan or calendar until these inputs are confirmed.

## 3. What is evergreen content?

Evergreen content remains relevant and valuable whenever it is consumed. It answers questions that do not change: how-to guides, explanations of industry fundamentals, step-by-step processes, lists of resources and FAQs.

It contrasts with time-sensitive content — news announcements, promotions, event posts and seasonal campaigns — which has a short shelf life and must not enter the evergreen rotation.

**Why evergreen content matters for East African SMEs:**

- Limited production capacity makes constant-new-content models unsustainable for most small businesses.
- Trust-building content (how-to, why, what to expect) is consumed repeatedly in East African markets, where buyers research extensively before purchasing.
- WhatsApp shares extend the reach of evergreen posts beyond the algorithm window, circulating content in groups and broadcast lists long after the original post date.
- A well-maintained evergreen library reduces the monthly content production burden by 30–40%.

## 4. Decision rules

| Situation | Action | Failure or risk avoided |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory, performance history and expiry constraints are current and attributable | Produce the full register and 90-day rotation and cite the evidence used. | Decisions based on stale or unrelated evidence. |
| Inventory or performance data missing or contradictory | Stop that decision, request clarification, or issue a labelled partial register. | Fabricated scores and false confidence. |
| Post fails screening question 1 or 4, or passes question 2 (dated reference) | Exclude from the register. | Time-sensitive content entering the rotation. |
| Total score 12–15 | Tier 1 — include immediately; refresh before first republication. | Under-using the strongest assets. |
| Total score 8–11 | Tier 2 — refresh before republishing; review annually. | Republishing dated material. |
| Total score below 8 | Time-sensitive or low-value — keep out of rotation. | Filling slots with weak content. |
| Primary goal is conversion | Shift mix to 30/30/40 (evergreen / new / timely-promotional). | Rotation that ignores commercial need. |
| Primary goal is awareness | Shift mix to 50/40/10. | Over-promotion to cold audiences. |
| Same post last published on a platform within 60 days | Do not schedule it there, whatever its tier. | Audience fatigue. |
| Benchmark is undated or not the client's own | Use account evidence, or label the benchmark as a provisional comparator. | Presenting a benchmark as the client's result. |

## 5. Procedure

### Step 1 — Identify evergreen candidates

Run the audit on the client's existing content (last 12 months minimum). Use the data-collection approach in [`meta-content-audit`](../../meta-content-audit/SKILL.md) if a full audit has not yet been completed.

For each post, ask four screening questions:

1. **Would this post be just as useful to a reader in 6 months as it is today?** Yes = evergreen candidate. No = time-sensitive; exclude.
2. **Does it reference a specific date, promotion, event or news story?** Yes = time-sensitive; exclude.
3. **Has it generated saves, shares or link clicks — not just likes?** Yes = high-value candidate. Saves and shares are stronger signals of durable value than likes.
4. **Is it a how-to, FAQ, process guide, list or explanation?** Yes = likely evergreen by format.

A post must pass questions 1 and 4 to advance to scoring. Questions 2 and 3 are filters: question 2 removes time-sensitive content; question 3 ranks candidates.

**Evergreen content types for East African SMEs:**

- "How to [do something your customer needs to do]"
- "What to look for when choosing [your product/service category]"
- "5 things most people don't know about [your industry]"
- "[Industry] mistakes and how to avoid them"
- "Step-by-step guide to [relevant process]"
- "Why [common belief in your market] is wrong"
- Explainer posts about the client's core product or service
- FAQs drawn from WhatsApp or in-person customer enquiries

### Step 2 — Score evergreen value

Score each candidate that passed Step 1 on three dimensions (1–5 each, maximum 15).

| Dimension | Score 1 | Score 3 | Score 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Relevance durability** | Likely outdated in 3 months | Relevant for 12 months | Relevant for 2+ years |
| **Audience value** | Low engagement; few saves or shares | Average engagement; some saves | High saves/shares; several meaningful comments |
| **Production investment** | Quick text post or single image | Moderate effort — original copy with visual | Long-form, high-effort content (carousel, video script, detailed guide) |

| Total score | Classification | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 12–15 | **Tier 1 — Priority evergreen** | Include in rotation immediately; refresh before first republication |
| 8–11 | **Tier 2 — Conditional evergreen** | Refresh before republishing; review annually |
| Below 8 | **Time-sensitive or low-value** | Do not include in rotation |

**Evergreen register template** (a simple spreadsheet):

| Post title | Platform | Original date | Score | Tier | Last republished | Refresh needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Step 3 — Refresh protocol

A refreshed evergreen post is not a copy-paste republication. It is an updated version that serves the same durable purpose with current context. Repeat audiences need a new entry point; new audiences need accuracy.

Before republishing any evergreen post:

- [ ] Update statistics, prices or data points that may have changed since first publication.
- [ ] Remove or replace references to specific dates, past promotions or expired events.
- [ ] Check that all linked URLs still work — website pages, WhatsApp numbers, booking links.
- [ ] Update the opening hook — a new first sentence or headline that gives repeat viewers a fresh entry point.
- [ ] Add one current local reference (a recent East African news item, a current Ugandan cultural moment or a relevant seasonal hook) to signal freshness.
- [ ] If the original used stock imagery or a dated visual, consider replacing it with original photography or an updated graphic.
- [ ] If the original was AI-assisted, run it through the anti-ai-slop [humanising rewrite passes](../../../ai-marketing/anti-ai-slop/references/humanising-rewrite-passes.md) before republishing.

Time per refresh: 20–30 minutes for Tier 1 posts; 30–45 minutes for Tier 2 posts (which need more substantial updating).

### Step 4 — Build the evergreen rotation

**Default content ratio across the full schedule: 40% evergreen (refreshed) / 40% new original / 20% timely or promotional.**

This serves clients with limited production capacity while keeping audience trust through new content and commercial viability through promotional posts. It aligns with the **10-4-1 rule** (Bodnar and Cohen, 2012): evergreen posts fill the recurring value-content slots, new original content fills the original-post slots, and promotional content fills the 1-in-15 promotional slot. Adjust for conversion (30/30/40) or awareness (50/40/10) goals, stating the reason.

**Example — 5-posts/week Instagram account:**

| Weekly slot | Share | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Evergreen rotation (refreshed) | 40% | 2 posts/week |
| New original content | 40% | 2 posts/week |
| Timely / promotional | 20% | 1 post/week |

**Republication cadence:**

- **Tier 1 (score 12–15):** republish every **60–90 days**.
- **Tier 2 (score 8–11):** republish every **90–120 days**, after completing the refresh protocol.
- Never republish the same post more than once per 60 days on any single platform, regardless of tier.

**Platform-specific evergreen guidance** (verify current platform behaviour before quoting it to a client):

| Platform | Evergreen behaviour | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Evergreen performs well; the algorithm keeps showing posts to new audiences over time. | Republish with updated copy; do not simply reshare the original. Pin the top-performing evergreen post to the top of the Page for new visitors. |
| Instagram | Feed posts lose algorithmic reach after 48–72 hours, but Stories saved to Highlights stay permanently; evergreen-topic Reels keep surfacing through discovery for weeks. | Save top evergreen posts to Highlights by category (for example "How To", "About Us", "FAQs"). Prioritise evergreen topics for Reels production. |
| WhatsApp Broadcast | Ideal for evergreen, because it helps recipients rather than merely promoting. | Rotate evergreen how-to and FAQ content monthly to subscriber lists. Keep broadcasts to 2–4 sentences with a link or a clear CTA to reply or call. |
| LinkedIn | Evergreen performs exceptionally well; posts circulate via shares for weeks and appear in search. | Republish every 60 days with a new opening sentence. Archive the original rather than deleting it; similar topics months apart are not penalised. |
| YouTube | Evergreen is the dominant content type; a tutorial posted in 2022 may still drive views and subscribers in 2026. | Prioritise evergreen topics for all YouTube production. Update descriptions and pinned comments when information changes rather than deleting and re-uploading. |
| TikTok | Lower inherent evergreen value because of the trend-driven algorithm; how-to and "did you know" content lasts longer than trend content. | Test 1–2 evergreen TikToks a month. If a how-to video sustains views after 2 weeks, add it to the evergreen library. |
| X/Twitter | Content decays quickly, but threads on durable topics (industry explanations, frameworks, lists of tools) attract ongoing engagement via search and reposts. | Republish high-performing evergreen threads every 90 days with a new opening post. |

### Step 5 — Evergreen content calendar (90 days)

Produce a 90-day rotation plan from the scored and tiered register. Integrate it into the master calendar produced by [`11-content-calendar`](../../../pipeline/11-content-calendar/SKILL.md): evergreen slots replace, not add to, the existing posting schedule.

- Assign Tier 1 posts to the earliest available slots.
- Space republications so the same post does not appear within 60 days on the same platform.
- Mark "Refresh needed" as Yes for any post being republished for the first time, or last refreshed more than 90 days ago.
- Feed refreshed evergreen posts into the parent skill's repurposing chain — a refreshed evergreen blog post, for example, can seed a carousel and a WhatsApp broadcast in the same week.

| Week | Platform | Post type | Evergreen topic | Tier | Last published | Refresh needed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 1 | Facebook | Post | [Client-specific topic] | 1 | [DD/MM/YYYY] | Yes |
| 1 | WhatsApp | Broadcast | [Client-specific topic] | 1 | [DD/MM/YYYY] | Yes |
| 2 | LinkedIn | Post | [Client-specific topic] | 1 | [DD/MM/YYYY] | Yes |
| 2 | Instagram | Reel | [Client-specific topic] | 2 | [DD/MM/YYYY] | Yes |
| 3 | Facebook | Post | [Client-specific topic] | 1 | [DD/MM/YYYY] | No |
| 3 | YouTube | Video | [Client-specific topic] | 1 | [DD/MM/YYYY] | No |

Populate all 13 weeks, with at least 2 evergreen slots each week (matching the 40% ratio), using the client's actual evergreen topics from the register.

## 6. Framework reference

- **10-4-1 rule** (Bodnar and Cohen, 2012) — calibrate the evergreen share within the full content mix.
- **Hero/Hub/Hygiene** (YouTube/Google) — classify evergreen content: Hygiene content (FAQ, how-to, explainer) maps directly to the evergreen library; Hub content (regular series) may have partial evergreen value; Hero content (campaigns, announcements) is time-sensitive and stays out of the rotation.
- **RACE** (Chaffey and Ellis-Chadwick, 2022) — check that the library covers every customer-journey stage: Reach (awareness posts), Act (educational and how-to posts), Convert (process guides, what-to-expect posts) and Engage (community and trust-building posts).

## 7. Acceptance checklist

- Evergreen identification uses the 3-dimension scoring system with explicit numerical scores, not a subjective judgement.
- The refresh protocol applies at least 6 specific checks before any republication, with an estimated time per post.
- The rotation ratio is stated as a percentage split (default 40/40/20, or adapted to the client's goal or frequency with a stated reason).
- Platform notes cover at least 5 platforms with meaningfully different evergreen strategies, not paraphrases of the same advice.
- The 90-day rotation calendar is in table format, all columns populated with the client's actual topics.
- Tier 1 and Tier 2 cadences are stated numerically in days (60–90 and 90–120), never as "regularly" or "periodically".
- WhatsApp Broadcast is named as an evergreen distribution channel with guidance on message length and content type.
- The output integrates with `11-content-calendar` (slots align with the master schedule), `meta-content-repurposing` (evergreen posts enter the repurposing chain) and `meta-content-audit` (audit data informs the scoring).
- Every recommendation traces to an input, names an owner or next action, and marks assumptions and unassessed checks; if the inventory cannot be accessed, return only the supported sections plus a recovery list — never fill gaps with East African defaults.
