# Personalised Video Audit Outreach

Merged from skills/business-development/biz-dev-video-outreach on 2026-09-29 at 69cca10 (S06 tree); preservation map: [biz-dev-video-outreach.md](../../../../docs/kaizen/consolidation-2026-09-29/preservation/biz-dev-video-outreach.md)

## When to use this reference

The parent [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) designs the whole lawful prospecting system. Use this reference when the deliverable is a **personalised video audit for one named prospect**: a 3–5-minute screen recording of the prospect's own website, Facebook Page, Instagram account or Google Business Profile, narrated with specific observations, plus the short message that carries it and a two-message follow-up.

Why it works: most prospects expect agencies to send generic pitches. A narrated walkthrough of their own digital presence signals three things at once: this person has looked at my business, they know something I do not, and they are not asking for anything yet.

Evidence status. Fihn (2025) reports that personalised video audits get a response rate 5–10 times higher than a cold email and convert at roughly one paying client per 10 videos sent. That is one practitioner's claim, not a benchmark: verify before stating (no register record), and never quote it to a client as an expected rate. The personalised message is direct marketing to an identified person, so the parent's data-protection checks apply before any video is sent (see [Data-protection and platform checks for outreach](data-protection-checks-for-outreach.md); registers UG-DPPA-2019, UG-DPPA-S26-DIRECT-MARKETING-2026, KE-ODPC-DIRECT-MARKETING-2026, WHATSAPP-BUSINESS-POLICY).

## Inputs

Ask for these before generating the audit script:

1. **Prospect business name and industry** — trading name and sector.
2. **Country and city** — defaults to Uganda/Kampala if not specified.
3. **Digital presence to audit** — the URL(s) or handle(s): website, Facebook Page, Instagram, Google Business Profile or WhatsApp Business number.
4. **What you have observed** — 2–3 specific things noticed about the prospect's presence (gaps, missed opportunities or quick wins). If no audit has been done yet, work through the pre-video audit framework in step 1.
5. **Your proposed improvement** — the one thing that could be improved in 7–10 days with immediate results.
6. **How the video will be sent** — email (Loom link), WhatsApp (voice note or video message) or LinkedIn message.
7. **Existing relationship** — cold prospect, referral from a mutual contact, or someone who has engaged with your content.
8. **Lawful basis for the contact** (from the parent skill) — where the contact details came from and the basis for using them in each market; if unknown, draft only and mark sending `not assessed`.

## Decision rules

| Condition | Action | Failure or risk avoided |
|---|---|---|
| The audit finds more than three issues | Keep only the 2–3 most impactful and most fixable in a short campaign | Listing problems instead of demonstrating expertise; an overwhelming video |
| No genuine third insight exists | Record two observations only; never force a third | Manufactured problems |
| The video is to go by WhatsApp | Ask permission first; send the video only after the prospect replies "yes" | Unwanted business-initiated messages and account restriction (WHATSAPP-BUSINESS-POLICY) |
| No reply after the video | Send at most two follow-ups (day 3 and day 7), then stop | Pressure, complaints and breach of the parent's three-touch limit |
| A follow-up or script cites a client result or a local success story | Use it only with a verified record and the client's consent; otherwise remove it | Fabricated or unauthorised proof |
| The prospect asks to stop or objects | Suppress at once and confirm within the legal window (Uganda: 14 days, UG-DPPA-S26-DIRECT-MARKETING-2026) | Breach of objection rights |
| The audit covers a Facebook Page in Uganda | Check access status at the campaign date (UG-FACEBOOK-ACCESS-2026: status unstable; do not assert Facebook as blocked or open) | Advising on a channel the prospect's customers cannot reach |
| The script states a platform mechanism (for example how Reels reach non-followers) | Verify before stating (no register record); phrase as an observation, not a rule | Unsupported platform claims |

## Procedure

### Step 1 — Pre-video audit framework

Complete this checklist before recording. Each block takes under two minutes; review whichever platforms the prospect uses.

**Facebook Page (2 minutes)**

- [ ] Does the Page have a complete About section with a WhatsApp number?
- [ ] Is the call-to-action button set to the right action (WhatsApp / Book Now)?
- [ ] When was the last post? How often are they posting?
- [ ] Are comments going unanswered?
- [ ] Is the cover photo current, and does it include a call to action?
- [ ] Is there a pinned post with an offer or welcome message?

**Instagram (2 minutes)**

- [ ] Does the bio describe what the business does and for whom?
- [ ] Is there a keyword in the username or display name?
- [ ] Is the link-in-bio set up and current?
- [ ] When was the last post? Are there any Reels?
- [ ] Do they respond to comments?
- [ ] Are Highlights set up with covers?

**Website (2 minutes)**

- [ ] Does the homepage load in under 3 seconds on mobile? (An audit heuristic, not a platform rule; for Ugandan network context see UG-MOBILE-SPEED-2026.)
- [ ] Is there a clear above-the-fold call to action?
- [ ] Is there a WhatsApp chat widget?
- [ ] Is the contact information easy to find on the first screen?
- [ ] Is there social proof (testimonials, reviews, logos)?

**Google Business Profile (1 minute)**

- [ ] Does a listing exist, and is it claimed?
- [ ] Are there recent reviews? Are they responded to?
- [ ] Are the opening hours and description up to date?
- [ ] Are there photos uploaded?

**Golden rule:** identify only 2–3 observations. More than three overwhelms the prospect and signals that you are listing problems rather than demonstrating expertise.

### Step 2 — Video audit script

Scripts and templates below are engine house wording from the retired skill, not quotations from Fihn (2025); adapt before use. Write a narration script from the step 1 observations. It is spoken, not read aloud: write in natural spoken language.

| Segment | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 20 seconds | Who you are, why you are sharing, no commitment |
| Observation 1 — the quick win | 60–90 seconds | The improvement with the most immediate impact and the simplest fix; lead with a genuine compliment |
| Observation 2 — the opportunity | 60–90 seconds | Slightly larger: a missed opportunity rather than a broken element |
| Observation 3 (optional) — the untapped asset | 30–45 seconds | Only if there is a clear third insight |
| Close | 30 seconds | Soft, low-commitment question |
| Total | 3–5 minutes | |

**Opening (20 seconds)**

> "Hi [First Name], my name is [Your Name] — I'm a social media and digital marketing consultant. I was doing some research and came across [Business Name], and I noticed a few things that I think could make a real difference for you quickly. I'm going to do a quick 3-minute walkthrough — nothing to buy, no commitment needed. I just thought it was worth sharing."

[Begin screen share of their Facebook Page / website / Instagram.]

**Observation 1 — the quick win (60–90 seconds)**

> "First — I want to say, [genuine compliment: 'your product photography is excellent' / 'your reviews are really strong' / 'I can see you've built a good community here']. That's a real asset.
>
> The thing I noticed is [specific observation]. For example, when I tried to message you, I couldn't find a WhatsApp number. On this button here — [click it on screen] — it takes me to a form rather than straight to a conversation. [Local customer behaviour, for example: 'In Uganda, most customers won't fill a form; they'll just move on to a competitor who has a WhatsApp link.'] That's a 5-minute fix that could meaningfully change how many enquiries come through."

The customer-behaviour line is the source author's field observation; keep it only where you can support it for that market and sector.

**Observation 2 — the opportunity (60–90 seconds)**

> "The second thing I noticed is [observation]. I'm looking at your last 30 posts — [scroll through] — and there's really strong content here. But there are no Reels. [Platform mechanism, verified at the recording date: for example, that Reels are shown to people who don't already follow you, so an account without them mostly reaches existing followers.] A simple Reel — even filmed on a phone — would open this up to a much wider audience. [Only with a verified, consented example: 'I've seen accounts in [their industry] in Kampala double their enquiries in 30 days just from adding 2 Reels a week.']"

**Observation 3 (optional) — the untapped asset (30–45 seconds)**

> "One more thing — and this is the one I find most businesses don't realise. You have [X followers / X past customers / a WhatsApp database]. That's a warm audience that already knows and trusts you. Most businesses advertise to strangers when the easiest revenue is from people who've already bought from them. There's a specific campaign type that works very well here."

Past-customer reactivation is planned in `07-email-marketing-strategy` (win-back and reactivation reference).

**Close (30 seconds)**

> "That's it — those are the [two / three] things I'd look at first. I have a specific idea for a test I'd like to run for you to address [Observation 1 or 2] — it's a 7-day exercise, no upfront cost, and you can see whether it's worth a longer conversation. Would it be useful if I sent you a one-page outline?
>
> No pressure either way — happy to answer questions too. Thanks for your time."

The one-page outline and the 7-day, no-upfront-cost test are built in `biz-dev-pricing-menu` (risk-reversed entry offer reference).

### Step 3 — Outreach message sent with the video

Scripts and templates below are engine house wording from the retired skill, not quotations from Fihn (2025); adapt before use. Keep each version under five lines. The video does the work; the message is only the delivery wrapper. Add the sender's identity and a simple opt-out line where the channel and market require it (parent skill, Quality Standards).

**Email**

> Subject: Quick video on [Business Name] — 3 minutes
>
> Hi [First Name],
>
> I noticed a few things on [their platform] that I thought were worth sharing — recorded a short walkthrough: [Loom link]
>
> Takes 3 minutes. No ask, no pitch.
>
> [Your name]

**WhatsApp (permission first)**

> "Hi [First Name], I'm [Your Name] — a social media consultant. I noticed a couple of things on your [Facebook / Instagram / website] that could help you get more enquiries. I made a short video — 3 minutes — would it be OK to send it over?"

Always ask permission before sending a video on WhatsApp. It signals respect and makes it far more likely the video is watched. Send the video only after the prospect replies "yes". Check first-contact use against the current WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy (WHATSAPP-BUSINESS-POLICY).

**LinkedIn**

> "Hi [First Name] — I was researching [industry] businesses in [city] and came across [Business Name]. I put together a 3-minute video with two or three observations about your online presence. Not pitching anything — just thought it was genuinely worth sharing. Happy to send it if you're open to it."

### Step 4 — Follow-up sequence

Send only if there is no response after the video. Maximum two follow-ups.

**Follow-up 1 (day 3)**

> "Hi [First Name] — just checking you received the video. Totally fine if it's not a fit. Just want to make sure it reached you."

**Follow-up 2 (day 7)**

> "Hi [First Name] — last message from me on this. I've since worked with [a similar business described anonymously: 'a Kampala salon' / 'an NGO in Nairobi' / 'a school in Entebbe'] and got [brief verified result, for example 'their enquiries doubled in 10 days']. If the timing is ever right, you know where to find me."

The result in follow-up 2 must be verified and used with that client's consent, even when anonymised.

No third follow-up. Move on. A non-responder is a not-yet, not a no: keep them in the awareness pipeline by posting useful content they can find organically, not by further direct messages.

## Release checklist

- [ ] The script is in natural spoken language (not formal written language) and can be delivered conversationally without reading.
- [ ] Exactly 2–3 specific observations come from the pre-video audit framework — not more, not fewer.
- [ ] Each observation names a specific element of the prospect's actual digital presence, not a generic critique of "most businesses".
- [ ] The close ends with a soft, low-commitment question, not a pitch or a request to book a call.
- [ ] Email, WhatsApp and LinkedIn message versions are all present, and each is under five lines.
- [ ] The WhatsApp version asks permission before sending the video.
- [ ] Follow-ups are present and limited to two; the second includes a brief third-party result that is verified and consented.
- [ ] Contact source and lawful basis are recorded; the prospect is not on the suppression list.
- [ ] Platform mechanisms, local-behaviour lines and response-rate figures are verified or removed.
- [ ] British English throughout; no American spelling variants.
