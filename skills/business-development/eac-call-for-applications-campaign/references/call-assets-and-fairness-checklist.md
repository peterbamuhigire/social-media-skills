# Call Assets and Fairness Checklist

## Asset Pack

- Master call notice.
- Applicant guidelines.
- Short WhatsApp post.
- Long WhatsApp/community post.
- LinkedIn post.
- Facebook post.
- Email invitation.
- Partner dissemination email.
- Reminder messages.
- FAQ.
- Data-use and consent note.

## Required Content in Every Call Notice

- Who is eligible.
- What support is offered.
- What information applicants must provide.
- Selection criteria and process.
- Deadline and timezone.
- Language and accessibility support.
- Contact/query route.
- Data-use and confidentiality note.
- Statement that applying does not guarantee selection.

## Fairness Checks

- Same eligibility criteria across all channels.
- Same deadline across all channels.
- Same benefits and obligations across all languages.
- Material clarifications added to FAQ for all applicants.
- Reviewer-bias reminder included in selection process notes.
- Outreach includes women-led, non-capital-city, and smaller-market networks where relevant.
- Form is mobile-friendly and not unnecessarily long.
