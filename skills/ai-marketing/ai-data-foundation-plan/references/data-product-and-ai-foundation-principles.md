# Data Product and AI Foundation Principles

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Sanjeev Mohan,
*Designing the AI-Driven Data Foundations: Architecture, Principles, and
Practice*.

## Planning checks

Treat data and AI as products with named consumers, owners, contracts, quality
signals, access rules, lineage, freshness, cost, and feedback. Prefer modular,
interoperable foundations that can serve operational, analytical, and AI use
cases without moving data unnecessarily. Score portability and exit effort before
committing to a vendor or architecture.

For marketing use cases, expose consent, audience boundaries, segmentation
quality, model drift, human approval, correction, and cost per session. A higher
click-through rate does not offset privacy harm, discriminatory targeting, or
loss of trust.
