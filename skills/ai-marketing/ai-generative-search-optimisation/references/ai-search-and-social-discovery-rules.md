# AI Search and Social Discovery — Operating Rules

Read this reference when a brief touches AI answers, search visibility, LinkedIn or expert publishing aimed at AI citation, customer-language research, or discoverability across social profiles, communities and the next destination. It is concept guidance, not current platform authority. Current platform, market and legal claims must come from [`docs/source-registers/source-register.json`](../../../../docs/source-registers/source-register.json) or a fresh primary source; otherwise mark them `NOT_ASSESSED`.

## 1. Planning lenses (durable)

**Three response modes.** Plan for (a) what an AI system already "remembers" about the brand, (b) what it retrieves as current information, and (c) what it reasons through for deeper questions. Use the modes as a planning lens only; do not present them as a fixed cross-platform taxonomy.

**Separate the outcomes.** Record each stage separately and never merge them: representation → retrieval → citation or answer contribution → referral → qualified action → revenue. A mention or an AI answer is not proof of reach, traffic, trust or sales. Do not report "AI rank"; name the observation (brand mention, cited source, sampled grounding query, sentiment, referral, qualified enquiry, sale).

**Make the brand legible.** State who the brand is, what it does, for whom, where and under what limits. Use consistent names, plain relationship sentences ("X is a Kampala clinic that offers Y to Z"), evidence, dates and authentic profiles. Never manufacture mentions, reviews, UGC or "authority".

**Match the content unit to the job.**

| Job | Content unit | Proof and guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Brand representation | Consistent profile identity, offer, audience, location, limits, links, pinned explanation | Owner-approved fact sheet, profile audit, update date |
| Retrieval support | Useful, time-stamped answers linking to the canonical page; native descriptions and accessible text | Source/rights record, canonical URL, no citation promise |
| Reasoning support | Expert threads, explainers, case evidence, comparisons, transparent trade-offs | Named author, sources, counter-case, moderation review |
| Off-site reputation | Real customer evidence; public correction when wrong; never seeded praise or suppressed criticism | Consent/rights, response log, escalation owner |
| Measurement | Prompt observations tracked apart from platform analytics, referrals, self-report and qualified actions | Metric definition, sample, date, attribution limits |
| Agent handoff | Accurate, accessible destination and a consent-safe WhatsApp/form flow | Task scenario, privacy/legal check, failure path |

**Baseline before changing anything.** Combine customer language, comments/DMs, search and social analytics, referral data, "How did you find us?" answers, prompt observations and available logs. Each source is partial; keep its definition and limits.

**One reversible experiment at a time.** Each has a hypothesis, a primary outcome, a trust/cultural/accessibility guardrail, a stop rule and a rollback. A content calendar is not an experiment record.

**Agent-mediated tasks.** Prepare only when a real customer task exists: accurate profile and catalogue data, accessible landing pages, current feeds and consent-safe handoffs matter more than speculative files or protocols.

## 2. Corrections to apply to social guidance

- Remove fixed "universal rules" (a 50-word opening, FAQ on every page, a universal monthly cadence, three-second performance claims, promised GEO lifts). Use them only as a documented client experiment or a local acceptance choice.
- Do not repeat traffic, adoption, gender, market-share, tool or platform numbers from books or vendor decks. Verify or mark `NOT_ASSESSED`.
- Treat profiles, reviews, comments, creator work and UGC as part of the discoverability surface, with rights, privacy, moderation, cultural and correction gates. Authenticity is a requirement, not a growth hack.
- For Google Search, rely on core SEO, helpful people-first content, crawlability, technical eligibility and Search Console measurement. Per the register (source IDs `GOOGLE-AI-SEARCH-GUIDE-2026`, `GOOGLE-SEARCH-ESSENTIALS-2026`), Google does not require `llms.txt`, artificial chunking, special AI markup or inauthentic mentions.
- Treat Bing's AI performance reporting (register `BING-AI-PERFORMANCE-2026`) as a measurement surface, not a ranking, authority or business-value score.

## 3. LinkedIn and expert publishing aimed at AI citation (sample-bound hypotheses)

A vendor study of AI citations for B2B prompts (register `MELTWATER-LINKEDIN-REPORT-2026`, `LINKEDIN-MELTWATER-TAKEAWAYS-2026`, `MELTWATER-AI-VISIBILITY-BLOG-2026`) reported LinkedIn as a leading cited source in its sample, with member profiles cited more than Company Pages and structured, question-led content common among highly cited examples. Keep the sample, denominator, date and source with any figure quoted. The study does not show causation, traffic, lead quality or sales, and does not justify claims that LinkedIn always outranks other sources or that any format guarantees citation.

Operating rules:

- Build a bench of credible topic owners using their real role, organisation, geography and experience. Never manufacture authority.
- Answer a real buyer question near the start. Prefer decision support, comparisons, how-to guidance, definitions, risks, costs and implementation lessons.
- Use clear native structure (useful opening, descriptive sections or numbered steps, named entities, sourced data, caveats, one honest next action). Structure serves readers first; it is not a ranking hack.
- Coordinate employee or expert posts with Company Page support, keep each post useful on its own, and link the canonical website resource when the full answer belongs there.
- Prefer original, experience-based material and labelled analysis over remixed filler. Record source, rights, approval and claim status.
- Treat posting frequency, article cadence and format mix as test variables, not platform laws. Protect quality, reviewer capacity and trust.
- Take part in independent discussions, reviews and professional communities legitimately. Never manufacture UGC, reviews, comments, citations or forum mentions.

**Prompt-test record:** exact prompt set, model/product, date, locale, answer, cited URLs, brand accuracy, author/profile, content format, referral activity and business outcome where available. Compare like with like; do not change strategy from one cited answer or one high-performing post.

## 4. Customer-language bank and intent map

Use a dated language bank and intent map, not a pile of "high-volume" terms.

1. Collect language from owned questions, customer conversations, search data, live observation and authorised platform research.
2. Cross-check sources; record locale, date, confidence, ambiguity and privacy/consent boundaries.
3. Group terms and questions into themes that can support a post, reply, guide, service page or escalation.
4. Map each asset to an intent and a next action.
5. Review qualified conversations, referrals and actions — not only reach, follower counts or rankings.

| Signal | What it can show | What it cannot prove by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Question or comment language | Vocabulary, confusion, unmet need, service risk | Consent to reuse a person's words; a market-wide conclusion |
| Reach, views, reactions, replies | Distribution and interaction on a named surface | Trust, revenue, search rank or AI citation |
| Referral or tagged visit | A measurable path to an owned destination | Incremental causation without an attribution design |
| Qualified conversation or action | Progress toward the business job | Performance for another audience or platform |
| Correction, moderation or opt-out event | A trust or safety failure needing response | That the original claim was acceptable |

Quarantine unless re-verified: old network rosters and interface paths, ranking or AI-citation claims tied to likes/shares/hashtags, fixed keyword counts or density rules, and any statistic, legal assertion, quote, image or testimonial without current evidence and rights.

## 5. Acceptance rule

Ship a recommendation only when it is (a) a labelled durable concept from this reference or (b) a current claim with a source-register record (scope, dates, freshness, support status, uncertainty, owner). Otherwise narrow it or mark it `NOT_ASSESSED`. Publishing, outreach, spend, data collection or live account changes still require explicit authority. Re-audit this reference quarterly or when platform, measurement, legal or audience conditions change.
