# Social product Kaizen adapters

Use the smallest adapter that matches the output. The initial audit for both the
engine and the product is published at `min(raw_score, 65)`; improvements target
95/100 only after evidence, guardrails, and re-audit.

| Product | Inspect | Evidence before standardising |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign | Audience problem, promise, funnel role, creative variants, channel fit, permissions, spend, and outcomes | Brief-to-output map, source/rights register, experiment result, guardrails |
| Single content asset | Clarity, usefulness, cultural fit, accessibility, factuality, CTA, and provenance | Review checklist, source/permission record, readability/accessibility check |
| Calendar/content system | Pillars, production flow, approvals, reuse, workload, WhatsApp/manual steps, and failure recovery | Calendar audit, cycle time, missed/late items, owner and escalation evidence |
| Report/dashboard | Metric definitions, denominators, source freshness, narrative, uncertainty, and decision action | Reconciled export, claim-to-source map, reviewer result, next decision |
| Training or policy | Task clarity, safe boundaries, examples, escalation, and adoption friction | Learner task test, completion/quality evidence, exception and feedback log |
| Website/social landing content | Audience job, page hierarchy, proof in context, accessibility, SEO, CTA friction, and handoff | Rendered review, accessibility/performance checks, content evidence, conversion guardrails |

## Root-cause prompts

Before changing creative, ask whether the defect is primarily audience/problem,
message/creative, channel/distribution, process/capacity, measurement/data, or
trust/rights/safety. Choose one reversible countermeasure and retain failed or
inconclusive tests as learning.
