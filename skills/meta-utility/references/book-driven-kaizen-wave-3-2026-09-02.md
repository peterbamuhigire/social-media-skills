# Book-driven Kaizen Wave 3: social-media application

Use this reference for channel strategy, content systems, campaigns, reports, and AI-assisted
marketing workflows.

Owning skill: [Kaizen owner](../kaizen-improvement-system/SKILL.md).

## Transfer controls

- Tie each content decision to audience intent, a meaningful action, a learning hypothesis,
  and a guardrail for trust, rights, safety, accessibility, and cultural fit.
- Separate exploration from exploitation. Predefine eligibility, exposure, outcome and
  counter-metrics, uncertainty, stop rules, and a safe baseline; do not call reach a win.
- Use story structure to clarify a real audience problem and proof, while preserving truthful
  claims, human review, provenance, and a controlled publication handoff.
- Maintain entity/location consistency and useful retrieval-friendly content, but treat ranking
  and platform behavior as current, unguaranteed claims.

## Boundaries

Book tactics and platform examples are not current platform policy or causal evidence. Verify
current policies, formats, recommendation behavior, privacy, and AI capabilities. Never
fabricate reviews, engagement, testimonials, or attribution. Mark unsupported claims
`NOT_ASSESSED`.

## Measures and routing

Track qualified actions, retention, negative signals, accessibility/rights defects, source and
AI provenance coverage, experiment confidence, learning-to-standardisation time, and rollback
success. Route to channel, content, analytics, anti-slop, legal, and currentness gates.

## Currentness gate

Use current first-party platform policy and Digital Research source registers; record access
date, freshness, support, uncertainty, and review trigger for every volatile claim.
