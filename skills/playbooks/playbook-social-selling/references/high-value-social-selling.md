## When to use this reference

Read this reference when the offer is premium or high-ticket and the buyer is senior, affluent or enterprise: premium products, high-ticket services, agency offers, executive advisory, enterprise software, luxury/lifestyle products or affluent-market experiences. The client needs LinkedIn/social authority, lead magnets, email nurture, outbound contact campaigns or sales follow-up for high-value prospects, and the goal is fewer, better leads, higher trust, stronger positioning and premium conversion rather than broad low-quality reach.

The parent [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) owns the general 5-step sequence, DM handling, the WhatsApp conversion path and the daily routine. This reference changes those defaults for premium buyers; the detailed pre-publication gate is [premium-social-selling-gate.md](premium-social-selling-gate.md). Drafting outreach does not authorise sending it.

Evidence status. LinkedIn outreach and conduct follow the Professional Community Policies (register PREMIUM-LI-POLICY-2026) and the User Agreement Dos and Don'ts (register PREMIUM-LI-AGREEMENT-2026). No platform limit, price or benchmark is stated here.

## Inputs

| Input | Source | If absent |
|---|---|---|
| Objective, premium audience, market and intended decision | Client or approved brief | Ask for it or state a narrow working assumption |
| Existing channel, content, commercial or performance evidence for the premium offer | Client systems, supplied files or verified research | Mark the check unassessed and avoid performance claims |
| Approval, policy, budget, access or risk constraints | Accountable client owner | Stop before publishing, spending, contacting people, collecting data or making regulated claims |

## Decision rules

| Situation | Action | Failure avoided |
|---|---|---|
| The buyer is senior, affluent, enterprise or high-ticket | Use proof-led, selective outreach and a longer trust path | Volume tactics damaging premium positioning |
| Consulting or professional-services offer | Apply [LinkedIn authority to pipeline](../../../platforms/platform-linkedin/references/consulting-authority-to-pipeline.md) and keep the website proof and opportunity handoff | Authority content that never reaches a qualified opportunity |
| Authority, lead-magnet, outreach or offer copy is being written | Apply [premium-commercial-writing](../../../content-writing/premium-commercial-writing/SKILL.md) for proof, value framing and price integrity | Generic or discount-led language for a premium offer |
| The strategy is ready to publish | Run the [premium social selling gate](premium-social-selling-gate.md) first | Releasing a plan with a red flag unresolved |
| Evidence is contradictory or materially incomplete | Pause the affected recommendation and request the accountable source | Confident advice built on an unresolved premise |

## Procedure

1. **Define the premium audience:** buyer role, status context, pain or risk, platform behaviour, proof expectations and decision path.
2. **Build authority positioning:** expertise, point of view, proof, case studies, founder story, contrarian insight and category clarity.
3. **Design lead capture:** premium lead magnet, diagnostic, private briefing, consultation, benchmark, audit, calculator, invitation or executive roundtable.
4. **Design nurture:** an email/social sequence that educates, proves, handles risk and invites a concrete next step.
5. **Design outreach:** named-account/contact campaign, referral path, executive-assistant or gatekeeper handling, a personalised reason to meet, and a follow-up cadence.
6. **Align sales:** qualification, discovery, objection handling, proposal handoff, CRM stages and a response-time SLA. For consulting/professional services, apply LinkedIn authority to pipeline (decision rules above).
7. **Apply premium commercial writing** to the authority content, lead-magnet promise, outreach language and offer framing.
8. **Run the premium social selling gate** before publishing the strategy.

## Quality bar

- Content attracts the right buyer and repels low-fit buyers.
- Social proof is specific, not decorative.
- The CTA matches buyer temperature: follow, download, reply, book diagnostic, attend briefing, request proposal or start pilot.
- Outreach is researched and relevant, not spam.
- Premium brands do not over-promote, over-discount or sound desperate.
- Block release on an F from `ai-marketing/ai-slop-audit`.

## Outputs

- Premium social selling strategy.
- LinkedIn/content authority plan.
- Lead magnet and nurture sequence architecture.
- Executive outreach and follow-up plan.
- Sales-marketing alignment notes.

Each output names the chosen route, owners, sequence, assumptions and measurable acceptance checks, and links every recommendation to supplied evidence or labels it as an assumption.

## Anti-patterns specific to premium selling

- Producing channel tactics before defining the decision and consumer. Fix: state the required outcome and handoff first.
- Copying a global template without adapting Uganda/East Africa access, language, payment or trust conditions. Fix: record which local assumptions apply (UGX, EAT, WhatsApp-first where relevant).
- Reporting activity as success without an acceptance condition. Fix: name the observable result and evidence source; judge premium selling by high-intent actions, not likes (see the gate's Measurement section).
