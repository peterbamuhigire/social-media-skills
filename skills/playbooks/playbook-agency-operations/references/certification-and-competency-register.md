# Certification and competency register

Read when keeping the team's platform certifications current, listing credentials in a pitch, planning effectiveness training, or mapping staff skills to a professional marketing competency framework.

Added 29 Sep 2026 (Social Kaizen S10-T10, benchmark rows SL05-C7, SL06-C6, SL19-C1 to C4). This file is the single owner of certification facts in the engine; `biz-dev-credentials`, `playbook-client-retainer-management` and the `training-*` skills point here. Re-verify every row at its review date: programmes, validity and prices change.

**East Africa.** Google and Meta certifications are online and free to sit (Meta cost `NOT_ASSESSED`), so they are the realistic baseline for a Ugandan or Kenyan team; plan exam sittings around data costs and use office Wi-Fi rather than mobile bundles. IPA and CIM programmes are UK-based and paid in GBP; treat them as optional development for senior staff. A Ugandan or Kenyan professional marketing body's competency framework was not verified: `NOT_ASSESSED`.

Rules:

1. Every person who runs a client's ad account holds the current certification for that platform and product, or works under someone who does.
2. Set the reminder 30 days before expiry; review the whole register monthly in the operations meeting.
3. List a credential in a pitch or credentials pack only while it is in date, with the person's consent; never list a lapsed one or a company badge the agency does not hold (for example "Google Partner" without Partner status).
4. Record the verification date of each programme fact above; when a programme changes, update this file first.

## 4. Effectiveness training (SL19-C3)

For staff who write strategy or report results, the IPA Effectiveness Essentials Certificate and the Advanced Certificate in Effectiveness cover objective setting, evaluation and multi-platform measurement (register `IPA-QUALIFICATIONS`). Where cost rules them out, run an internal session using `advertising-attribution-and-measurement` and `meta-reporting`, and record it in the register as internal training, not a qualification.
