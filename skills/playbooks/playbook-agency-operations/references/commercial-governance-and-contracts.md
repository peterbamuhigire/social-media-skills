# Commercial governance and contracts

Read when drafting or reviewing an agency–client agreement, adding audit and transparency terms, deciding how media is bought and disclosed, or explaining to a client which industry contract models the agency follows.

Added 29 Sep 2026 (Social Kaizen S10-T10, benchmark rows SL16-C3, SL18-C2 and SL18-C4). This is commercial screening guidance, not legal advice: every contract goes to the agency's and the client's counsel before signature.

## 1. The reference models and how they apply in East Africa

| Model | What it is | Access | Status for UG/KE agencies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 ISBA/IPA Creative Services Framework Agreement (CSFA) | UK industry-standard template for advertiser–creative agency relationships (not media buying), published 27 Nov 2025 with new generative-AI clauses, clearer reconciliation and a faster contracting structure (register `ISBA-IPA-CSFA-2025`) | Guidance note, general terms and scope of work are for ISBA and IPA members; the sustainability clause guidelines are free | Reference model, not binding. Clause text `NOT_ASSESSED` (member-only) |
| ANA media buying contract template (updated June 2023) | US advertiser association template for agency media buying (register `ANA-MEDIA-CONTRACT-2023`) | Press release public; template for ANA members | Reference model, not binding. Template text `NOT_ASSESSED`; terms below are from ANA's published summary |
| IPA/ISBA/MCCA/PRCA Agency Remuneration Best Practice Guide | Joint UK guide to setting and reviewing agency pay (register `IPA-REMUNERATION-GUIDE`) | Free PDF | Reference model; see [remuneration models and evidence](../../../business-development/biz-dev-pricing-menu/references/remuneration-models-evidence.md) |

**East Africa.** None of these frameworks is binding in Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania or Rwanda. Use them as checklists of what a well-run agreement covers, then write the contract under local law with local counsel. In Kenya, the Marketing Society of Kenya and the Association of Practitioners in Advertising produced the advertising code the Advertising Standards Body of Kenya hosts (register `KE-ASBK-CODE-2003`, partial); whether either body publishes a standard agency contract or remuneration guide is `NOT_ASSESSED`. No Ugandan advertisers' or agencies' association standard contract was verified: `NOT_ASSESSED`. Money terms are in UGX (or KES), and payment by mobile money or bank transfer is written into the schedule.

## 2. Contract checklist (creative, content and social retainers)

Adds to the contract essentials in [agency operating procedures](agency-operating-procedures.md#contract-essentials); do not duplicate them.

| Clause area | What the agreement should settle | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Scope of work and reconciliation | Deliverables, volumes, and a periodic reconciliation of planned versus delivered work and hours; how over- and under-delivery are settled | CSFA 2025 highlights clearer reconciliation; stops silent scope creep |
| Remuneration model | Which model (fixed fee, time-based, output, payment by results, hybrid), review date, what triggers a review | See the remuneration reference; the model shapes incentives |
| Pass-through costs | Ad spend, boosts, influencer fees, stock and tool licences billed at cost, with invoices on request; any handling fee stated as a fee | Keeps agency income visible |
| Intellectual property | Who owns final work, pre-existing agency materials, rejected concepts, templates; licence terms for stock, music and talent; AI-assisted work (see Section 4) | Most disputes start here |
| Talent, likeness and voice | Usage period, territory, media, and a ban on AI replication without separate written consent | Deepfake and consent risk |
| Data protection | Roles (controller and processor), security, breach notice, return or deletion at exit, cross-border transfer; Uganda DPPA 2019 (register `UG-DPPA-2019`) and Kenya's regime | Client lists and ad-account data are personal data |
| Audit and records | What the client may audit, how often, notice, who pays, record retention | See Section 3 |
| Platform and account access | Client owns ad accounts, pages and pixels; agency has partner access; admin rights returned at exit | Account hostage situations |
| Termination and exit | Notice, work in progress, handover of files, credentials revoked, final reconciliation | Clean exits protect reputation |
| Pitch and speculative work | Who owns pitch ideas that are not paid for | See [pitch conduct and AI clauses](../../../business-development/biz-dev-proposal/references/pitch-conduct-and-ai-clauses.md) |

## 3. Transparency and audit rights (media buying)

ANA's June 2023 update of its media buying template, as summarised by ANA (register `ANA-MEDIA-CONTRACT-2023`):

- separate definitions for **compliance audits** and **performance audits**, with no limit on the number of audits and flexibility to add other audit types;
- **non-transparent services** (replacing the earlier principal and mark-up wording): the agency needs the advertiser's approval before buying on a non-transparent basis, and must report such activity in detail by media channel. ANA said members had often agreed to non-transparent services without fully understanding them;
- a **transaction data schedule** listing the data to obtain from vendors;
- **prior consent** before the agency uses any artificial intelligence applications;
- placeholders for ESG and DEI terms.

**Engine rules for any agency that places media for a client:**

1. State in the contract whether each buy is **transparent** (agency acts as agent; client pays actual media cost plus a disclosed fee) or **non-transparent / principal** (agency or its trading desk buys inventory itself and resells it at a price that includes an undisclosed margin). Principal trading is not banned; it must be **disclosed and opted into in writing**, channel by channel.
2. Disclose rebates, volume bonuses, free spots and commissions received from media owners, and state whether they pass to the client.
3. Give the client proof of delivery: platform invoices and exported reports for digital; for radio, TV and outdoor, the station's or contractor's proof of flight. Whether Ugandan and Kenyan stations issue standard transmission certificates is `NOT_ASSESSED`; agree the proof format with each media owner before booking.
4. Keep records for the audit period the contract sets; a small agency should still keep invoices, insertion orders and reports per campaign.
5. Where the client's ad accounts are used (the usual case for Meta, TikTok and Google), the client already sees actual spend; say so in the contract and bill the agency fee separately.

ISBA's current position on principal media was not located in this wave: `NOT_ASSESSED`. ISBA's 2024 member survey found that only 52% of respondents had visibility of which technology their agencies charge for (register `ISBA-REMUNERATION-2024`; public highlights only), which supports asking for a technology and fee disclosure schedule.

## 4. Generative AI in the agreement

The 2025 CSFA added generative-AI clauses, and the ANA template requires prior consent before AI applications are used. ISBA's 2024 survey found 8% of advertisers had amended agency contracts to include AI terms, with a further 42% in progress (registers `ISBA-IPA-CSFA-2025`, `ANA-MEDIA-CONTRACT-2023`, `ISBA-REMUNERATION-2024`). The clause checklist the agency offers clients is in [pitch conduct and AI clauses](../../../business-development/biz-dev-proposal/references/pitch-conduct-and-ai-clauses.md#3-generative-ai-contract-clauses); the disclosure and provenance duties behind it are in [AI transparency and provenance](../../../policies/policy-ai-content-ethics/references/ai-transparency-and-provenance.md).
