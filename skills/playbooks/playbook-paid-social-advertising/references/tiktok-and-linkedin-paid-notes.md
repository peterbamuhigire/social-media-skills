# TikTok and LinkedIn paid notes

When to read: when TikTok or LinkedIn ads are in scope. Facts are from the currentness register (checked 2026-09-23); everything else is a check on the day.

## TikTok

Objectives by stage (AD-06; source page last updated April 2025, confidence medium):

| Stage | Objectives |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach |
| Consideration | Traffic, Video views, Community interaction, Branded Mission |
| Conversion | App promotion, Lead generation, Sales (Sales merged the former Website conversions and Product sales objectives) |

Not assessed: Smart+ and GMV Max automation and shop products; TikTok Shop availability in Uganda/Kenya. Branded-content disclosure uses the platform's branded-content toggle (check current name); creator partnerships follow the disclosure register in `08-influencer-marketing-strategy`.

Audience reach: DataReportal Digital 2026 reports TikTok ad reach of 18.4 M adults (18+) in Kenya and gives no TikTok figure for Uganda (MK-01, MK-02). Ad reach is not the same as users.

Creative practice: native, vertical, sound-on-aware but captioned, hook in the opening moments, creator-style delivery; test several hooks per offer.

## LinkedIn

Objectives (AD-07): Brand awareness; Website visits, Engagement, Video views; Lead generation, Website conversions, Job applicants, Talent leads (Talent leads needs a Recruiter contract).

Thought Leader Ads (AD-07): promote posts by employees, creators and other members with the author's approval; supported objectives are Brand awareness, Engagement, and Video views for video posts. Eligible posts: single-image or text, video, LinkedIn Live/Event, articles and newsletters. Not eligible: celebrations, documents, polls, multi-image posts and reposts.

Audience reach: LinkedIn figures in DataReportal are registered members, not monthly active users, so they are not comparable with other platforms (MK-01: Kenya 6.30 M members; MK-02: Uganda 1.80 M members).

Use cases in East Africa: B2B lead generation to captive target universes (licensed banks, insurers, manufacturers, NGOs), employer branding, executive thought leadership amplified with Thought Leader Ads after the post has shown organic pull. Pair with `strategy-b2b-customer-community` for named-account programmes and `platform-linkedin` for organic practice.

Lead-gen forms: keep fields to what sales uses; hidden fields for campaign tracking; route to CRM within the agreed response time; consent text per jurisdiction (PL-01, PL-02).
