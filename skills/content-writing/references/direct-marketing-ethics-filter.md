# Direct-Marketing Ethics Filter (Canonical)

Apply this filter to every selling asset — ad, caption, sales page, email, WhatsApp or SMS message, proposal, influencer brief, webinar or call script — before release. It is a screening control, not legal advice. Any "block" finding stops release until fixed; any "check" item needs the named evidence or approval. Current legal claims use [`docs/source-registers/source-register.json`](../../../docs/source-registers/source-register.json) and the [legal/market release gate](../../../docs/quality-gates/legal-market-release-gate.md).

## Block list (never ship)

| # | Practice | Honest alternative |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fake scarcity, fake deadlines, invented "price rises", countdowns that reset, artificially damaged stock to extend a sale | A real limit or deadline, stated with the true reason, and honoured |
| 2 | Fabricated, AI-generated or unattributed testimonials; quotation marks around the advertiser's own words; staff or relatives posing as customers | Real, consented testimonials with name/role/place as agreed and a consent record |
| 3 | Fake engagement: bought followers, likes, reviews or comments; seeded posts made to look organic | Genuine early advocates engaging as real users; truthful "founding member" framing. In the US the FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, effective 21 Oct 2024) bans fake reviews and buying or selling fake indicators of social-media influence (register AD-09, checked 2026-09-23) |
| 4 | Disguised advertising: editorial-style or "torn-out news page" layouts without an ad label; undisclosed paid endorsements | Clear "Ad", "Paid partnership" or equivalent label, placed up front (see influencer disclosure in `08-influencer-marketing-strategy`) |
| 5 | Renting, buying or copying contact lists without a lawful basis; scraping personal contact data for bulk messages | Opted-in own lists; partners mailing their own opted-in lists; lawful B2B prospecting per `biz-dev-lawful-prospecting-outreach` |
| 6 | Aggressive follow-up: daily unsolicited "probes", guilt messages, invented third-party pressure ("X asked about you"), one-word nags | A capped, value-adding sequence (usually three touches) that stops on request |
| 7 | Confirm-shaming and obstructive cancellation or unsubscribe flows | An easy, visible exit, honest statement of what is lost, and a pause option |
| 8 | Unsubstantiated health, income, investment or "get rich" claims | Substantiated claims only, with sector-regulator review (health, financial promotions, betting, alcohol) |
| 9 | Inflated stature: fake credentials, implied endorsements, invented awards or client logos used without permission | Earned, verifiable credentials and permissioned logos |
| 10 | Fear appeals beyond the facts, or pushing buyers into debt to purchase | Proportionate risk communication; respect affordability |
| 11 | Pseudo-science in selling (subliminal persuasion, "brain hacks", guaranteed subconscious effects) | Observable, testable claims only |
| 12 | Disparaging a named competitor without evidence | Evidence-based comparison after legal review, or a category/behaviour "rival" |

## Check list (evidence or approval required)

- **"New"** only for a genuinely new or materially improved product.
- **Value anchors** ("worth UGX X") only when X is a real price charged or a documented cost.
- **Guarantees** are lawful and honoured; prefer process guarantees for services ("If we miss the launch date, next month's fee is waived") over income promises.
- **Comparative claims** have current evidence and legal sign-off.
- **Minors and families** in content: parental consent; never show a child's school or home exterior; post locations after leaving.
- **Personal data** in lead forms, giveaways and retargeting: purpose stated, consent recorded, opt-out honoured.

## Direct-marketing data law — East Africa (verified points, register 2026-09-23)

- **Uganda (PL-01):** controllers, processors and collectors must register with the PDPO (annual); processing personal data requires prior consent (s.7); a data subject may, by written notice, require a controller to stop processing for direct marketing and the controller must respond within 14 days (s.26). The Act has **no** ePrivacy-style opt-in rule for marketing, but consent to collect still applies. Do not write that the Act "requires opt-in for marketing".
- **Kenya (PL-02):** direct marketing needs consent and a simplified, free opt-out; the right to object to direct marketing is absolute; direct-marketing emails must not conceal the sender and must give a valid opt-out address; businesses wholly or mainly in direct marketing must register with the ODPC regardless of size.
- **Rwanda (PL-03) and Tanzania (PL-04):** registration duties exist; direct-marketing provisions are `NOT_ASSESSED` — check before use.
- **WhatsApp and SMS:** business-initiated messaging is governed by platform policy and telecom rules; confirm current WhatsApp Business policy and opt-in before any broadcast (policy check, not assumed).
