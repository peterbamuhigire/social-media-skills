# Idea Sources And Series

Use this reference when blog ideation should produce clusters and repeatable series instead of isolated titles.

Primary sources:

- Danny Iny et al., *Blog Post Ideas*
- Ann Handley, *Everybody Writes*

## Core rule

Generate ideas from source buckets, then organise them into clusters, series, and buyer-stage sequences.

## High-value source buckets

- audience questions
- objections
- customer stories
- myth-busting angles
- curated resources
- market commentary
- seasonal hooks
- behind-the-scenes process

## Series logic

For each major topic, plan:

1. one flagship guide
2. one how-to
3. one objection or comparison post
4. one proof or story post
5. one short derivative post idea for social or email

## Quality rule

An idea bank is stronger when each idea includes:

- source bucket
- buyer stage
- best distribution channel after publishing
- likely CTA posture
