# Format-Specific Premium Gates

Use the relevant gate before delivering copy. Keep the primary skill's structure, then apply these checks.

## Social Posts And Captions

Premium social copy must:

- open with a specific hook, not a generic announcement
- identify the reader or situation quickly
- create value before asking for action
- use one CTA matched to platform behaviour
- show proof through detail, example, result, process, or point of view
- avoid sounding desperate, discount-led, or engagement-baited

Upgrade weak hooks by shifting from announcement to reader value:

- from "We are pleased to launch..." to "Kampala founders now have a faster way to..."
- from "Quality you can trust" to the quality control, standard, or guarantee that creates trust
- from "DM us today" to what the reader receives after the DM

## Campaigns

Premium campaign writing must:

- state one campaign promise
- assign each channel a different job: attention, education, proof, conversion, reminder, or follow-up
- sequence proof before the strongest ask
- include objection-handling content, not only promotional posts
- protect urgency by using only real deadlines, quantities, or eligibility limits
- ensure the campaign idea can be repeated across assets without becoming flat

## Blog Posts And Articles

Premium blog content must:

- answer the core search question in the opening section
- take a useful position, not merely summarise common advice
- include local or sector-specific context
- cite data and name sources where facts matter
- include practical takeaways
- include internal link suggestions and at least one FAQ section when SEO/GEO matters
- convert naturally through a relevant CTA, not a hard sell after weak education

## Whitepapers, eBooks, And Documents

Premium documents must:

- have a central thesis
- include an executive summary or reader promise that can stand alone
- distinguish between evidence, interpretation, and recommendation
- make every recommendation specific enough to act on
- use charts, tables, or structured bullets where they help comprehension
- cite statistics and flag unsupported assumptions
- close with a commercially useful next step

For donor, investor, and enterprise readers, restraint matters. Avoid casual phrasing, inflated claims, and unsupported certainty.

## Sales Pages And Direct Response

Premium sales copy must:

- match the reader's awareness level
- lead with the problem, outcome, or offer depending on readiness
- explain the mechanism that makes the offer work
- build proof before presenting price
- address the most likely objection directly
- include risk reversal or safety logic where appropriate
- avoid fake scarcity, cheap urgency, and exaggerated transformation claims

The offer should feel valuable before the price is revealed.

## Email And WhatsApp

Premium owned-audience copy must:

- sound like one person writing to one person
- keep the first screen useful on mobile
- use a subject line or opening that earns attention without tricks
- make the CTA easy to complete
- segment VIP, high-intent, and low-intent audiences where possible
- follow up with value, not "just checking in"

For WhatsApp, be shorter and warmer. For email, use subject line, preheader, body, CTA, and PS deliberately.

## Brochures And Sales Collateral

Premium collateral must:

- lead with the reader's desired outcome, not the company history
- present services as outcomes and decisions, not just a menu
- include proof inside the main flow
- make contact details easy to find
- brief the designer on hierarchy, not only copy blocks
- give sales teams language they can repeat in meetings

## Proposals And Executive Content

Premium executive writing must:

- start from the client's commercial problem
- show judgement about tradeoffs
- connect recommendations to business outcomes
- be concise enough for a decision-maker
- separate must-do actions from optional improvements
- avoid consultant fog and abstract transformation language
