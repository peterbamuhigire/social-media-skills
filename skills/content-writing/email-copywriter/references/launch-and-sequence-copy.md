# Launch And Sequence Copy

Use this reference when writing emails that belong to a welcome sequence, timed launch, waitlist sequence, event promotion, or reactivation flow.

Primary sources:

- Jeff Walker, *Launch*
- Joanna Wiebe, *Buttons*

## Core rule

An email sequence should progress.
Do not write five emails that all make the same ask in slightly different words.

## Sequence roles

- welcome and expectation-setting
- education
- proof
- offer
- follow-up or close

## CTA rule

Each email gets one primary action.
That action must match the stage:

- early stage: read, reply, watch
- mid stage: understand, compare, see proof
- late stage: book, buy, apply, register

## Subject line rule

Do not optimise subject lines for curiosity alone.
Match the promise to the email's real role in the sequence.
