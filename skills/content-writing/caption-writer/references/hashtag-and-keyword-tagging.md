# Hashtag and Keyword Tagging

Merged from skills/content-writing/hashtag-strategy on 2026-09-29 at 7c60138; preservation map: [hashtag-strategy.md](../../../../docs/kaizen/consolidation-2026-09-29/preservation/hashtag-strategy.md)

## When to use this reference

Read this reference when the client wants more than the hashtag line under a single caption: a standing, client-specific hashtag strategy document, a tiered tag set per platform, a "tags to avoid" list, or a monthly hashtag review. For a one-off caption, the hashtag counts in [caption-build-method.md § Platform-Specific Rules](caption-build-method.md) and the [EA-Specific Hashtag Communities](caption-build-method.md) list are enough; use the standard set this reference produces once one exists for the client.

Produce the whole strategy document in one output: the cover plus all seven sections below. Use the client's industry, location and audience to select and justify every hashtag. Do not produce generic lists. For Uganda and East Africa clients, give priority to the EA-specific tags listed in each section where they fit.

## Inputs

Ask for the following before building the strategy:

| Input | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Client name | Trading name of the business |
| Industry | Sector, for example fashion retail, professional services, food and beverage, health, NGO |
| Country / city | Default Uganda/East Africa; name the city (for example Kampala, Nairobi, Dar es Salaam) |
| Primary goal | What the client wants from social media: awareness, community, sales or partnerships |
| Primary platform | Where hashtag strategy matters most: typically Instagram and TikTok; less critical on Facebook and LinkedIn |
| Target audience description | Who the client is trying to reach: demographics, interests, location |
| Brand name | Exact spelling as it will appear in the branded hashtag |
| Existing branded hashtags | Tags the client has already used or promoted to its audience |
| Active platforms | Every platform the client posts on; this sets the scope of the usage guide |

## Decision rules

| Situation | Action | Failure avoided |
|---|---|---|
| The client already uses branded hashtags | Keep them, and state which of the five branded categories each belongs to. | Splitting an established UGC archive across a new tag. |
| A campaign has ended | Retire its campaign hashtag; do not carry it on as an evergreen tag. | Stale campaign tags diluting the brand archive. |
| A post is about a global observance | Use the observance tag only when the post relates directly to the occasion. | Tagging global days on unrelated content. |
| A tag shows only **Recent** and no **Top Posts** on Instagram search | Treat it as possibly restricted; remove it from the strategy at once. | Posting into a restricted or shadow-banned tag. |
| A tag has not contributed to discovery in 4 or more consecutive posts | Remove it from the standard set and test 2–3 replacement niche tags. | Keeping underperformers out of habit. |
| The platform is WhatsApp | Use no hashtags. | Tags that do nothing on WhatsApp. |
| The platform is Facebook | Limit to 1 branded + 1 niche tag. | Over-tagging where hashtags add limited value. |

Platform behaviours in this reference (restriction checks, the Insights "From Hashtags" figure, ranking penalties for irrelevant tags, commonly restricted tags) change often. Confirm each against the live platform before stating it to a client, as the SKILL.md Quality Standards require.

## Procedure: the hashtag strategy document

For every hashtag, say when to use it, which platform it performs best on and what type of content it suits. Justify each selection; never list tags without explanation.

### Cover

```
[Client Name] — Hashtag Strategy
Prepared for: [Client Name]
Industry: [Industry]
Primary market: [City / Country]
Date: [Date]
```

### Section 1: Branded hashtags (5)

Branded hashtags belong only to the client. They build a searchable content library, encourage user-generated content (UGC) and make it easy for customers to find and join the brand community. Use at least the primary branded hashtag on every post, on every platform. Generate all five for the client from the brand name and industry; fold in any existing branded tags and note their category.

| # | Type | Format and examples | Use on | Purpose | Promotion / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary branded | `#[BrandName]` | Every post, every platform | A complete, searchable archive of all brand content | Put it in the profile bio on every platform; print it on receipts, packaging or physical signage; invite customers to use it when they share photos |
| 2 | Campaign | `#[BrandName][CampaignName]`, fresh per campaign (e.g. `#[BrandName]Launch2025`, `#[BrandName]Ramadan`) | All posts for that campaign; prompt customers to use it to enter competitions or share experiences | Keeps campaign content separate and trackable; builds a UGC gallery per campaign | Retire it when the campaign ends; never use it as an evergreen tag |
| 3 | Community | `#[BrandCommunityName]` for customers, fans and advocates (e.g. `#KawaHeroes` for a coffee brand, `#NakibuukaFamily` for a restaurant) | Community posts, UGC reposts, customer appreciation content | Builds identity and belonging; encourages organic tagging by loyal customers | Feature it on product packaging, in Stories and when resharing customer content |
| 4 | Product or service | `#[BrandName][ProductOrService]` (e.g. `#[BrandName]ColdPress`, `#[BrandName]Tailoring`) | Product-specific posts only, not general brand content | Lets customers interested in one product find it without sorting through unrelated posts | — |
| 5 | Value or mission | What the brand stands for, not what it sells (e.g. `#MadeWithCare`, `#BuildingUganda`, `#HomegrownQuality`, `#SupportLocal[City]`) | Behind-the-scenes, values-led and community posts | Attracts an audience that shares the brand's values; these tend to be the most loyal customers | — |

### Section 2: Niche hashtags (10)

Niche hashtags have between 1,000 and 100,000 posts. They are closely relevant to a topic, product or location and face less competition than broad tags, so content stays visible for longer and reaches a genuinely interested audience. Generate 10 from the client's industry and location:

| Hashtag | Platform | Estimated reach | Best content type |
|---|---|---|---|
| [hashtag] | [platform] | [1K–100K] | [image / video / carousel] |

EA-specific niche tags to consider (select those that fit the client's industry):

| Tag | Industry fit |
|---|---|
| #KampalaFashion | Fashion, retail, beauty |
| #UgandaFood | Food and beverage, agriculture |
| #NairobiBusiness | B2B, professional services (Kenya) |
| #KampalaEats | Restaurants, food delivery, catering |
| #UgandaTech | Technology, software, fintech |
| #EastAfricaHealth | Health, wellness, medical |
| #MadeInKenya | Products, retail (Kenya) |
| #DarEsSalaamBusiness | B2B, professional services (Tanzania) |
| #UgandaNGO | NGOs, social enterprises, development sector |
| #KampalaEvents | Events, entertainment, hospitality |
| #UgandaWeddings | Events, floristry, catering, fashion |
| #UgandaFarming | Agriculture, agri-tech, rural business |

For the final strategy, select the 10 most relevant, add industry-specific global niche tags (for example `#NaturalHairUganda` for a beauty brand), and present them in the table format above.

### Section 3: Community hashtags (10)

Community hashtags connect the brand to wider audience groups: people who identify with a community, movement or shared interest beyond the product category. They have between 100,000 and 500,000 posts and drive discovery by new audiences. Generate 10 that fit the client's audience, in the same table format as Section 2.

EA-specific community tags to include where relevant:

| Tag | Community |
|---|---|
| #EastAfricaBusiness | EA business community broadly |
| #AfricanEntrepreneur | Entrepreneurs across Africa |
| #MadeInAfrica | African-made products and brands |
| #AfricanWomenInBusiness | Women entrepreneurs, female-led brands |
| #AfricaRising | Pan-African growth and development narrative |
| #BlackOwnedBusiness | Black-owned enterprises (resonates with diaspora audiences) |
| #SocialEnterprise | NGOs, social businesses, impact organisations |
| #StartupAfrica | Tech, innovation and startup ecosystem |
| #SMEAfrica | Small and medium businesses |
| #AfricanCreatives | Creative industry professionals |

Select those that fit the client's audience and add global community tags for the industry (for example `#WomenInTech`, `#SustainableFashion`, `#PlantBased`).

### Section 4: Awareness / trending hashtags (5)

Broad tags with 500,000+ posts. They maximise reach but offer little targeting precision, because the content competes with an enormous volume of posts. Use them sparingly: 1–2 per post at most, always combined with niche tags. Select 5 from:

- **Industry-wide (choose 1–2):** `#Marketing`, `#SocialMedia`, `#SmallBusiness`, `#Entrepreneurship`, `#Business`, `#Branding`, `#ContentCreator`, `#DigitalMarketing`
- **Location (include at least 1):** `#Uganda`, `#Kampala`, `#EastAfrica`, `#Africa`, `#Kenya`, `#Nairobi`, `#Tanzania`
- **Global observance (only when relevant):** days that match the content, such as `#InternationalWomensDay`, `#WorldEnvironmentDay`, `#SmallBusinessSaturday`, used only when the post relates directly to the occasion

Present the final 5 with a note on when to use each.

### Section 5: Platform-specific usage guide

Apply this guide every time a post is scheduled; mixing sets wrongly across platforms reduces performance. The counts match the caption rules in [caption-build-method.md § Platform-Specific Rules](caption-build-method.md); the "sets to combine" column is the extra detail this reference adds.

| Platform | Recommended count | Placement | Sets to combine |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 5–10 | End of caption or first comment | 1 branded + 2–3 niche + 2–3 community + 1 awareness |
| Facebook | 1–3 | In the caption | 1 branded + 1 niche only; Facebook hashtags add limited value |
| LinkedIn | 3–5 | End of post | 1 branded + 2–3 niche or community; industry-relevant only |
| TikTok | 3–5 | In caption, naturally or at the end | 1 niche + 1 trending/broad + 1 branded |
| X / Twitter | 1–2 | Woven naturally into the post text | Branded or 1 trending; never stacked at the end |
| WhatsApp | None | Not applicable | Hashtags do not function on WhatsApp |

**Building a post-specific set.** Start each post from the client's standard set and swap 2–3 tags for ones specific to the topic. Generate the standard set in the output from the tags chosen in Sections 1–4, ready to copy for each primary platform. Instagram standard set template:

`#[PrimaryBranded]` `#[NicheTag1]` `#[NicheTag2]` `#[CommunityTag1]` `#[CommunityTag2]` `#[LocationTag]` `#[AwarenessTag]`

### Section 6: Hashtags to avoid

Make this section specific to the client, not a generic warning.

| Category | Examples | Why to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement-bait tags | `#follow4follow`, `#likeforlike`, `#f4f`, `#l4l`, `#followback` | Attract bot accounts and inactive profiles, not customers; inflate follower counts with no commercial value |
| Overused generic tags | `#love`, `#instagood`, `#photooftheday`, `#beautiful`, `#happy` | With hundreds of millions of posts, new content disappears within seconds; no audience uses these tags to find content it wants |
| Possibly restricted or shadow-banned tags | Instagram commonly restricts variants of `#desk`, `#beautyblogger`, `#easter`; restrictions change often | Instagram periodically restricts tags linked to spam or inappropriate content. Check: search the tag; if **Top Posts** does not appear and only **Recent** does, it may be restricted. Remove it at once, and check any unfamiliar tag before use |
| Competitor brand names | Any competitor's brand tag | Ineffective for discovery and looks unprofessional. Use for monitoring only: search the tag to see competitor content; never post with it |
| Misleading or irrelevant tags | Any tag unrelated to the post | Instagram's ranking penalises irrelevant hashtagging, and it damages trust when people click a tag and find unrelated content |

### Section 7: Hashtag performance tracking

Review hashtag performance monthly, retire underperformers and test replacements.

**Checking whether hashtags work (Instagram):**

1. Open a post and tap **View Insights**.
2. Scroll to **Impressions** and find the **From Hashtags** figure.
3. A high "from hashtags" figure means the tags are driving new-audience discovery; they are working.
4. A zero or near-zero figure means the tags are not generating discovery.

**Monthly tracking.** At month end, review the 10 most recent posts:

- note which posts had the highest "from hashtags" impressions;
- identify hashtags that keep appearing in high-performing posts;
- remove from the standard set any hashtag that has not contributed to discovery in 4 or more consecutive posts;
- research 2–3 replacement niche tags with Instagram's search suggestions (search a hashtag, then tap **Related** to see what Instagram recommends).

**Quarterly refresh.** Every 3 months, review the whole strategy: check every niche tag for restriction, refresh community tags to reflect shifts in audience or content mix, and update the standard set for each platform.

**Reporting.** Add a "Hashtag Performance" row to the monthly social media report (see [meta-reporting](../../../meta-analytics-ops/meta-reporting/SKILL.md)). Record average impressions from hashtags per post and flag tags that produced discovery well above or below average.

## Human review gate

A human consultant who knows the client's audience reviews every hashtag strategy document before delivery. AI-generated selections must meet the Golden Rule: each set must be as precisely targeted and culturally informed as one chosen by a skilled human digital strategist with deep knowledge of East African social media. Generic, untargeted or culturally misaligned sets are not acceptable, however efficiently they were produced.

## Release checklist

- [ ] All 5 branded hashtags are built for this client, not generic placeholders.
- [ ] The 10 niche hashtags fit the client's actual industry and city, not a generic list.
- [ ] The 10 community hashtags reflect the client's target audience, not only broad EA tags.
- [ ] The platform usage table is filled with the correct counts and combinations for each active platform.
- [ ] The hashtags-to-avoid section is specific to the client.
- [ ] A standard post set is generated and ready to copy for each primary platform.
- [ ] Every hashtag set respects the platform count limits; no overloading.
- [ ] Platform behaviours stated to the client have been checked on the live platform, or marked `not assessed`.
- [ ] British English throughout.
