# Format copy fitting

When to read: when fitting approved copy to a specific ad format. Only limits recorded in the currentness register are stated as facts; everything else is a check against the live platform help centre on the day of build.

## 1. Register-checked specs (checked 2026-09-23)

| Format | Spec | Register |
|---|---|---|
| Google responsive search ads | 3–15 headlines of up to 30 characters; 2–4 descriptions of up to 90 characters; two path fields of up to 15 characters; double-width scripts count 2 per character | AD-05 |
| Meta Facebook/Instagram feed image | Recommended 4:5, 1440×1800; supported 1.91:1 to 4:5 | AD-08 |
| Meta Stories and Reels | 9:16, 1440×2560; keep about 14% top, 35% bottom and 6% each side free of text, logos and key elements | AD-08 |

Everything else (primary-text truncation points, headline limits in Meta, TikTok caption length, LinkedIn intro-text truncation, YouTube bumper length) must be checked on the live help centre and recorded with the date in the build sheet.

## 2. Responsive search ad asset writing

- Write headlines as independent units: any combination must read correctly.
- Cover: keyword relevance (at least three headlines contain the core term), benefit, proof, offer, CTA, brand, local qualifier ("in Kampala").
- Descriptions expand the promise with proof and a clear next step.
- Pin only when legally or brand-required (for example a mandatory disclaimer); pinning reduces combinations — record the reason.
- Do not repeat the same idea in several headlines; variety lets the system test.
- See `paid-search-advertising` for account structure and match with landing pages.

## 3. Paid social primary text

- First line = hook family; proof early; one CTA; price and terms where they are the decision.
- Write a short (one to two lines) and a long (problem, proof, offer, CTA) version for temperature testing.
- Captions on all video; the message must survive sound-off viewing.
- End card or final frame restates offer and deadline if real.

## 4. Click-to-WhatsApp and lead forms

- Opening message: one sentence the user would plausibly send ("Hi, I'd like the price list for [service] in [area]").
- Lead-form intro: what they get, what happens next, response time promise.
- One qualifying question where the sales team is the bottleneck (qualifying friction trades volume for quality).
- Privacy notice link and consent wording are mandatory; do not pre-tick consent.

## 5. Radio (15/30/60 seconds)

- One message; brand named early and at the end; the response route (WhatsApp number, short code) said twice and slowly.
- Write for the ear: short sentences, no lists of more than three, numbers in words where clearer.
- Transcreate for vernacular stations with a fluent reviewer.

## 6. Outdoor and transit

- About seven words or fewer for a moving audience is a common planning heuristic, not a rule; test legibility at the real viewing distance.
- One image, one line, brand, one response route.
- Taxi/matatu and boda branding: design as a triplet template for recognition.

## 7. Build-sheet record

For each ad: format, placement, text fields with character counts, spec source (register ID or help-centre URL and date), CTA, destination URL with UTMs (see `meta-utm-tracking`), disclosure label if paid partnership, approval owner and date.
