# Budget triangulation and allocation

Read before proposing any advertising budget figure. Parent skill: [advertising-strategy-and-budget](../SKILL.md).

## 1. Methods available (choose three to triangulate)

Kelley and Sheehan (c. 2021–22) list more than twenty budgeting methods. Most are variants of four families:

| Family | Examples | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ratio | % of past or forecast sales; % of profit; allocation per unit or per outlet | Simple, affordable | Backwards: sales drive budget, not the reverse |
| Task | Objective-and-task; zero-based; marketing task | Ties money to outcomes | Needs honest cost assumptions |
| Competitive | Match competition; share of voice; share point | Market-aware | Local spend data is thin |
| Residual | All-you-can-afford; remainder; arbitrary | Honest about cash | Not a strategy |

Default triangulation: % of forecast sales + objective-and-task + share-of-voice check.

## 2. Procedure

1. **% of forecast sales.** Forecast revenue × a ratio from the client's own history. If no history, state the ratio as an assumption and its source. Do not import foreign category ratios as facts.
2. **Objective-and-task.** Target outcome → required reach and frequency, or required leads → media cost from the client's own CPM/CPL history (or a small test) → production → research → contingency. Show the arithmetic.
3. **Share-of-voice check.** Estimate category spend (Meta Ad Library activity, media monitoring, observed outdoor and radio presence; record method and date). Defending share: aim SOV ≈ share of market. Growing: SOV above share of market. The "twice SOV to gain one share point" rule of thumb is a hypothesis to test, not a law.
4. **Floor.** The minimum-effective level: enough weight in each chosen market, audience and month to register. If the budget is below the floor for the full scope, cut scope (fewer districts, audiences, months).
5. **Ceiling.** The point of diminishing returns: where incremental reach per shilling flattens and frequency climbs without new people. Use the reach curve in [media-planning](../../media-planning/SKILL.md).
6. **Reconcile.** Show the three figures, the gap and the reason for the chosen number.
7. **Three budget questions.** Prepare answers: what do we get for this investment; what happens at +X%; what happens at −X%.

## 3. Two-phase allocation

**Phase 1 — by activity** (illustrative split; set per plan):

| Line | Illustrative share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Paid media | ~75% | Client-owned spend, passed through |
| Production | ~15% | Assets that last longer can justify more |
| Research and measurement | ~5% | Pre-tests, polls, lift reads |
| Contingency | ~5% | Named purpose: production overrun, opportunistic media deal, or trial of a new channel |

Decide separately whether agency fees, internal staff and tools sit inside or outside the campaign budget.

**Phase 2 — by segment, geography and timing.**
- Segment weights follow value and reachability.
- Geography by category and brand development (see CDI/BDI in media-planning).
- Timing by buying season and the client's fiscal calendar (budgets are often cut in the last fiscal quarter).
- Test cells: deliberately raise or cut spend in matched areas to read the effect.

## 4. Minimum-effective concentration rules

| Budget position | Action |
|---|---|
| Below floor for all scope | One market, one audience, one burst; state what is dropped |
| Near floor | Pulse (bursts with gaps) rather than thin continuity |
| Comfortably above floor | Continuity in the core, bursts in season, test cells in expansion areas |
| Near ceiling | Move money to new audiences, new channels or creative testing |

## 5. Currency, VAT and finance handoffs

- State UGX (or the named market's currency) and the FX date for any USD-priced platform.
- Uganda: allow for VAT on non-resident digital-service invoices (e.g. Meta ads without a registered TIN) and route the tax position to chwezi-accounting-doctrine before stating it (register PL-05, partial; checked 2026-09-23).
- Advertising is normally expensed, not capitalised; route accounting treatment to the finance engine.

## 6. Sentence patterns

- "At UGX [x] a month, the plan concentrates on [district] and [audience] for [n] weeks, because below about UGX [floor] the message would not be heard often enough to change behaviour."
- "Three methods point to UGX [a], [b] and [c]. We recommend [chosen] because [reason]; the main uncertainty is [assumption], which the first four-week test will resolve."
- "If the budget is cut by 20%, we will drop [element] first and keep [core], because [reason]."
