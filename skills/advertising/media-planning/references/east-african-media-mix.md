# East African media mix notes

Read when planning media for Uganda, Kenya or the wider EAC. Parent: [media-planning](../SKILL.md). These are planning hypotheses, not audience facts. Verify current audience and rate data for each plan.

## 1. Medium roles (starting hypotheses)

| Medium | Typical role | Verify before use |
|---|---|---|
| FM radio, including vernacular stations | Broad and local reach, language fit, presenter endorsement; in Uganda the main news source (section 2) | Station audience survey date, coverage map, spot logs |
| TV | Demonstration, national launches | Audience measurement source, production cost |
| Outdoor (static billboards, transit: taxis, matatus, buses) | Local repetition, route presence | Permits from the relevant city or county authority; site photographs |
| Digital screens (LED, DOOH networks) and programmatic display, CTV or digital audio | Timed or dayparted repetition; targeted digital reach | Play logs, proof of play, app and seller lists: plan the weight here, specify the buy in [programmatic-and-brand-safety](../../programmatic-and-brand-safety/SKILL.md) |
| Newspapers (business pull-outs, Sunday editions, classifieds) | Business and older readers, time-bound offers | Circulation audit date |
| Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, click-to-WhatsApp) | Targeted reach, demonstration, response | Current objectives and specs (register AD-01, AD-08) |
| TikTok, YouTube | Short and long video, younger audiences, tutorials | Current ad objectives (AD-06); local ad-reach data |
| LinkedIn | B2B and professional audiences, Thought Leader Ads | AD-07; members are not monthly users |
| Search (Google) | Existing demand | Local search demand for the category |
| WhatsApp (owned lists, Channels, Status) | Retention, service, conversion | Consent and business-messaging policy; no national usage figure admitted (MK-03) |
| Community and trust channels (churches, mosques, SACCOs, associations, market announcements) | High-trust local reach | Permission, sensitivity, fairness |

## 2. Market data admitted by the currentness register (checked 2026-09-23; Tanzania, Rwanda, radio and GSMA rows read 2026-09-29)

- **Kenya (MK-01, DataReportal Digital 2026, published 8 Nov 2025):** internet users 23.4 M (40.5%); social-media user identities 18.4 M; ad reach — Facebook 17.0 M, YouTube 12.1 M, TikTok 18.4 M aged 18+, Instagram 3.95 M, LinkedIn 6.30 M members (registered members, not comparable). Ad reach is not users; do not compare years directly.
- **Uganda (MK-02, same series):** internet users 11.4 M (22.0%); Facebook ad reach 2.40 M, measured while Facebook was blocked (use as a floor, not the audience size); no YouTube or TikTok ad-reach figures published. Access was reported restored on 13 Jun 2026 (trade press and a minister's post; no UCC statement found; register UG-FACEBOOK-ACCESS-2026, official status `NOT_ASSESSED`), so treat Facebook's status as unstable and verify it at the campaign date.
- **Tanzania (register `DATAREPORTAL-TZ-2026`, Digital 2026, published 8 Nov 2025, read 2026-09-29):** population 71.0 M; internet users 20.6 M (29.1%); social-media user identities 7.95 M (11.2%); cellular connections 89.0 M (125% of population, so many people hold more than one SIM); ad reach Facebook 7.95 M, Instagram 4.10 M, LinkedIn 1.70 M members, Messenger 1.40 M. YouTube and TikTok ad reach were not stated in the read (`NOT_ASSESSED`).
- **Rwanda (register `DATAREPORTAL-RW-2026`, same series):** population 14.7 M; internet users 5.01 M (34.2%); social-media user identities 1.35 M (9.2%); cellular connections 13.0 M (88.7%); ad reach Facebook 1.35 M, Instagram 576 k, LinkedIn 570 k members, X 302 k, Messenger 103 k. YouTube and TikTok ad reach `NOT_ASSESSED`.
- **Radio in Uganda (register `AFROBAROMETER-UG-RADIO-2026`, Afrobarometer AD1221, published 13 Aug 2026 from 2024 survey findings):** share of Ugandans getting news at least a few times a week: radio 77%, television 37%, social media 23%, other internet sources 19%, newspapers 9%. This is news use, not advertising exposure; station-level listenership still needs a dated audience survey.
- **Mobile-first, sub-Saharan Africa (register `GSMA-SSA-MOBILE-ECONOMY`, GSMA Mobile Economy Sub-Saharan Africa 2024, 2023 data):** 527 M unique mobile subscribers (44% of the population); mobile internet users 27% of the population; a 60% usage gap (covered by mobile broadband but not using it), the highest of any region; smartphones 51% of connections. The 2025 edition could not be opened on 2026-09-29 (HTTP 403), so any newer figures are `NOT_ASSESSED`. Plan for feature phones, SMS, USSD and radio alongside smartphones.
- **WhatsApp (MK-03):** no credible country figure admitted. Do not state one without a named, dated survey.
- **Mobile network (MK-04, NET-01 to NET-05, 2026-09-24):** Kenya snapshot: search-reported Ookla mobile median about 45 Mbps and a StatRanker figure of 50.63 Mbps (about Oct 2025); the month and any latency figure are not re-verified, and crowdsourced tests skew urban and 4G/5G. Uganda: UCC quality-of-service operator mean downloads of 5.3-16.3 Mbps in 30 towns at peak hours (Aug-Sep 2024, reported via Ecofin; the UCC table is an image) and a SpeedOf.Me browser median of 8.4 Mbps (H1 2026, mixed desktop and mobile). Ookla and Opensignal figures for Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda: NOT_ASSESSED, so run the check before quoting. Plan video weight and landing pages for the slower end of the audience, not the median.

## 3. Practical cautions

- Platform ad-reach estimates are advertiser tools, not censuses; use them as ranges.
- Radio proof of airing and outdoor photographs are the post-buy evidence; ask for them in the booking terms.
- Digital screens and programmatic lines need play logs, seller and app lists and verification reports; the minimum deal terms are in the [programmatic and brand-safety skill](../../programmatic-and-brand-safety/references/ctv-dooh-and-audio.md). Classic static outdoor and FM radio are planned and proved here.
- Election periods: check political-advertising and content rules before any booking; Meta political and social-issue ads need authorisation and a "paid for by" disclaimer (AD-04), and Meta no longer runs such ads in the EU (AD-03).
- Internet shutdowns: Uganda suspended mobile internet from 13 to 18 Jan 2026 around the general election, and social media stayed restricted until about 26 Jan (UG-INTERNET-SHUTDOWN-2026, UG-SOCIAL-RESTORATION-2026). Around elections and national events, keep radio, SMS or outdoor in the mix, set a pause rule for digital spend, and exclude outage days from post-buy comparisons. The crisis playbook has the full [shutdown contingency](../../../playbooks/playbook-crisis-communications/references/internet-shutdown-contingency.md).
- Uganda ad invoices from non-resident platforms may carry VAT; route to the finance engine (PL-05, partial).
- Diaspora audiences (real estate, remittance products) need separate targeting and timing by time zone.
