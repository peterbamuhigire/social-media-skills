# East African media mix notes

Read when planning media for Uganda, Kenya or the wider EAC. Parent: [media-planning](../SKILL.md). These are planning hypotheses, not audience facts. Verify current audience and rate data for each plan.

## 1. Medium roles (starting hypotheses)

| Medium | Typical role | Verify before use |
|---|---|---|
| FM radio, including vernacular stations | Broad and local reach, language fit, presenter endorsement | Station audience survey date, coverage map, spot logs |
| TV | Demonstration, national launches | Audience measurement source, production cost |
| Outdoor, LED screens, transit (taxis, matatus, buses) | Local repetition, route presence | Permits from the relevant city or county authority; site photographs |
| Newspapers (business pull-outs, Sunday editions, classifieds) | Business and older readers, time-bound offers | Circulation audit date |
| Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, click-to-WhatsApp) | Targeted reach, demonstration, response | Current objectives and specs (register AD-01, AD-08) |
| TikTok, YouTube | Short and long video, younger audiences, tutorials | Current ad objectives (AD-06); local ad-reach data |
| LinkedIn | B2B and professional audiences, Thought Leader Ads | AD-07; members are not monthly users |
| Search (Google) | Existing demand | Local search demand for the category |
| WhatsApp (owned lists, Channels, Status) | Retention, service, conversion | Consent and business-messaging policy; no national usage figure admitted (MK-03) |
| Community and trust channels (churches, mosques, SACCOs, associations, market announcements) | High-trust local reach | Permission, sensitivity, fairness |

## 2. Market data admitted by the currentness register (checked 2026-09-23)

- **Kenya (MK-01, DataReportal Digital 2026, published 8 Nov 2025):** internet users 23.4 M (40.5%); social-media user identities 18.4 M; ad reach — Facebook 17.0 M, YouTube 12.1 M, TikTok 18.4 M aged 18+, Instagram 3.95 M, LinkedIn 6.30 M members (registered members, not comparable). Ad reach is not users; do not compare years directly.
- **Uganda (MK-02, same series):** internet users 11.4 M (22.0%); Facebook ad reach 2.40 M, measured while Facebook was blocked (use as a floor, not the audience size); no YouTube or TikTok ad-reach figures published. A June 2026 restoration of access is reported by trade press only and is `NOT_ASSESSED`.
- **WhatsApp (MK-03):** no credible country figure admitted. Do not state one without a named, dated survey.
- **Mobile network (MK-04):** Kenya median mobile download about 45 Mbps, 28 ms (Ookla, quarter to June 2026); Uganda mobile median not published. Plan video weight and landing pages for the slower end of the audience, not the median.

## 3. Practical cautions

- Platform ad-reach estimates are advertiser tools, not censuses; use them as ranges.
- Radio proof of airing and outdoor photographs are the post-buy evidence; ask for them in the booking terms.
- Election periods: check political-advertising and content rules before any booking; Meta political and social-issue ads need authorisation and a "paid for by" disclaimer (AD-04), and Meta no longer runs such ads in the EU (AD-03).
- Uganda ad invoices from non-resident platforms may carry VAT; route to the finance engine (PL-05, partial).
- Diaspora audiences (real estate, remittance products) need separate targeting and timing by time zone.
