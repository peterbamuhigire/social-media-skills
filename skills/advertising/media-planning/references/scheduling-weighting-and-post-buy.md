# Scheduling, weighting and post-buy

Read when building the flowchart, geographic weights or the reconciliation. Parent: [media-planning](../SKILL.md). Standard media-planning practice; geographic index formulas are the conventional CDI/BDI definitions.

## 1. Scheduling patterns

| Pattern | Shape | Use when | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continuity | Even weight throughout | Frequent purchases, steady demand, large budget | Thin weight if budget is small |
| Flighting | Bursts with gaps (no activity) | Seasonal demand, small budget, event windows | Awareness decays in gaps |
| Pulsing | Continuous low base plus bursts | Year-round demand with peaks | Needs enough base weight to register |

Wear-in and wear-out: a new creative needs exposures before it works; after heavy repetition responses fall. Plan creative rotation or refresh points and watch frequency and response per exposure.

## 2. East African calendar hooks (check dates each year)

School-term openings and fees periods; Christmas and New Year; Easter; Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha; month-end payday; budget-reading week; harvest cash windows by region; tourism high seasons; public holidays such as Martyrs' Day; election periods (heightened content and political-advertising sensitivity).

## 3. Geographic weighting

| Index | Formula |
|---|---|
| CDI (category development index) | Area share of category sales ÷ area share of population × 100 |
| BDI (brand development index) | Area share of brand sales ÷ area share of population × 100 |

| CDI | BDI | Common reading (practice heuristic) | Default action |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Low | Opportunity | Invest; check distribution first |
| High | High | Core | Defend |
| Low | High | Brand strong in a small category | Maintain or harvest |
| Low | Low | Weak | Avoid unless strategic |

Distribution check: do not advertise where stock or service is thin. Record distribution coverage by area (share of outlets stocking, delivery coverage, branch presence).

## 5. Post-buy reconciliation

Prepare the template before launch; complete it within an agreed number of days after the flight.

| Line | Planned delivery | Actual delivery | Variance | Proof | Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FM spots | 84 spots, breakfast | 79 aired | −6% | Station log | Two outages | Request make-good spots |
| Meta in-target impressions | 1.2m | 1.35m | +12% | Export | Lower CPM | Note; no action |
| Outdoor | 6 sites, 4 weeks | 6 sites, 3 weeks | −25% | Site photos | Late posting | Credit or extension |

Rules: compare like with like; attach proof; separate media delivery from business results; record make-goods agreed.

## 6. Measurement hooks per medium

Give each medium its own response code: WhatsApp keyword, short code, UTM-tagged link (see [UTM governance](../../../meta-analytics-ops/meta-utm-tracking/SKILL.md)), QR code or "how did you hear about us?" field. Offline media also create an echo in untraced channels; see [attribution and measurement](../../advertising-attribution-and-measurement/SKILL.md).
