# Contests, Promotions and Gaming Rules

Merged from skills/playbooks/playbook-social-media-contests on 2026-09-29 at cda737c (S04 tree); preservation map: [playbook-social-media-contests.md](../../../../docs/kaizen/consolidation-2026-09-29/preservation/playbook-social-media-contests.md)

## When to use this reference

Read this reference when a campaign includes a social media contest, giveaway, prize draw, caption competition, hashtag challenge or WhatsApp mini-contest. It supplies goal-to-mechanic selection, UGX prize benchmarks, platform promotion rules, a phase-by-phase timeline, a terms-and-conditions outline, gamification and measurement. Produce an operating playbook with named roles, ordered actions, controls and measures; a worked example must use a labelled scenario, never invented client evidence. The contest sits inside the parent campaign ([SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)): its dates, budget line and KPIs must agree with the campaign's §5, §8 and §9.

Related: [ugc-creator-and-customer-content.md](../../08-influencer-marketing-strategy/references/ugc-creator-and-customer-content.md) for permission logs and republishing contest entries; [platform-whatsapp](../../../platforms/platform-whatsapp/SKILL.md) for WhatsApp Business set-up; [meta-reporting](../../../meta-analytics-ops/meta-reporting/SKILL.md) for the report format; [east-african-english](../../../language/east-african-english/SKILL.md) for register; [anti-ai-slop](../../../ai-marketing/anti-ai-slop/SKILL.md) before release.

## Inputs

Ask for the following before producing the contest plan:

| Input | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Client business name and industry | Trading name and sector |
| Country / city | Default Uganda |
| Primary goal | Follower growth, engagement, UGC collection, list building or product launch |
| Platform(s) | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, or a combination |
| Prize idea or budget | In UGX, or state that a sponsor is being sought |
| Contest duration | Number of days available |
| Target audience | Age range, location, interests |
| Roles, timing and approval limits | Delivery owner; without them produce a draft only and do not schedule, spend or publish |

## Decision rules

| Condition | Action | Failure or risk avoided |
|---|---|---|
| No single primary goal is stated | Do not design the contest; obtain the goal first | Prize mechanics without strategy attract prize-hunters, not customers |
| More than one entry mechanic is proposed | Keep one primary mechanic | Stacked mechanics (comment + tag + share + follow) kill participation |
| Eligibility, terms, prize fulfilment or platform approval is unclear | Hold the launch and resolve the named requirement | An unfair or unenforceable contest |
| Winner is chosen by chance and a prize of value is offered | Check with counsel whether national lottery or gaming law needs a permit before launch (Uganda and Kenya: verify before stating, no register record for contest permits) | Running an unlicensed lottery |
| Brand is a betting or gaming operator, or the prize is a bet or gaming credit | In Kenya, do not use celebrities, influencers or content creators and route adverts through BCLB approval (verify at use; see register KE-BCLB-GAMBLING-ADS-2025 and KE-CA-BLOGGER-LICENCE-2026, directive text `NOT_ASSESSED`); in Tanzania, online gambling promotion is barred (see register TZ-ONLINE-CONTENT-2020); elsewhere check the sector regulator | Breaching gambling-advertising rules |
| Cash or Mobile Money prize | Set eligibility to 18+ | Paying cash prizes to minors |
| Product prize aimed at younger audiences | Require a parent or guardian to enter on the child's behalf | Collecting children's data without guardian consent |
| Contest runs longer than 7 days | Add a gamification mechanic (§8 of the procedure) | Momentum loss; contests beyond 21 days stall without one |
| Voting mechanic is used | State in the T&Cs that entries with evidence of organised block-voting are disqualified | Gamed votes and disputed winners |
| Prize may exceed a tax threshold | Refer the client to a tax adviser or lawyer; give no tax advice | Unsupported tax claims |
| Action publishes, spends, messages people or changes production state | Require explicit approval first | Unauthorised external impact |

## Procedure

### 1. Choose the goal and when to run

Tie the contest to one primary goal.

| Goal | Best mechanic | Signal of success |
|---|---|---|
| Follower growth | Tag-a-friend; share to enter (Story share only) | Net new followers during the contest |
| Engagement boost | Comment to win; caption competition | Comment count, saves, shares |
| UGC collection | Photo/video submission; hashtag challenge | Number of usable content pieces |
| WhatsApp or email list building | WhatsApp entry; DM to enter | Contacts added to CRM or broadcast list |
| Product-launch awareness | Quiz; hashtag challenge | Reach, impressions, product-page visits |
| Community loyalty | Voting; leaderboard | Repeat participants, share rate |

Run a contest when organic reach has plateaued, at a product launch, at a seasonal moment (back-to-school, Christmas, Eid, Independence Day), or to seed a new platform account with content.

### 2. Select the entry mechanic

| Mechanic | How it works | Pros | Cons | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Comment to win | Comment on the post to enter; may require answering a question | High volume; no friction; easy to moderate | Low quality; bots common; followers forget | Engagement spikes, quick campaigns |
| Tag a friend | Tag one or more friends in comments | Organic reach boost; viral loop | Can feel spammy; Instagram limits tags-as-entry | Follower growth on Facebook/Instagram |
| Photo/video submission | Post a photo or video with a hashtag, or send it by DM/WhatsApp | High-quality UGC; strong brand association | High effort; fewer entries | UGC campaigns, product demos |
| Caption competition | Write the best caption for a brand image | Creative engagement; fun tone | Subjective judging; time to moderate | Personality-led brands, FMCG |
| WhatsApp entry | Announce publicly; entries arrive as WhatsApp messages or voice notes | Private; personal; high local trust | Manual moderation; does not scale past about 500 entries | Local SMEs, airtime giveaways, close-knit communities |
| Hashtag challenge | Post content with a branded hashtag on TikTok or Instagram | Virality potential; UGC at scale | Needs audience effort; needs seeding by a creator | TikTok product launches, youth brands |
| Quiz / knowledge question | Answer correctly to qualify | Positions the brand as an authority; filters for an engaged audience | Lower volume | B2B, education, financial services |

Use one primary mechanic per contest.

### 3. Set the prize

Generic prizes (cash, data bundles, smartphones) attract prize-hunters who unfollow straight afterwards. Brand-relevant prizes attract real customers and generate authentic UGC.

| Prize type | Attracts | Recommended use |
|---|---|---|
| Brand product or service | Real customers | Default choice for all SMEs |
| Cash via Mobile Money | Everyone | Only when the brand's product is unsuitable or the prize is small |
| Data bundle / airtime | Everyone; practical | WhatsApp mini-contests, low-budget campaigns |
| Experience (workshop, event ticket, meal) | Engaged fans | Service businesses, hospitality, education |
| Sponsored prize | Varies | Co-marketing; shares the cost of the prize |

**UGX prize-value benchmarks** (engine heuristics, undated; confirm against the client's budget):

| Contest scale | Recommended prize value |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp mini-contest (micro) | UGX 10,000 – 50,000 airtime or MoMo |
| Facebook/Instagram page giveaway | UGX 100,000 – 500,000 product or cash |
| Multi-week campaign, UGC focus | UGX 500,000 – 2,000,000 in product or bundle |
| Major launch / brand activation | UGX 2,000,000+ or a sponsored prize package |

Keep the prize proportionate to entry effort: a photo-submission contest needs a bigger prize than comment-to-win.

**Mobile Money delivery:** pay cash prizes by MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. Collect the winner's number privately (DM or WhatsApp). Confirm delivery and screenshot the transaction as proof for your content.

**Sponsored prizes:** approach complementary businesses to co-sponsor. Example: a salon brands the contest and a skincare supplier provides the prize; both tag each other. Document the arrangement in writing and refer the client to a lawyer for formal sponsorship contracts.

### 4. Apply platform rules

Platform promotion policies change; verify each at use against the current official policy page (no register record).

**Facebook and Instagram (Meta).** Meta's promotion policies apply to all contests on Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts.

| Allowed | Prohibited |
|---|---|
| Like the post to enter | Requiring users to share a post to their personal timeline as a mandatory entry condition |
| Comment on the post to enter | Running contests on personal Facebook profiles (use a Page) |
| Tag a friend in comments | Tagging people in photos without their consent |
| Follow the account as a condition | Implying that Meta endorses or sponsors the contest |
| Share to a personal Story (Instagram only; not mandatory on feed) | |
| DM the Page to enter | |

Required disclaimer in every contest post caption:

> "This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Facebook/Instagram/Meta."

**TikTok.** Hashtag challenges are permitted. Paid promotion of a contest must comply with TikTok's branded-content policy. Do not promise prizes that depend on video views alone (view-manipulation risk).

**WhatsApp.** WhatsApp's Terms of Service prohibit bulk unsolicited messaging. Run WhatsApp entries opt-in only: participants message the business first. Manage the contest from a WhatsApp Business account.

### 5. Plan the timeline

| Contest type | Minimum | Recommended | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp mini-contest | 1 day | 3–5 days | 7 days |
| Comment/tag-to-win | 3 days | 5–7 days | 14 days |
| Photo/UGC submission | 7 days | 10–14 days | 21 days |
| Hashtag challenge | 7 days | 14 days | 28 days |

| Phase | Actions |
|---|---|
| Pre-launch (3–5 days before) | Tease the prize on Stories and feed; confirm the prize is in hand or the sponsor has confirmed; draft the T&Cs; prepare the entry-tracking sheet; schedule launch copy and the creative brief |
| Launch day | Publish at peak audience time (typically 7–9 am or 7–9 pm EAT for EA audiences); pin the post; share to Stories with a direct link; send to the existing WhatsApp broadcast list (opt-in subscribers only) |
| Entry period | Daily reminder Stories; share standout entries as mid-contest content (with permission); answer every question in comments; log entries daily in a spreadsheet |
| Closing day | "Last chance to enter" Story 24 hours before close; close entries at the stated time; export or screenshot all valid entries |
| Winner selection and announcement (within 24–48 hours of close) | Select the winner by random draw or judging, as stated in advance; verify eligibility; contact the winner privately before any public announcement; announce publicly with the winner's permission and tag their account; deliver the prize within 48 hours of the announcement |
| Post-contest (days 1–7 after) | Follow-up in step 9 |

### 6. Run a WhatsApp mini-contest (the most common Ugandan SME format)

Low cost, high participation, personal feel.

1. **Announce on Facebook and/or Instagram.** Post a clear image of the prize. State what to do, where to send the entry, the deadline and how the winner will be chosen. Include the Meta disclaimer.
2. **Collect entries on WhatsApp.** Participants send a photo, voice note, answer, or their name and location to the business number, in the format "Send 'CONTEST + your name' to [number] by [date/time]."
3. **Acknowledge every entry** with a WhatsApp Business auto-reply: "Thank you for entering! We've received your entry. The winner will be announced on [date] on our Facebook page."
4. **Track entries** in a spreadsheet: name, phone number, time received, entry content. This is the audit trail.
5. **Select the winner.** For random draws, number the list and use a random number generator (for example random.org). Screenshot the result.
6. **Announce publicly** on Facebook/Instagram; tag the winner if they have a social account; show the prize delivery (a photo of the MoMo confirmation or the product handover).
7. **Keep contact data responsibly.** People who messaged you have opted in to hear from you. Add them to a broadcast list for future updates, tell them they may receive future messages and how to opt out.

### 7. Legal and compliance

Not legal advice; confirm with counsel. Record each check's source and outcome in the evidence log.

**T&Cs outline** (every contest has one; publish as a Facebook Note, as a link in bio via a free Linktree or similar, or in the caption; check that the Note format is still available before relying on it):

1. Promoter name and contact details
2. Contest period: exact start and end date and time, with time zone (EAT)
3. Eligibility: age (minimum 18 unless the product is family-oriented; consult the client), country, employees excluded
4. How to enter: exact steps
5. Prize description: exact value or product; no cash alternative unless stated
6. Winner-selection method: random draw or judged criteria
7. Winner notification method and deadline, for example "winner will be contacted via DM within 48 hours"
8. Prize-delivery timeline
9. Data-use statement: how participant data will be used
10. Platform disclaimer: Meta not responsible

Add, where relevant: the UGC-use grant from [ugc-creator-and-customer-content.md](../../08-influencer-marketing-strategy/references/ugc-creator-and-customer-content.md) step 4 for submission contests; the block-voting disqualification rule for voting contests.

**Age:** 18+ by default for cash or Mobile Money prizes; for product prizes aimed at younger audiences, a parent or guardian enters on the child's behalf.

**Data protection:** Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 applies. Tell participants their contact details are collected solely for contest administration and, if applicable, future marketing; give a clear opt-out. A data subject may require in writing that processing for direct marketing stop, with a response due within 14 days (verify at use; see register UG-DPPA-2019 and UG-DPPA-S26-DIRECT-MARKETING-2026). For Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda consent, opt-out and registration duties, use the disclosure register in [influencer-term-sheet-and-disclosure.md](../../08-influencer-marketing-strategy/references/influencer-term-sheet-and-disclosure.md) §3.

**Prize taxes:** in Uganda, prizes above a threshold may attract withholding tax (verify before stating, no register record). Refer the client to a tax adviser or lawyer; do not advise on specific tax obligations.

**Lotteries and gaming:** a chance-based draw for a prize of value may fall within national lottery or gaming law. Ask counsel whether a permit is needed before launch (Uganda and Kenya: verify before stating, no register record). Betting and gaming clients follow the decision-table row above.

### 8. Sustain longer contests with gamification

For contests running longer than 7 days:

| Mechanic | How to implement | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Public leaderboard | Post weekly rankings of top entries by votes or points | UGC photo contests, creative competitions |
| Public voting | Followers like their favourite submission; most likes wins | Caption contests, photo submissions |
| Points accumulation | Award points for entry, referral and daily check-in; post running totals | Loyalty programmes, extended campaigns |
| Progress bar | Post a graphic: "X entries received — goal: 500" | List-building contests |
| Milestone rewards | "If we reach 1,000 comments, we'll add a second prize" | Follower-growth campaigns |

Voting can be gamed: disqualify entries with evidence of organised block-voting and say so in the T&Cs.

### 9. Follow up after the contest

- **Repurpose entries as UGC:** obtain written permission first (a WhatsApp or comment reply counts as consent if you asked explicitly); make a "best entries" gallery post or carousel; use top entries in future organic content and creative briefs. Log each in the UGC permissions log.
- **Winner content:** post the announcement with the prize-handover photo or MoMo confirmation screenshot; tag the winner (with permission); thank all participants.
- **Non-winners:** post a consolation offer (discount code, free consultation or a small incentive for all participants) to turn entrants into customers and soften the disappointment.
- **Organic retargeting:** a follow-up post, "Didn't win? Here's how to get [product] this week…", and a WhatsApp broadcast to entrants with a relevant offer.
- **Audience building:** keep the WhatsApp contacts as a new broadcast segment; record follower count before and after for reporting.

### 10. Measure

Report within 7 days of close and include the results in the client's monthly social media report ([meta-reporting](../../../meta-analytics-ops/meta-reporting/SKILL.md)).

| Metric | How to measure | Benchmark (EA SME; engine heuristic, undated) |
|---|---|---|
| Total entries | Count from spreadsheet or comment thread | Varies by prize value and audience size |
| Net follower growth | Followers on close day minus followers on launch day | 5–20% of starting follower count |
| Engagement rate on the contest post | (Likes + comments + shares) ÷ reach × 100 | 8–20% for contest posts (against 1–3% organic average) |
| Reach and impressions | Platform Insights | 2–5× average post reach |
| UGC pieces collected | Count of usable photo/video entries | Stated in the pre-contest brief |
| WhatsApp contacts added | Count from entry log | Track separately per campaign |
| Post-contest conversion | Purchases or enquiries from the follow-up offer | Track by promo code or DM enquiry count |

## Release checklist

- [ ] **Goal–mechanic alignment:** the mechanic serves the stated primary goal; no generic "run a giveaway" advice.
- [ ] **Platform compliance:** mechanics comply with current Meta and WhatsApp promotion policies; the Meta disclaimer is in every post template.
- [ ] **UGX-calibrated prizes:** value benchmarked in UGX; prize brand-relevant, not generic; Mobile Money delivery included for cash prizes.
- [ ] **Complete timeline:** pre-launch, entry period, winner selection, announcement and post-contest follow-up with specific day counts.
- [ ] **WhatsApp mini-contest format** included and adapted where WhatsApp is relevant.
- [ ] **T&Cs outline** provided; tax, lottery/gaming and legal matters flagged and referred onward; unverified items marked.
- [ ] **Measurable outcomes:** metrics and measurement method named in every contest brief.
- [ ] **British English throughout;** professional East African register per [east-african-english](../../../language/east-african-english/SKILL.md).
