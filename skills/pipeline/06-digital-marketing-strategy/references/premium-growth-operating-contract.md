## Build the decision before the channel plan

1. Define the buyer decision, business constraint and offer. Interview customers, prospects who declined, sales staff and service staff where authorised. Keep verbatim evidence separate from interpretation; record sampling and whose views are absent.
2. Identify the buying situation, alternatives, barriers, willingness-to-pay evidence and reason this business can win. Do not infer wealth, motivation or platform use from demographic labels alone.
3. Audit acquisition, conversion, fulfilment and retention. A response-time or product-quality defect may deserve investment before more traffic.
4. Compare viable channel combinations, including partnerships, referrals and offline routes. State which channels are deferred and what evidence would reopen them.
5. Select a bounded first experiment and a sequenced roadmap. Define budget, team hours, owner, destination, follow-up, success measure, guardrail, stop rule and review date.

| Workpaper | Minimum fields | Reject when |
|---|---|---|
| Customer evidence | Question, method, segment, date, consent/rights, observation, interpretation, contradiction | A synthetic persona is presented as research |
| Positioning | Buyer, buying situation, alternative, distinct benefit, proof, limits, price rationale | Any competitor could use the statement unchanged |
| Channel investment | Audience evidence, channel job, spend, production cost, labour, expected learning, capacity, owner | Every channel is selected or spend precedes a credible destination |
| Creative brief | Buyer tension, one idea, source asset, specific proof, native format, CTA, accessibility, rights | Stock prestige claims replace a demonstrable benefit |
| Lead handoff | Lead definition, source, consent scope, qualification, owner, next action, response commitment, rejection reason | A like or download is treated as a sales-qualified opportunity |
| Experiment | Hypothesis, comparison, unit, time window, minimum evidence, primary measure, guardrails, decision | A before/after change is called causal without controls |
| Service review | Delivered work, results, limitations, client actions, failures, next investment decision | Output counts are the only evidence of value |

## Measurement without inflated ROI

Define all formulas and boundaries with the finance owner before setting targets.

- **Attributed revenue / media spend** is ROAS when revenue and media cost share the specified scope. It excludes other costs and is not profit or causal ROI.
- **CAC** uses the acquisition costs included by the agreed policy divided by new customers from the matching acquisition cohort. Report media-only and fully loaded CAC separately; include agency fees, creative, tools and sales costs where applicable. Account for sales-cycle lag.
- **Contribution before marketing** deducts the agreed variable fulfilment/service costs, returns and discounts from revenue. Do not subtract marketing twice.
- **Incremental marketing ROI** = (incremental contribution before marketing − incremental marketing cost) / incremental marketing cost. Use only where incremental contribution has a defensible causal estimate. When it does not, report attributed contribution and explicitly limit the inference.
- **Customer lifetime contribution** needs retention, service costs, period, discounting where material, and evidence. Annual revenue is not lifetime profit. Use scenarios when cohorts are immature.
- **Payback** is the period when cumulative contribution covers acquisition investment; reconcile cash collection separately from booked revenue.

Synthetic illustration, not a client result: UGX 12m attributed revenue and UGX 3m media spend give 4.0x ROAS. At 40% contribution before marketing, contribution is UGX 4.8m. With UGX 2m additional agency/creative cost, contribution after total marketing is negative UGX 0.2m. The calculation does not establish incrementality. A premium report must expose this loss even when the ad dashboard looks attractive.

Reconcile native platform exports, website events and CRM outcomes. Keep deduplication rules, refunds, currency, date windows, attribution windows and missing/offline conversions visible. Never sum platform-attributed conversions as unique customers. Prefer a suitable holdout or randomised test when feasible; sparse data calls for narrower conclusions and learning tests, not forced statistical significance.

For GA4, verify available reporting models and dimension scope in [Google's attribution settings](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10597962?hl=en). Accessed 2026-09-22: first-click, linear, time-decay and position-based reporting models were removed in November 2023. Do not present a conceptual attribution model as a selectable GA4 setting. Recheck before implementation; this reference does not prove account configuration.

## Premium and luxury choices

Premium consulting should demonstrate expertise, judgement, reliable service, useful proprietary work and accountable follow-through. Luxury goods or experiences may additionally depend on aesthetic identity, provenance, symbolic meaning and selective distribution. Choose the business model explicitly. Do not import artificial scarcity, inaccessible interfaces, snobbery or universal no-discount rules into professional services.

Build a proof ladder: observable process → inspectable sample → permissioned case → independently checkable result. Label demonstrations as demonstrations. When no client proof exists, offer a bounded diagnostic or pilot with honest learning goals. Never invent logos, awards, endorsements or premium price acceptance.

## Release and renewal

Complete an applicability record for jurisdiction, audience age, sector, personal data, advertising claims, endorsements, rights and procurement requirements. The [ICC Code](https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/the-icc-advertising-and-marketing-communications-code/) is self-regulatory guidance, not blanket legal certification. Recheck platform policies and applicable local law before execution.

Review one real creative unit before expanding a calendar. Run the client-name substitution test, claim-proof check, native-format review and accessibility/rights review. Revise any interchangeable content. At renewal, explain the observed value, unresolved uncertainty, learning and next resource decision. Withhold outcome claims unsupported by client evidence.
