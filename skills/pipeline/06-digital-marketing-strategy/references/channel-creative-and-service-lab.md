# Channel creative and service lab

Parent: [Digital Marketing Strategy](../SKILL.md).

Use alongside the selected platform skill. This is an original operating method;
posting cadence, creative length and success thresholds are experiment choices,
not universal algorithm rules. Check dated official evidence and the intended
account before using current formats, ad products, music, messaging or commerce.

## Creative development

1. Choose a real customer question, desire or decision. Collect product/service
   evidence, original footage, genuine customer language and objections.
2. Develop distinct concepts, not cosmetic variations of one generic hook. Each
   concept needs a proposition, proof mechanism, opening, demonstration, ending
   and next step. State what the audience should understand after viewing.
3. Specify shots, sound, captions, on-screen text, brand details, rights and
   accessibility. Route art direction and rendered review to design-system-skills.
4. Produce a representative unit, inspect it on the intended device/surface, and
   revise comprehension, factual accuracy, pacing and visual finish before scale.
5. Test a meaningful variable with a stated audience and observation window.
   Record production effort and commercial outcomes as well as attention.

Premium craft can look documentary, playful, editorial or polished depending on
the brand. Low production quality is not authenticity. Expensive production is
not proof of relevance. Preserve truthful colour, material, scale, use conditions
and results when showing a product. Disclose synthetic demonstrations where
omission would mislead; never fabricate a customer or a before/after result.

## Platform-specific production decisions

| Platform | Useful candidate concepts | Production checks | Commercial handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Practical demonstration, local service explanation, relevant event, customer question, moderated group discussion | Caption supplies context, image is readable, link preview accurate, event details current, moderator available | Page/contact/website destination, enquiry owner, response commitment, resolution record |
| Instagram | Craft/detail study, comparison carousel, process Reel, permissioned customer use, collection of related advice | Standalone first frame, legible sequence, true product rendering, accurate captions, usable crop, coherent brand without a rigid grid | Relevant profile/Story destination where supported, qualified DM or website enquiry, sales follow-up |
| TikTok | In-use demonstration, useful explanation, process observation, expert response, episodic customer question | Opening earns attention honestly, proof is visible, sound licensed, captions accurate, pacing fits comprehension | Verified available destination/ad route, lead/order evidence, service capacity and refund checks |

Select formats on audience and task evidence. A carousel should advance the
argument on each page; a Reel should demonstrate something worth seeing. Do not
force a trend, joke, dance, local phrase or theatrical confession into the brand.

## Creator and rights workpaper

Record creator/audience fit, verified work samples, authenticity concerns,
deliverables, fee basis, revision scope, approval, disclosure, music/likeness/IP
rights, permitted territories, duration, paid amplification, account access,
cancellation and reporting. Obtain actual quotes; do not use uncited Uganda fee
bands or follower count as a quality substitute. A public post, tag or repost
button does not by itself settle the rights for a commercial campaign.

## Community service design

Distinguish enquiry, complaint, product safety issue, abuse, spam and media
request. Set response and escalation commitments from staffing and risk. Answer
ordinary questions publicly when useful; move personal details to an authorised
private route. Acknowledge a complaint, investigate and explain the next action;
do not promise resolution before understanding the issue. Do not hide legitimate
criticism to manufacture approval. Record corrections and service recovery.

Create a group only when its member purpose and moderation capacity justify it.
Respect other communities' rules. No mass member harvesting or unsolicited
promotional messages. Obtain approval before publishing or contacting anyone.

## Paid and organic evidence

Keep organic usefulness and paid conversion evidence separate. An organic winner
is a candidate for paid testing, not a prerequisite or proof of paid success.
Before authorised spend: verify account/region/product eligibility, offer,
destination, tracking, lead routing, consent, budget cap, approval and stop rule.
Check sensitive sectors, audience age and ad/creator policies for the actual use.

Define event → source system → denominator → time window → deduplication →
qualified outcome → owner. Track content attention, destination actions,
qualified enquiries, accepted opportunities, wins and contribution separately.
Reconcile platform attribution with CRM and cash records; do not add duplicated
conversions. Native lift or split-test features require eligibility and adequate
design; a feature's existence is not evidence that this account can run it.

## Experiment card

Record buyer, hypothesis, competing explanation, creative variable, audience,
comparison, observation window, spend/effort cap, main measure, guardrail,
minimum evidence, decision owner and stop/restart rule. Keep changes interpretable.
For sparse data, use qualitative/task evidence and bounded directional findings;
do not force a significance claim or declare a winner after a favourable day.

Synthetic example: two truthful demonstrations produce different enquiry rates.
The higher-volume version attracts requests outside the delivery area and creates
more complaints. Judge qualified contribution and service burden before scaling.
The reach winner may be the wrong commercial choice.

## Evidence-bearing monthly report

Show the starting hypothesis, work delivered, actual results, data limits,
quality/rights/service incidents, learning and next investment decision. Separate
management targets from observed results. Include the full cost and sales-cycle
lag. Do not manufacture a universal completion-rate, reach, engagement, follower
growth, hashtag-count or posting-frequency benchmark.

## Rejection checklist

- No fabricated testimony, credentials, stock result or false scarcity.
- No bought engagement, pods, spam, unauthorised scraping or unapproved spend.
- No book-derived algorithm formula or stale feature specification.
- No unlicensed sound, undisclosed commercial relationship or unexplained rights gap.
- No content calendar expanded before a representative unit passes review.
- No click/lead promise without a tested destination and accountable follow-up.
- No apparent ROI that hides fees, labour, returns, service cost or attribution limits.

## Production pack: adaptable working examples

Use these as original demonstration briefs, not client cases or algorithm rules.
The example practice diagnoses enquiry handling for service businesses. Replace
that offer with the actual client's buyer problem and evidence. The assumed team
can prepare one core demonstration and adapt it across selected channels; a real
effort estimate determines how many units are commissioned.

### Setup and handoff record

Before production, record the authorised account owner and roles, recovery route,
profile/service details, contact destination, brand voice, rights library,
moderation owner, approved response commitment and measurement access. Inspect
current native account settings and regional features; record the actual result
rather than copying a remembered menu path. Test the destination and CRM handoff.
Export the record with named owner, evidence and open items for each field.

### Four-week pilot calendar example

Weeks are a planning sequence; this is not a recommended posting frequency.
Assign dates only after approving effort, inputs, rights and client review time.

| Window | Core job and source | LinkedIn adaptation | Facebook adaptation | Instagram adaptation | TikTok adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Explain the enquiry-handoff problem using a labelled fictional journey | Practitioner decision note with annotated journey | Plain-language question and demonstration for the relevant community | Carousel showing enquiry, ownership and recovery states | Screen/desk demonstration of an enquiry reaching the wrong owner |
| Week 2 | Demonstrate a diagnostic using synthetic records | Document walkthrough of the three decisions the diagnostic changes | Short captioned walkthrough with a practical question | Reel showing the work artefact and the practitioner's explanation | One-question demonstration: how do we know the enquiry arrived? |
| Week 3 | Address an actual buyer objection collected with permission | Explain when a simple repair is sufficient | Answer the objection in accessible language; invite relevant questions | Annotated comparison of repair and rebuild choices | Show the two alternatives and one reason to choose each |
| Week 4 | Explain the real bounded diagnostic offer and review learning | Scope, buyer fit, proof, limits and service-page invitation | Clear service explanation with contact options and response expectation | Visual scope card tied to the working service page | Demonstrate what the buyer receives; make the next step clear |

Select only channels with a justified job. A row can be deferred. Do not publish
all variants automatically. Each calendar record needs unit ID, platform,
specific audience, source/rights, script/copy, asset, CTA, owner, reviewer,
production effort, date, approved status and outcome observation. Separate
content publication from service-response coverage.

### Ready-to-adapt content units

**LinkedIn decision note:**

> A contact form can show “sent” while the enquiry never reaches its owner.
> In this fictional service journey, the missing step is receipt confirmation.
> Before commissioning a redesign, I would check three things: who receives the
> enquiry, what the visitor sees after a failure, and who follows up.
> The attached diagram shows the handoff and recovery path. It illustrates a
> diagnostic method; it is not a client result.

Use only if the named practitioner endorses the method and the diagram exists.
Add the actual relevant service link when an invitation fits the context.

**Facebook community/service unit:** caption a real, authorised demonstration:
“Here is how we check whether an enquiry reaches the right person. This example
uses fictional details. The useful test is receipt at the destination, followed
by an assigned next action.” Ask a relevant practical question only if someone
will answer. Do not request likes, shares or tagging to manufacture distribution.

**Instagram carousel storyboard:** card 1 shows the specific failure; card 2
shows the synthetic journey; card 3 names the owner and receipt evidence; card 4
shows an accessible failure/retry state; final card explains who the diagnostic
is for. Write caption/alt text that carries the essential meaning. Remove cards
that add decoration without explanation. Label the demonstration on the asset.

**TikTok demonstration shot list:** open on the observable problem; show a
synthetic submission; cut to the destination record; explain the missing or
working handoff; finish with the specific check a viewer can make. Use natural
speech, readable captions and cleared audio. Choose length from clarity and
current format limits, not a claimed universal retention formula. Review that
cuts do not misrepresent elapsed time, product behaviour or client results.

### Response pack and boundaries

| Situation | Adaptable response | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Public service-fit question | “The diagnostic covers [actual scope]. It suits [evidenced buying situation]. The service page explains the outputs and limits.” | Link the real scope; avoid public personal-data collection |
| Public complaint | “I’m sorry this part failed. We can investigate through [authorised private support route]. Please keep account details out of the comments.” | Create an owned support case; confirm facts before promising a remedy |
| LinkedIn connection with no prior conversation | Use a brief truthful reason for connecting, with no fabricated familiarity or automatic sales sequence | A connection is not consent to a mailing list or repeated pitch |
| Prospect explicitly requests more information | “You asked about [specific need]. Here is [relevant evidence]. Would the scoped diagnostic help you decide [actual decision]?” | Honour the request's scope; stop if declined |
| Sensitive data, threat or urgent safety concern | Use the approved escalation response for that jurisdiction/service | Route to the responsible owner; do not improvise public advice |

Do not promise response or resolution times unless staffed and agreed. Keep
personal details in authorised systems. Record outcome and handoff, not just
whether a comment was answered.

### Production acceptance

Deliver the selected calendar, scripts/storyboards, asset/rights manifest,
response pack, setup/handoff record and measurement sheet together. Check one
complete unit in its actual format before approving the batch. Hold any unit
with a missing source, misleading edit, untested destination or absent service
owner. Compare observed buyer questions and qualified actions at review; retain
poor results and rejected units as learning evidence.

## Current primary-source checks

Opened independently by the researcher and root on 2026-09-22; review by
2026-10-22 or before campaign execution. These establish documented guidance,
not universal performance or availability for a particular account.

- [TikTok Commercial Music Library](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library?lang=en-GB), updated July 2026: select campaign region and check usable placements. Retain the actual licence/selection evidence for the chosen sound.
- [TikTok ad-testing guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/ad-testing-guide?redirected=1): define objectives, isolate variables and avoid premature conclusions. Determine sample sufficiency for the actual experiment; stable-looking metrics alone do not prove significance.
- [TikTok Pixel setup](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/get-started-pixel?lang=en): inspect the documented event-connection and diagnostic route. Installation and event accuracy still need authorised implementation evidence.
- [Meta's original-content guidance](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/amp/): distinguish original/meaningfully transformed material from low-value reuse. Originality does not itself clear third-party rights or guarantee reach.

The official-source sweep found no support for a universal TikTok three-day
reset, daily posting quota, guaranteed trending-audio distribution, Instagram
username/display-name-only indexing, broadcast-channel immunity, or Facebook
three-post minimum. These are excluded from execution rules; absence in this
search is not proof about every undisclosed ranking mechanism.
