# Lead Magnets and List Building

Merged from skills/playbooks/playbook-lead-magnet-system on 2026-09-29 at cda737c (S04 tree); preservation map: [playbook-lead-magnet-system.md](../../../../docs/kaizen/consolidation-2026-09-29/preservation/playbook-lead-magnet-system.md)

## When to use this reference

Read this reference when the client needs a lead magnet system: choosing and titling the free offer, building the website placements, designing WhatsApp and email delivery, setting up double opt-in and scheduling list hygiene. It expands [strategy-document-sections.md § 1. List Building Strategy](strategy-document-sections.md), which names the opt-in mechanisms and expected conversion rates, into a build plan with owners.

A lead magnet is a free, high-value offer exchanged for a prospect's contact details: an email address, a WhatsApp number or both. Its purpose is an owned audience asset that no platform algorithm, account suspension or policy change can take away from the business.

Once subscribers arrive, the welcome sequence in [email-funnel-build-sequence.md](email-funnel-build-sequence.md) takes over.

## Inputs

Ask for the following before producing any output:

| # | Input | What to capture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business name | Trading name of the client |
| 2 | Industry | Sector and niche (for example financial services / personal loans) |
| 3 | Country / city | Default Uganda/East Africa |
| 4 | Primary goal | What subscribers should ultimately do: purchase, book or enquire |
| 5 | Current list size | Email subscribers and WhatsApp contacts, if any |
| 6 | Existing lead magnet | Any current free offer or sign-up mechanism |
| 7 | Primary capture channel | Website, social media bio, WhatsApp or a combination |

## Decision rules

| Situation | Action | Failure avoided |
|---|---|---|
| The asset attracts downloads but no qualified next step | Tighten the audience promise and the follow-up path | Lead volume without commercial relevance |
| The proposed offer is "subscribe to our newsletter" | Replace it with an offer that passes all four specificity criteria | A sign-up with no exchange of value, so no reason to hand over contact details |
| Several formats look attractive | Produce one format at a time, chosen on audience behaviour and client capability | Spreading effort thin, or defaulting to whatever is quickest |
| Fewer than four placements are live | Do not drive paid traffic to the lead magnet | Paying for visitors who meet an incomplete capture path |
| Client has both WhatsApp and email | Offer the choice: "Get the checklist by WhatsApp or by email." | Forcing a channel the prospect does not prefer; lower opt-in |
| A prospect has not initiated WhatsApp contact or confirmed by email | Do not add them to any list or broadcast group | Processing personal data for marketing without consent |

## The specificity formula

Bly (2018) sets four criteria. A lead magnet must meet all four:

| Criterion | What it means |
|---|---|
| Specific outcome | Promises one concrete result, not a vague benefit |
| High perceived value | The audience would pay for it if it were not free |
| Instantly deliverable | Received immediately, with no waiting |
| Unique expertise | Shows knowledge only this business can offer |

Generic sign-up prompts fail because they offer no exchange of value: the audience has no reason to give contact details for updates it did not ask for. Build the title from specific outcome + audience + timeframe.

## Procedure

### 1. Choose one of six formats

| Format | Best for | Length or scale | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Special report or white paper | B2B clients and professional services; a topic the audience is actively researching | 5–12 pages | *The 2025 Uganda SME Digital Marketing Report: What Is Working and What Is Not* |
| Checklist | Practical, action-oriented audiences who will apply a step-by-step process at once | One page | *The 10-Step WhatsApp Business Setup Checklist for Ugandan Retailers* |
| Video training | Audiences who prefer visual learning; recorded demonstration of a skill, tool or process | 10–20 minutes | *How to Set Up a Facebook Business Page in Under 20 Minutes* |
| Quiz or assessment | Audiences who need to see their own gap before buying; delivered as a scored, personalised result | Short assessment | *Is Your Business Ready for Social Media Advertising? Take the 5-Minute Assessment* |
| Free tool or template | Clients whose expertise is practical and process-driven; plug-and-play and time-saving | One tool | *The Monthly Social Media Content Calendar Template (Excel/Google Sheets)* |
| Webinar or live workshop | Established clients with an audience ready to attend live; interactive with Q&A; highest perceived value, most preparation | Live session | *Free 60-Minute Workshop: How to Get Your First 10 Clients Using WhatsApp* |

### 2. Build the four placements, in this order

1. **Hero section, above the fold.** The offer appears at the top of the homepage without scrolling: a headline stating the specific outcome, a one-sentence description, an image of the lead magnet (cover mock-up or screenshot) and a single input field (email or WhatsApp number).
2. **Sidebar or pop-up.** Trigger after 30 seconds on the page or 50% scroll depth, whichever comes first; never on page load. Keep it dismissible; a forced interstitial damages trust.
3. **Post-content.** Place the offer at the close of every blog post, article and YouTube video description. People who reach the end are the most engaged and the likeliest to opt in.
4. **Exit intent.** Triggered when the cursor moves towards the browser close button (desktop) or the back button is pressed (mobile). Use a different angle or headline from the hero section, because the visitor has already seen the main message.

### 3. Design delivery for both channels (East Africa: WhatsApp-first)

For EA clients, a WhatsApp number is often worth more than an email address. The source skill states WhatsApp open rates of 80%+ against 20–40% for email in the region; verify before stating (see register WHATSAPP-USAGE-EA-2026 for current usage evidence; no record covers the open-rate comparison).

- **WhatsApp delivery.** Set the WhatsApp Business auto-reply to send the lead magnet link or PDF as soon as the prospect sends a trigger message (for example "SEND CHECKLIST") to the business number. Promote the trigger message in the bio link, social posts and Stories.
- **Email delivery.** Standard automation: trigger on form submission, deliver within 5 minutes, then start the welcome sequence ([email-funnel-build-sequence.md](email-funnel-build-sequence.md)).
- Where both channels exist, let the prospect choose.

### 4. Require double opt-in

Every sign-up triggers a confirmation step before the contact is added to any list or broadcast group.

- **Email:** a confirmation email with a single click to confirm. Deliver the lead magnet after confirmation, not before.
- **WhatsApp:** the prospect must start the conversation by messaging the business number. Treat that trigger message as opt-in only where the prompt states the business will send updates, and record it (verify against WHATSAPP-BUSINESS-POLICY). Never add a contact to a broadcast list unless they initiated contact first.

Legal basis: Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 and Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 require informed, freely given, specific consent before personal data is processed for marketing. Double opt-in is the most defensible consent mechanism available. Verify at use; see register UG-DPPA-2019, UG-DPPA-S26-DIRECT-MARKETING-2026 and KE-ODPC-DIRECT-MARKETING-2026, and WHATSAPP-BUSINESS-POLICY for WhatsApp's own opt-in rules.

### 5. Apply the list hygiene protocol from day one

| Action | Trigger | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Remove hard bounces | Automatic bounce notification | Within 24 hours |
| Re-engagement campaign | 90 days of inactivity | Send a three-email sequence: "Are you still there?" |
| Remove unresponsive contacts | No engagement after the re-engagement sequence | When the sequence completes |
| Review list health | Monthly | Check open rate, click rate and unsubscribe rate |

Working floors from the source skill (label as provisional comparators): a healthy EA email list keeps an open rate of at least 20%; a WhatsApp broadcast list keeps a read rate of at least 60%.

## Template: lead magnet system brief

Produce for the client:

1. Recommended format, with the rationale from audience and goal.
2. Lead magnet title, applying the specificity formula (specific outcome + audience + timeframe).
3. Content outline: headings and key points.
4. Delivery mechanism: WhatsApp flow, email automation or both.
5. Placement plan: how each of the four positions will be built and by whom.
6. Opt-in compliance summary: the consent mechanism and its regulatory alignment.
7. List hygiene schedule: dates for the first review, the re-engagement trigger and the monthly audit.

## Release checklist

- [ ] Format chosen from audience research and client capability, not whichever is quickest to produce.
- [ ] Specificity formula fully applied: the title promises one concrete outcome, not a general benefit.
- [ ] Double opt-in configured and tested for both email and WhatsApp paths before any promotion.
- [ ] All four placements identified, assigned to a named person and scheduled.
- [ ] List hygiene documented with specific triggers and timelines, not "clean the list occasionally".
- [ ] Delivery tested end to end: a real opt-in journey completed and confirmed working before traffic is driven.
- [ ] EA regulatory compliance stated explicitly: the consent mechanism aligns with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 or the local equivalent.
