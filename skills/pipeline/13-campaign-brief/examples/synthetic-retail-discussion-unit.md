# Synthetic small-retailer discussion unit

**Status: SYNTHETIC TRAINING EXAMPLE — NOT AN APPROVED MADUUKA OFFER, PRODUCT CLAIM, RESEARCH FINDING OR PUBLISHABLE CAMPAIGN.** The name identifies the portfolio exercise only. It does not describe an actual Maduuka capability, service or customer result.

This example tests whether four channel adaptations can keep one narrow, honest discussion prompt without inventing an offer. It is a partial content unit, not an operational campaign brief.

## Decision frame

- **Audience hypothesis:** Ugandan small-retail shop owners or operators who may want to reflect on daily work routines. Audience size, need and channel preference are not evidenced.
- **Communication job:** invite people to name one routine they would like to make easier.
- **Funnel stage:** exploratory listening, not sales or lead generation.
- **Offer:** none. No software, consultation, checklist, demo, result or service is offered.
- **Action:** an optional public reply naming one routine. Do not solicit financial, customer or other sensitive information.
- **Decision use:** at most, use responses to form a better customer-research question. Comments alone do not establish demand, representativeness or product fit.

## Four channel adaptations

### Facebook — direct community question

**Hook:** Shop owners: which part of the day takes more effort than it should?

**Copy:** Checking stock, recording sales or closing the day? Name one routine you would like to make easier, or add your own. This is a fictional discussion exercise; there is no product or service offer.

**Action:** Optional comment. Do not ask for sales amounts, customer details or private business records.

**Format and access:** Text-first draft for a Facebook feed post. Meta Help describes text and photo/video post choices and an audience selector; confirm the live account's audience and accessibility controls. No graphic is included.

### Instagram — compact prompt with one focal choice

**Hook:** One shop day. Which routine would you simplify first?

**Copy:** Stock check, sales record or end-of-day close? Pick one—or name a different routine. Fictional discussion prompt only; no product or service is being offered.

**Action:** Optional reply. No product tag, contact prompt or creator collaboration.

**Format and access:** Caption draft for a feed post. No photo or carousel has been produced; check current account and composer availability, and keep essential information in the caption if an image is later approved. Any exchange of value with a creator requires a fresh disclosure and rights review.

### LinkedIn — reflective business question

**Hook:** Which repeated shop task is most worth improving?

**Copy:** A retail day brings repeated decisions about stock, sales records and closing routines. If you could make one of those routines easier, which would you choose, and what makes it difficult today? This is a fictional research prompt, not a product claim, customer result or service offer.

**Action:** Optional comment with one routine. No lead form or sales follow-up is implied.

**Format and access:** Text post draft. LinkedIn's current help page describes text posts and photo/video options. If imagery is added later, provide useful image alternative text where available. No Page or personal account is identified.

### TikTok — spoken-choice script

**Opening:** “Shop owners: which daily routine would you most like to make easier?”

**Sequence:** Show or speak three choices: “Stock check”; “Recording sales”; “Closing the day”. Close: “Pick one—or name another. This is a fictional discussion prompt, not a product or service offer.”

**Action:** Optional comment with one routine. Do not request sensitive information.

**Format and access:** Script only; no recorded person, footage, music, captions, thumbnail or finished video exists. If produced after approval, review captions, keep key information understandable without sound and check all rights. For a post promoting a real business or third party, verify TikTok's current content-disclosure and commercial-music controls before use.

Each variant retains the qualification that the prompt is fictional and there is no product or service offer. If a platform limit or layout makes that qualification difficult to carry, shorten the hook or withhold the variant. Do not remove the qualification to make the copy sound more commercial.
