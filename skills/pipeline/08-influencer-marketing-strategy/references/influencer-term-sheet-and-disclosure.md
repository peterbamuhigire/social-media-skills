# Influencer term sheet, brief and disclosure register

Read before sending an offer, writing a creator brief, handing terms to a lawyer, or approving content for go-live. Parent skill: [08-influencer-marketing-strategy](../SKILL.md). The term sheet structure follows the contract anatomy in Hennessy, B. (2018) *Influencer*, Citadel Press. It is a pre-lawyer checklist, not a contract; formal agreements are drafted by qualified counsel.

## 1. Term sheet (complete before lawyer drafting)

| Section | Record |
|---|---|
| Parties | Legal name and handle; who hires (brand, agency, publisher); payee name and account match the contract name |
| Campaign | Brand, product, campaign name, markets |
| Schedule | Shoot date, city, duration, call-sheet owner, dietary or access needs |
| Deliverables | Exact count and format per platform; resolution and aspect ratios (check current platform specs); number of edited images or cuts; draft and final dates |
| Flight | Go-live window; posts stay live and unarchived until the flight ends |
| Exclusivity | Named competitors or category; duration; any pre-existing deals disclosed before signing |
| Usage | Baseline: brand's owned channels with credit for [period]; each extension (paid social, pre-roll, print, in-store, point of sale, third-party native) priced separately |
| Fee and payment | Distribution fee, talent fee, usage and exclusivity lines; deposit and balance; payment channel (bank or mobile money); invoice and tax documents; withholding tax check with the finance engine |
| Travel and expenses | Who books and pays; class of travel; no new demands after fee agreement |
| Brief compliance | Consequence of missed deadlines or non-compliant content (reshoot, withheld payment) |
| Approvals | Draft review turnaround (service level) and who approves |
| Disclosure | Required label wording and platform tool (see section 3) |
| Minors | Written parent or guardian consent for any child appearing; child-safety rules in section 4 |
| Data protection | Handling of giveaway entries or audience data under the applicable law (Uganda DPPA 2019; Kenya DPA 2019 — see register PL-01, PL-02) |
| Reputation clause | Morality and conduct terms, including political content during election periods; agreed exit holding statement |
| Disputes | Governing law and venue |

## 2. Brief additions (beyond the template in the parent skill)

- **Regulated-category guardrails:** alcohol (no driving or vehicles, age rules), betting, medicines and health claims, financial products, food claims, children. Check the current local rules for each category before briefing.
- **Talking points, not scripts:** "Use your own words for these points; do not read them verbatim."
- **Mood-board checkpoint** before the shoot.
- **Deliverable specifications** and approval service level.
- **Every sponsored post must give the audience something** (a tip, a demonstration, a real opinion), not just "I love this".

**Blockers on the unassessed items:** the Kenya BCLB directive text (secondary report only), Uganda UCC and Competition Act commencement, the Uganda Computer Misuse judgment text (ULII blocked automated access on 29 Sep 2026), the Tanzania 2021 amendment text, and Rwanda disclosure rules were not verified at primary source; keep them as checks and do not present them as clearance.

**Pre-campaign disclosure and compliance checklist**

1. Name every market the post can reach and open the row above; mark each unassessed cell `NOT_ASSESSED` in the deliverable.
2. Apply the strictest common standard (FTC/ASA) everywhere as best practice: plain label up front ("Ad", "Paid partnership with [Brand]") **and** the platform branded-content tool.
3. Disclose payment, free product, commission, affiliate link and family or employment ties, in the caption's first line and in the first seconds of video, in the audience's language.
4. Keep claims true and substantiated; no invented testimonials (Kenya CPA s.12, Competition Act s.55, ASBK cl.13.1, Tanzania FCA s.15-16).
5. Bind image and data use to the contract term; add an end-of-term takedown line (ODPC example, KE-07).
6. Giveaway entries, lists and DMs: collect consent, identify the sender, offer an opt-out and log it (Kenya reg.15-17; Uganda s.26).
7. Betting, alcohol, health, financial or child-directed content: check the sector regulator first; Tanzania bars gambling promotion online.
8. Record source ID, access date and the check outcome in the evidence log; escalate unresolved items to counsel.

**ICC Code 2024 (self-regulatory, not legislation; register `ICC-CODE-2024-TEXT`, landing page `PREMIUM-ICC-2026`).** Use it as the floor where no national rule exists (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda):

- Scope: "influencers" include individuals, organisations and machine-created or controlled avatars, so a virtual or AI influencer is covered; creators and brand ambassadors are covered whatever they are called, including small accounts (Definitions).
- Art. 18.2: influencer content must be immediately identifiable as marketing; disclose the brand connection each time it is shared; disclose affiliate links; a disclosure buried in a string of hashtags, in "see more" or in terms and conditions is not sufficient; creators must not claim a sponsorship that does not exist.
- Art. 18.3: a minor influencer works only under a contract with explicit parent or guardian consent and protection against undue exploitation; disclose the compensation; content featuring minors must be age-appropriate; apply Chapter E (children and teens, including children's personal data).
- Art. 7 (identification and transparency) and Art. 5 (truthfulness) apply to every post.

**Engine rule:** apply the FTC/ASA standard everywhere as best practice — label up front in plain words **and** use the platform's branded-content tool (check its current name and availability).

**On-camera line:** "Quick note before we start: this video is a paid partnership with [Brand]. They made it possible, and everything I say is my honest view."

## 4. Child safety for family and child content

Never show the home exterior or school; do not reveal when a child is alone; post location only after leaving; never force a child to pose; ask older children's permission; obtain written guardian consent; check child-protection and data-protection law for the market.

## 5. Go-live checklist

Contract signed · brief acknowledged · disclosure present in the first line or first seconds · platform tool applied · claims substantiated · usage and exclusivity dates logged · tracking link and code live · approval recorded · no archiving before the flight ends.
