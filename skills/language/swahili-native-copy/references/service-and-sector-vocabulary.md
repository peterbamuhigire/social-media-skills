# Service and Sector Vocabulary — Kiswahili Copy

Read this when writing Kiswahili for hospitality, retail, food and beverage, agriculture, booking flows, signage or price copy. Parent skill: [`swahili-native-copy`](../SKILL.md). Keep standard (`sanifu`) forms; mark (K) Kenya and (T) Tanzania variants where usage differs. Have a fluent reviewer check final copy.

## 1. Money, numbers and quantities

- Large numbers: `mia` (100), `elfu` (1,000), `laki` (100,000), `milioni` (1,000,000). The number follows the currency noun: `shilingi elfu hamsini` (50,000 shillings).
- Price words: `bei` (price) — `Ni bei gani?` / `Ni kiasi gani?` (How much?); `ghali` (expensive); `rahisi` (cheap); `bure` (free); `jumla` (total); `chenji` (change); `risiti` (receipt); `bili` (bill); `asilimia` (per cent); `punguzo` (discount).
- Payment: `kadi ya malipo` (payment card), `akaunti ya benki` (bank account), `kubadilisha fedha` (change money); `Naweza kulipa kwa kadi?` (Can I pay by card?).
- Numbers agree with their noun class: `vikombe viwili` (two cups), `watoto wawili` (two children); ordinals take `-a` concord: `ghorofa ya tatu` (third floor), `mara ya kwanza` (first time).

## 2. Booking, service and shopping phrases

| Job | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Make an appointment | `Nataka kuweka miadi.` |
| Book a table or room | `Naweza kuwekesha meza / chumba?` |
| Any rooms? | `Je, mnavyo vyumba?` |
| Breakfast included? | `Hiyo ni pamoja na kifungua kinywa?` |
| Check-out time | `Saa ngapi inabidi kutoka chumbani?` |
| Can I help you? (staff) | `Nikusaidie?` |
| Just looking | `Ninaangalia tu.` |
| I'm looking for… | `Ninatafuta…` |
| I'll take it | `Nitaichukua.` |
| That's all, thank you | `Ni hiyo tu, asante.` |
| Opening times | `Mnafungua saa ngapi?` / `Mnafunga saa ngapi?` (convert with the Swahili clock) |

Service words: `miadi` (appointment), `mkutano` (meeting), `mapokezi` (reception), `mgeni/wageni` (guest/guests).

## 3. Directions

`iko wapi?` (where is it?) · `moja kwa moja` (straight ahead) · `kushoto / kulia` (left / right) · `karibu na / mbali` (near / far) · `katikati ya mji` (town centre).

## 4. Signage and interface labels

`WAZI` (open) · `IMEFUNGWA` (closed) · `SELI` (sale, casual) · `DESKI LA MALIPO` (pay desk). Prefer native coinages in polished copy: `barua pepe` (email), `tovuti` (website), `mtandao` (internet), `kupakua` (download), `neno la siri` (password).

## 5. Food, hospitality and agriculture glossary

- **Hospitality core:** `Karibu / Karibuni`; `sinia` (shared serving tray — communal warmth); the "always something ready to share" ethos.
- **Signature dishes:** `ugali`, `pilau` (festive spiced rice), `kachumbari` (tomato-onion-chilli relish), `chapati`, `nyama choma` (grilled meat), `maandazi`, `vitumbua`, `sambusa/samosa`, `chai ya viungo` (spiced tea), `futari` and `uji` (Ramadan).
- **Spices:** `iliki` (cardamom), `karafuu` (cloves), `mdalasini` (cinnamon), `tangawizi` (ginger), `pilipili` (chilli), `tui la nazi` (coconut milk), `ukwaju` (tamarind).
- **Staples:** `mchele` (raw rice) / `wali` (cooked rice), `muhogo` (cassava), `ndizi` (banana/plantain), `maharage` (beans), `mtama` (sorghum/millet).
- **Fruit:** `embe` (mango), `nanasi` (pineapple), `parachichi` (avocado), `nazi` (coconut), `papai` (papaya).
- **Meat and fish:** `nyama ya ng'ombe` (beef), `nyama ya mbuzi` (goat), `kuku` (chicken), `samaki` (fish), `pweza` (octopus), `kamba` (prawns), `dagaa` (small dried fish), `sato` (tilapia).
- **Vegetables:** `mchicha` (amaranth), `kisamvu` (cassava leaves), `mboga` (vegetables), `nyanya` (tomato), `vitunguu` (onion).
- **Cooking patterns:** `wa/za kukaanga` (fried), `za kuchoma` (grilled/roasted), `wa kuchemsha` (boiled), `wa nazi` (in coconut sauce), `mchuzi wa…` (stew/curry of…), `supu ya… / mchemsho wa…` (soup of…).

## 6. Regional variants to watch

beer: `biya` (K) / `bia` (T) · toilet: `choo` (K) / `msalani` (T) · school: `skuli` / `shule` (T, standard) · ticket: `tikiti` / `tiketi` · tip: `tipu` / `bahashishi` · brochure: `brosha` / `kabrasha`. Keep standard spellings and avoid regional sound shifts in national copy.
