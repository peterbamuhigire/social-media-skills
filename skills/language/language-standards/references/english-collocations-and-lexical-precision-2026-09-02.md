# English Output Overlay - Social Media

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md) | Canonical study: `C:\wamp64\www\digital-research-skills\docs\continuous-improvement\english-collocations-and-lexical-precision-2026-09-02.md`

- Default to British English and neutral East African professional English unless the channel or audience requires another standard.
- Be conversational without being careless. One real audience, one useful idea, one honest action, and one recognisable point of view.
- Check collocations, grammar, spelling, connotation, and channel register. Advanced vocabulary, idioms, slang, humour, and metaphor are optional tools, not proof of quality.
- Use local detail only when true and relevant; never manufacture dialect, intimacy, lived experience, or cultural authority.
- Avoid automatic hooks, empty inspiration, hashtag clouds, repeated sentence shapes, false urgency, and unsupported intensifiers.
- Proof the first line, line breaks, tags, links, alt text, claims, names, rights, and platform-specific constraints. Read aloud.

Record audience, channel, purpose, locale, claim and rights checks, collocation or
idiom decisions, language/proof status, unresolved issues, reviewer, and date.

The two supplied books inform durable lexical and collocation checks only. They do
not establish current platform conventions or audience facts; use Digital Research
for current claims and do not copy book lists or examples.
