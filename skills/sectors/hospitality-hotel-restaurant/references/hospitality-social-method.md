# Hospitality social method

Moved from `SKILL.md` in Social Kaizen S09 (29 Sep 2026, start commit `0e0af8a`); text unchanged. Read when classifying the business and guest journey, choosing content pillars, working on search and AI discoverability, setting reputation and safety rules, measuring results or planning a restaurant launch.

## Strategy spine

Classify the business and demand occasion: accommodation, leisure/resort,
business travel, dining, delivery, celebration, meetings/events, catering,
local community or recruitment. Set one primary business outcome per campaign
and connect it to an owned destination: real booking, reservation, enquiry,
WhatsApp conversation, menu/order or email capture.

Build content around the guest journey:

1. **Discover:** location, people, food, rooms, design, local context and useful
   guides that answer real questions.
2. **Trust:** behind-the-scenes standards, service team, provenance, hygiene and
   safety practices, clear policies, accessible facilities and attributed proof.
3. **Choose:** room/outlet/event comparisons, menus, packages, inclusions,
   dates, price conditions and objections.
4. **Act:** one clear CTA, staffed response path, UTM/offer code, confirmation and
   response SLA agreed with the operator.
5. **Experience and return:** on-property help, guest permission for UGC,
   post-stay feedback, review response, loyalty/CRM only with consent, and
   seasonal reactivation.

## Content pillars

- rooms, suites, amenities and real service moments;
- food, beverage, menus, sourcing, kitchen craft and dietary clarity;
- events, meetings, weddings, packages and venue layouts;
- destination/local guide and transport/access information;
- people, training, culture and responsible operations;
- guest stories and UGC only with documented rights;
- offers, availability and seasonal moments with exact conditions;
- service recovery, FAQs and review responses.

Use channel-native formats, but keep brand entity facts, location, contact,
policies and offer conditions consistent across website, Google Business Profile,
social profiles, OTA/partner pages and messaging. Cadence and posting times are
experiments, not universal truths.

## Search and AI discoverability

Social is a corroboration and discovery surface, not proof of guaranteed AI
training-data inclusion or citation. Optimise legitimate profile completeness,
consistent entity facts, useful captions/transcripts, descriptive alt text,
accessible video, searchable menus/FAQs, local intent and referral tracking.
Never manufacture forum mentions, reviews, followers, consensus or “citations”.
The site remains the canonical source for volatile prices, availability,
policies and booking terms.

## Reputation and safety

- Reply to every material review with empathy, facts and an owner; move private
  data and unresolved complaints to a secure channel.
- Escalate food-safety, injury, discrimination, security, payment, privacy,
  allegation, crisis and regulator matters to the accountable operator.
- Do not argue publicly, reveal guest identity, repost children or staff without
  consent, make medical/allergen guarantees, or promise an outcome the property
  cannot deliver.
- Label sponsorships, creator partnerships and gifted stays; retain rights and
  usage period for every asset.

## Measurement

Report qualified enquiries/bookings/reservations, response time, cost per
qualified action, conversion by landing page, repeat/direct share, review
sentiment themes, UGC permission rate and operational fulfilment. Reach,
followers and engagement are supporting diagnostics. Separate platform-reported
metrics from verified booking/POS/PMS outcomes and mark attribution gaps.

## Restaurant launch example

For a restaurant launch, build one campaign around a verified menu/occasion,
show preparation and service proof, publish accessible short-form content,
route to a staffed reservation/order path, track a campaign code, and review
orders, response time, cancellations and complaints after the test.
