# Institutional Health Communication and Infodemic Response

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Sara Rubinelli,
*Institutional Health Communication in the Information Age*, and Stéphane
Duguin, *Cybersecurity for NGOs: Attack Prevention and Threat Response*.

## Social content operating model

1. Define the health objective, affected communities, evidence, action, limits,
   and qualified reviewer.
2. Translate accurately into platform-sized messages without deleting material
   uncertainty or context.
3. Listen for questions, harmful interpretations, coordinated manipulation,
   impersonation, and source confusion.
4. Respond through a trusted correction path; do not amplify harmful claims just
   to increase reach.
5. Measure comprehension, appropriate action, trust, equity, correction time,
   and escalation quality, not engagement alone.

Protect account access, domain identity, moderation evidence, and affected
people's privacy. Route threats, compromised accounts, or targeted harassment to
the incident owner rather than improvising in public.
