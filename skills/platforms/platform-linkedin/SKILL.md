# LinkedIn Presence Plan

Plans how a firm and its practitioners use LinkedIn to build reputation that reaches the website and the sales owner, for the client owner and delivery team.

Acknowledgement: Shared by Peter Bamuhigire, techguypeter.com, +256 784 464178.

<!-- dual-compat-start -->
## Use When
- The firm's LinkedIn presence is weak and it wants a plan for what the company and its leaders should post.
- A Company Page needs setting up or growing: admin roles, follower invitations, Showcase or Product Pages, LinkedIn Events or Live, and a page content plan.
- Founder or partner profiles need reviewing so they support the company's authority.
- The client wants a thirty-day LinkedIn pilot with clear measures before committing more time.

## Do Not Use When
- `playbook-social-selling` for sales outreach, employee advocacy and high-ticket selling on LinkedIn.
- `strategy-personal-brand` for building one individual's personal brand.
- `playbook-paid-social-advertising` for LinkedIn ad campaigns.
- Stop before posting, messaging or changing a Company Page or profile without the owner's authority.

## Required Inputs

| Artefact | Source/provider | Required? | If absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service scope, target buying roles, geography/language and buying situation | Approved brief or client interview | Yes | Stop; do not plan content without a named buyer and service. |
| Approved cases and samples, with confidentiality constraints | Client partner or practice lead | Yes | Use clearly labelled educational diagnostics; never present a demonstration as a client win. |
| Account baseline (profile, Company Page analytics, top posts) | Account admin or supplied audit | Conditional | Return a discovery plan, not an optimisation result; mark the baseline `not assessed`. |
| Website destinations, sales owner and CRM handoff | Client marketing or sales lead | Yes | Name the gap; the plan cannot promise a next step nobody can handle. |
| Available practitioner time and employee willingness | Practice lead | Yes | Size the pilot to the time stated; employee participation stays voluntary. |
| Current feature availability, field limits, image dimensions and privacy controls | Official LinkedIn help or the account | Conditional | Omit the specification or flag it for live verification. |

## Workflow

1. Confirm objective, buyer, decision owner and permission boundary; stop if the objective or owner is missing, or if the job is sales outreach or advocacy (`playbook-social-selling`).
2. Decide practitioner-led, company-led or both from actual competence, buyer and proof, and from the distinct job and production capacity of each asset.
3. Review each profile and the Company Page for consistency with the website, using the [practitioner, content and pilot method](references/linkedin-practitioner-content-and-pilot-method.md); for Company Pages, Showcase or Product Pages and Events apply [company pages, showcase and events](references/company-pages-showcase-and-events.md).
4. Plan content with the [seven-role content system](references/seven-role-content-system.md) and choose text, document, video or article/newsletter by the job each piece does.
5. Connect profile, evidence-bearing content, website destination and CRM handoff with [consulting authority to pipeline](references/consulting-authority-to-pipeline.md).
6. Build the thirty-day pilot to the agreed effort budget and review the first complete unit before expanding.
7. Run the release criteria, rights, confidentiality, accessibility and anti-slop checks; correct any failed check and rerun it before handoff.

## Outputs

| Artefact | Consumer | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn channel plan: practitioner/company roles, profile review, content roles and formats | Client owner and practitioners | Profile, content and website make the same supportable promise. |
| Company Page, Showcase/Product Page and Events plan | Page admin | Built from the company-pages reference; every legacy figure verified or removed. |
| Thirty-day pilot with content units | Delivery team | Each unit has buyer question, source, distinct argument, account, format, destination, owner and review date. |
| Measurement and review plan | Client owner and sales owner | Stages tracked separately with denominators and time windows; review decision options stated. |

## Evidence Produced

| Evidence | Format | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|---|
| Feature and policy verification log | Table: claim, source, date | Every account feature and policy claim verified or omitted. |
| Case and rights approval record | Table per case, sample or quote | Each item has approver, date, confidentiality limit and rights. |
| Pilot review record | Table: audience evidence, conversations, website enquiries, accepted opportunities, won work, delivery economics | Self-reported discovery and offline referrals included without exact-attribution claims. |

## Capability and Permission Boundaries

Read and search only; analysis is read-only. Publishing, spend, live account changes, outreach and personal-data processing need explicit, action-specific client authority. Drafting a message or post does not authorise sending it.

## Degraded Mode

Without an account baseline or approved cases, return the narrowest qualified result and mark the affected checks `not assessed`. A discovery plan, profile review checklist and labelled educational pilot can still be delivered.

## Decision Rules

| Condition | Action | Failure or risk avoided |
|---|---|---|
| Expertise belongs to a named practitioner | Lead with the personal profile and support it from the company page | Low-trust corporate-only publishing |
| No profile or page exists, or analytics are not accessible | Produce a set-up plan with assumptions labelled | False optimisation against invented history |
| Analytics show an established presence | Prioritise measured gaps and keep what already works | Destructive reset of working assets |
| A LinkedIn feature, field limit, event or newsletter mechanic is time-sensitive | Verify it against official LinkedIn help before stating it | Stale platform advice |
| The work is the organisation's Company Page, sub-pages or events | Apply [company pages, showcase and events](references/company-pages-showcase-and-events.md) and amplify through willing employees | Faceless page publishing and fragmented sub-pages |
| The consultant has no approved client case studies | Use a clearly labelled educational diagnostic, explain its limits and link to a service page describing scope and acceptance | A demonstration turned into a claimed client win |
| The client is an artist, creative or public figure | Show verified work, credits and commissioning scope, or accurate sourced public information within platform restrictions | Reusing consulting sales copy and assuming reach or endorsement rights |
| The pilot review shows weak results | Decide to continue, revise the proposition, improve the destination, reduce cadence or stop; diagnose before increasing volume | Publishing more into an unexplained failure |

## Quality Standards

- Profile, content and website make the same supportable promise.
- Each content unit contains specific evidence or useful professional judgement.
- Account features and current policy claims are verified or omitted.
- No engagement manipulation, fake cases, fabricated experience or universal targets remain.
- The website and sales owner can handle the next step promised by the content.
- Rights, confidentiality, accessibility and anti-slop reviews are recorded.
- No ranking formula, profile-completion multiplier, universal posting time, engagement quota or conversion benchmark is prescribed from books.

## Anti-Patterns

- Prescribing a universal ratio of personal and company posts. Fix: give each asset a distinct job sized to production capacity.
- Qualifying a profile by an assumed All-Star multiplier or URL pattern. Fix: review identity, headline, opening explanation, experience, credentials, proof and next step.
- Forced curiosity gaps, theatrical reveals, compulsory signature hashtags and formulaic questions. Fix: write a useful opening, develop one defensible idea and stop when the job is done.
- Treating poll responses as market research. Fix: report them as a self-selected sample.
- Organising reciprocal engagement, scraping profiles or sending unsolicited automated sequences. Fix: use the relationship workflow in the consulting reference with the owner's authority.
- Counting a like, profile view or connection acceptance as a qualified lead or marketing permission. Fix: track conversations, enquiries and accepted opportunities as separate stages.
- Inventing twenty posts to satisfy a template. Fix: plan only units with a real buyer question and source.
