# Seven-role LinkedIn content system

Return to [platform-linkedin](../SKILL.md). Use when planning a repeatable mix
for a practitioner or company page. Apply the same planning logic to other
channels only after adapting format, audience and measurement to that channel.

## Evidence boundary

Treat the seven roles supplied by Peter on 2026-09-19 as a planning taxonomy,
not a verified growth formula or a compulsory posting ratio. Keep topic pillars
(what the brand knows) separate from post roles (what a post does) and formats
(how it is delivered). One topic can support several roles and formats.

The located Chris Donnelly post describes five formats, not this exact seven-role
system. Do not attribute the expanded taxonomy or a business valuation to him
as established fact. See the [dated intake and source review](../../../../docs/continuous-improvement/2026-09-19-linkedin-aeo-kaizen.md).

## Choose the job before drafting

| Role | Reader value and drafting rule | Proof or stop condition | Useful observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | State one relevant opinion and explain it simply; use audience questions and prior account evidence to select ideas | Prior popularity is a topic signal, not proof that a claim is true; reject copied hooks and outrage bait | Relevant new followers or profile visits, where available; impressions separately |
| Educational | Teach one practical idea with steps, an example and a limit | Check instructions against current primary evidence; remove unsupported platform tactics | Saves where available, useful questions and visits to the full guide |
| Personal story | Tell a real experience in the author's natural professional voice; connect the detail to a reader lesson | Author approval and rights are required; do not invent lived experience or force intimacy | Substantive replies and relevant conversations |
| Proof of work | Show problem → decision → work → outcome → limitation | Require evidence, dates, baseline, measurement definition and client permission; otherwise describe the process without a result claim | Qualified enquiries referencing the case |
| Building in public | Explain a recent decision, what changed and what remains unresolved | Publish only approved information; distinguish a plan from a shipped change | Informed feedback and repeat participation |
| Lead magnet | Offer a finished resource that solves one named problem; state who it helps and how delivery works | Check the resource and delivery path before promising it; separate resource fulfilment from marketing subscription | Delivered resources, opted-in subscribers and qualified follow-up measured separately |
| Newsjacking | Verify a timely event, identify a relevant consequence and add expert interpretation | Record event date, primary source, review cutoff and correction owner; skip weak relevance or harmful opportunism | Relevant reach and useful discussion, with corrections as a guardrail |

Choose a direct link, question, request or no CTA according to the post's job.
A keyword comment is an optional fulfilment request, not permission to scrape
addresses, enrol someone in marketing, automate messages or contact them
repeatedly. Offer a clear delivery alternative. Follow the existing permission
and current platform-policy gates before executing any outreach.

## Planning and handoff record

Produce one row per proposed post:

`post_id | audience | topic pillar | primary role | account | format | single idea |
hook | source/proof IDs | rights/approval | CTA | canonical destination |
delivery owner | measurement | review date | publish/hold decision`

1. Inspect the audience, offer, existing posts and available production time.
   If account analytics are missing, label the mix a pilot; do not invent a baseline.
2. Select the roles needed for this campaign. Explain omissions; do not force
   all seven into every week. Prefer education when no relevant verified news exists.
3. Write one useful content unit; review its claim, voice, purpose and CTA before
   producing variants. Use ACT or buyer-stage mapping as a cross-check, not a
   second mandatory calendar quota.
4. Link a post to the page that fulfils its promise: education to a guide or
   template, proof to a case study, lead magnet to its delivery page, and an
   integration example to verified integration documentation. Do not send every
   visitor to the home page or force every post to have an external link.
5. Hand the website owner the reader question, approved promise, evidence IDs,
   destination URL, CTA, measurement identifier and update trigger. Keep the
   claim and its qualifications consistent across both surfaces.
6. Review by role and comparable observation window. Separate reach, trust
   signals, resource delivery, subscriptions and qualified enquiries. Never
   interpret likes or comments as qualified leads automatically.

## Small reversible pilot

Choose one audience question and draft an educational post plus a proof-of-work
variant only if approved proof exists. Keep the destination and offer stable.
Record the baseline window, production effort, observation period and minimum
evidence needed for a decision before publication. Test one change at a time.
Treat small samples as directional; do not claim causality from two posts.
Stop or correct on factual error, broken delivery, rights failure or unwanted
contact. Retain, revise or reject the variant with a reason and next review date.

## Worked failure path

A proposed case post claims a large increase but supplies no export or client
approval. Hold the result claim. Use an approved explanation of the decision
and method, or choose education instead. If a news slot has no credible event,
replace it with a useful evergreen post. Neither gap is a reason to invent proof.
