# Instagram Growth Diagnosis and Experiments

Merged from skills/platforms/platform-instagram-growth on 2026-09-29 at cda737c (S04 tree); preservation map: [platform-instagram-growth.md](../../../../docs/kaizen/consolidation-2026-09-29/preservation/platform-instagram-growth.md)

## When to use this reference

Read this reference when the client's question is about growth rather than presence: follower growth has stalled, reach has fallen, the account needs a phase-appropriate growth plan, or the team wants an experiment plan for Reels, hashtags, collaborations or broadcast channels. The parent [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) still governs evidence, permissions and measurement; this reference supplies the growth diagnosis, the phase ladder and the experiment options.

Evidence status. The phase ladder, growth rates, engagement-rate bands, reach ratios, format and signal hierarchies, timing windows and hashtag counts below are carried from legacy practitioner guidance (mainly Hietaniemi 2020, Butow and Walker 2025, Chaffey and Ellis-Chadwick 2022). None has a source-register record. Treat every number as a diagnostic hypothesis to test against the client's own account baseline, never as a universal target or a platform rule, and verify current Instagram behaviour before stating it to a client (verify before stating; no register record). The parent skill's rule stands: no universal save, completion, follower-growth or profile-to-click rate is a success standard. Follower volume that rises while saves, replies or enquiries fall is not success; correct the content or targeting instead.

## Decision rules

| Situation | Action | Failure avoided |
|---|---|---|
| Follower count known | Place the account on the phase ladder (§ Phase ladder) and tailor every recommendation to that phase | A generic plan that fits any account |
| Any of the plateau triggers holds for 3 consecutive weeks | Run the plateau diagnosis (§ Procedure step 3) before adding volume | Publishing more into a suppressed or mis-targeted account |
| Growth depends on reposted or aggregated content | Replace with original work | Demotion of accounts that mainly repost (Butow and Walker 2025; verify before stating) |
| Discovery content is designed graphics, infographics or text-heavy covers | Use authentic photos and video for non-follower discovery; keep designed graphics for existing followers | Graphics that rarely surface on Explore (verify before stating) |
| New followers do not match the persona | Run a follower-quality audit; retarget content | Mismatched followers depressing engagement rate |
| Client proposes purchased followers or follow/unfollow | Refuse; grow through content and engagement only | Permanently corrupted engagement rate, hard-to-recover suppressed reach, damaged trust signals |
| Sponsored or gifted content | Disclose clearly | Undisclosed paid endorsement |
| Paid boost proposed | Boost only proven organic Reels, within approved budget and explicit spend authority | Spending on unproven creative; spend without authority |
| Metric looks like a restriction | Check restriction causes (prohibited content, over-tagging, mass following) before concluding; do not diagnose from a missing interface tab | False shadow-ban diagnosis |

## Phase ladder

Account growth is not linear; each phase has its own dynamics and priorities. Identify the current phase and focus effort there. Benchmarks are the legacy source's "realistic organic" figures; test them against the account baseline.

| Phase | Followers | Dynamics | Priorities | Legacy benchmark (new followers/month) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Foundation | 0–500 | The algorithm has no data on the account; signal quality beats volume | Complete every profile field before posting; post only within one topic for the first 90 days (mixed-niche accounts are said to be penalised in discovery); at least 4 posts a week, with schedule consistency above volume; format priority Reels, then carousels, then static images; comment on 10–15 niche posts a day before and after publishing; no purchased followers | 50–150 |
| 2 Traction | 500–2,000 | Enough data for categorisation; shift to discoverability | Three-tier hashtag sets; daily Stories to stay active on non-post days; reply to every comment within the first hour and to Story DMs promptly; no follow/unfollow; identify 3–5 similar-sized accounts (500–3,000 followers) for mutual shoutouts or Collabs | 100–300 |
| 3 Momentum | 2,000–10,000 | Reels begin reaching non-followers; follower quality matters more than quantity | At least 3 Reels a week (static posts hold the grid but rarely win followers); follower-quality audit; Collabs with micro-influencers of 5,000–30,000 followers; a repeatable weekly schedule, for example Reel Monday, carousel Wednesday, Reel Friday, Stories daily; design posts for saves (educational content, templates, checklists) and shares (relatable, entertaining, aspirational) | 300–800 from consistent Reels |
| 4 Scale | 10,000–50,000 | Brand-partnership enquiries begin; link-in-bio clicks rise | Approach same-niche brands for gifted or paid posts, disclosed; batch content 2–4 weeks ahead with a scheduling tool (Later, Planoly or Meta Business Suite); boost top organic Reels (legacy minimum UGX 50,000 per boost; verify current pricing and obtain spend authority); repurpose Reels to TikTok and YouTube Shorts; link Instagram to WhatsApp Business for enquiries; lead magnet in bio (download or WhatsApp group link) | 1,000–5,000 |
| 5 Authority | 50,000+ | The account is a media property; growth tactics give way to authority and monetisation | Opinion, industry insight and expert commentary; use the audience for press mentions, podcast appearances and speaking, linking back to Instagram; make the account the hub feeding email list, WhatsApp broadcast and YouTube; contract a content producer so the owner focuses on strategy and on-camera presence | Driven by PR, partnerships and virality rather than tactical posting |

## Procedure

1. **Baseline.** Calculate the three ratios from the account's own insights:
   - Follower growth rate = (new followers ÷ starting follower count) × 100, monthly.
   - Engagement rate = (likes + comments + saves + shares) ÷ followers × 100.
   - Reach-to-follower ratio = (average post reach ÷ follower count) × 100.
2. **Compare with legacy bands** (hypotheses, not targets; verify before stating):

   | Phase | Legacy monthly growth rate |
   |---|---|
   | 1 (0–500) | 10–30% |
   | 2 (500–2,000) | 5–15% |
   | 3 (2,000–10,000) | 3–8% |
   | 4 (10,000–50,000) | 2–5% |
   | 5 (50,000+) | 1–3% organic; higher with PR or viral content |

   | Follower count | Legacy "healthy" engagement rate, Uganda/EA accounts |
   |---|---|
   | Under 2,000 | 8–15% |
   | 2,000–10,000 | 5–10% |
   | 10,000–50,000 | 3–6% |
   | 50,000+ | 2–4% |

   The source claims EA accounts outperform global benchmarks through tighter community networks and higher comment-to-like ratios (verify before stating; no register record).

   | Reach-to-follower ratio | Legacy reading |
   |---|---|
   | Healthy | 20–40% for feed posts; 40–80% for Reels |
   | Warning | Under 15% for feed posts: audit content quality, hashtag sets and posting frequency |
   | Critical | Under 10%: possible restriction, over-reliance on static posts, or an engagement-rate mismatch from purchased followers |
3. **Plateau diagnosis.** Investigate when any of these holds for 3 consecutive weeks: follower growth below 1% a month with no seasonal explanation; average Reel reach below 50% of follower count; engagement rate down more than 30% on the previous 30-day average; reach from hashtags near zero in Insights. Then:
   1. Audit the last 20 posts for content consistency and niche focus.
   2. Check for posts that may have triggered a restriction (prohibited content, over-tagging, mass-following activity).
   3. Review hashtag performance in Insights and rotate the hashtag sets.
   4. Publish 3–5 Reels over the next 2 weeks and monitor reach recovery.
   5. Check the current Instagram ranking diagnostic in [meta-algorithm-guide](../../../meta-analytics-ops/meta-algorithm-guide/SKILL.md).
4. **Choose experiments** from the levers below that match the phase and the diagnosed cause. Give each one hypothesis, one change, a measurement window, a stop rule and an owner. Change one lever at a time where capacity allows.
5. **Schedule** the format mix to stated capacity and write a 30-day engagement calendar that lists community actions (engage before posting, comment responses, proactive engagement on larger accounts) as tasks separate from publishing.
6. **Review** against qualified actions and enquiries as well as follower and reach movement; keep, revise or stop each experiment.

## Experiment levers

### Ranking signals (legacy model; verify current behaviour)

- The "3 Rs" (Chaffey and Ellis-Chadwick 2022): **relevance** (does the content match what the viewer engages with; post within one niche), **recency** (newer posts rank higher; a consistent schedule on the same days helps the account be indexed reliably) and **resonance** (saves, shares and comment depth signal more than passive likes).
- Legacy 2024–2025 signal order, highest first: shares to Stories or DMs; saves (educational carousels, checklists, how-to posts); comments (a question or thread outweighs a five-word comment); likes (many likes with few saves or shares gives limited reach).
- Legacy format order for non-follower reach: Reels (the main discovery tool); carousels (high dwell time; may be shown to followers again if they did not swipe through); static images (limited non-follower reach; use for grid cohesion and follower engagement); Stories (followers only; a retention tool, not a growth tool).
- 2024 changes reported by the source: original content prioritised over reposts and aggregation (Butow and Walker 2025); Explore surfaces photos and video to new audiences, rarely designed graphics or text-heavy carousel covers. Verify both before stating.

### Early-engagement window

In the 30 minutes before publishing, leave genuine (not emoji-only) comments on 5–10 niche posts. In the 30 minutes after publishing: reply to every comment, with a question where it extends the thread; reply to Stories mentions of the post; keep engaging on 5–10 peers' posts; stay present in the app. The source's rationale is that early engagement decides wider distribution and embeds the account in a community cluster (verify before stating). Respond to every comment within the first hour; delete only spam or abuse, because negative comments are still engagement.

### Profile for discovery

The source states that Instagram search indexes username, display name and bio (the parent skill warns not to claim only certain fields are indexed; verify current searchable fields).

- Username: include the primary keyword (`@kampala.skincare`, not `@beautybykamini`); avoid numbers, underscores and hard-to-type characters.
- Display name: keyword plus location, for example "Kampala Skincare | Natural Products".
- Bio: what the account does (one keyword-rich line), who it is for, location, one call to action ("DM us on WhatsApp" or "Order via link below"), at most 3–4 emoji as separators. Avoid vague phrases ("Bringing you the best…", "Official page of…"), long paragraphs and multiple calls to action.
- Highlights as a navigation menu: 5–7 at most; single-word names ("Products", "Reviews", "Team", "Events", "FAQ"); the most commercially important first (left); refresh every 30–60 days.
- Link: one WhatsApp click-to-chat link for accounts selling or taking enquiries directly (the source's preferred option for Uganda/EA); a Linktree or Beacons page when several destinations are needed; never a bare homepage; link a product, contact or booking page.

### Hashtag clusters (legacy tiers; parent skill warns against arbitrary counts and size tiers, so test them)

Legacy rationale from the original skill (before the five-tag cap): thirty broad tags with hundreds of millions of posts bury content; relevance-built clusters raise the chance of a targeted feed.

| Tier | Legacy size band | Purpose | Example (fashion, Uganda) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Niche | Under 50K posts | Best chance of ranking; targeted audience | `#KampalafashionBlogger`, `#UgandanDesigner` |
| Mid-range | 50K–500K posts | Moderate competition; relevant community | `#EastAfricanFashion`, `#AfricanStreetStyle` |
| Broad | 500K–5M posts | Exposure; short time at the top | `#AfricanFashion`, `#FashionBlogger` |

Legacy rules (superseded counts, kept for history only): avoid tags over 5 million posts for small accounts; tags in the caption rather than the first comment. Research: search a seed niche keyword (for example "Kampala food"); note the top 5–10 tags on the most-engaged posts (not the most-followed accounts); check volumes and build a shortlist across tiers; rotate sets of at most five tags; audit monthly with the hashtag-reach metric in Insights; use the "Related" suggestions and filter for EA tags. Current cap: Instagram's @creators account announced on 18 Dec 2025 that Instagram allows up to five hashtags in a reel or post (register `INSTAGRAM-HASHTAG-LIMIT-PRIMARY`, primary; secondary report `INSTAGRAM-HASHTAG-LIMIT-2025`). Earlier per-post counts of 8–15 or 20–30 are withdrawn. Whether the rollout reached every account is `NOT_ASSESSED`; confirm in the live app. For a full hashtag strategy document, use [caption-writer's hashtag reference](../../../content-writing/caption-writer/references/hashtag-and-keyword-tagging.md). All counts: verify before stating.

- Geo-tagging: tag the city or neighbourhood for local reach, the country for national campaigns. Hietaniemi (2020) estimates a location tag lifts engagement by over 80%; treat it as directional, not precise.
- Branded hashtag from day one (for example `#KampalaOrganicsByAmara`) in the bio, captions, packaging, receipts and email signatures, as the collection point for UGC and a community search hub.
- AR filter mechanic (legacy): a custom filter that users must follow the account to access, so every share carries the account name. The source names Meta's free Spark AR; Meta is understood to have closed Spark AR to third-party effects in January 2025 (reviewer note, not verified; no register record), so treat this mechanic as unavailable unless a current tool is confirmed live.

### Reels

- Hook in the first 2 seconds: visual surprise, bold statement or direct question; a first-frame text overlay readable without sound; no slow intro, logo or scene-setting.
- Completion: the source treats completion rate as the main growth signal and 70%+ as strong (verify before stating). Keep Reels to 15–30 seconds for Uganda/EA audiences because of data costs and interrupted mobile connections; end with a payoff (reveal, punchline, result or call to action); loop the last frame into the first.
- Audio: trending audio may surface the Reel on that audio's page (check the trending arrow in the audio library); original audio builds a branded sound and suits strong brand personalities; voiceover Reels suit educational and how-to content in the EA market. Choose sound for meaning and commercial rights (parent skill).
- After publishing: reply to every comment at once; share to Stories with a sticker ("Watch this", "Have you tried this?"); send by DM to 5–10 contacts who would genuinely find it useful.

### Collaboration and cross-growth

- Collabs: one co-authored post in both feeds; choose same-niche partners of similar or slightly larger size; content that serves both audiences, not pure promotion; both accounts must accept before it publishes (verify current mechanics).
- Micro-influencer exchange: accounts of 1,000–20,000 followers in the same niche and geography; mutual Stories shoutout or joint Reel, usually unpaid at similar sizes; track spikes in Insights (Followers > From collaborations; verify the path).
- Stories mentions: reshare customer, partner and peer mentions at once; tag relevant accounts in Stories rather than feed posts, where over-tagging is said to be penalised.
- Live with a guest ("Go Live With"): notifies both audiences; for Q&A, launches and event coverage; announce 24–48 hours ahead with a countdown sticker; 20–35 minutes for EA audiences because of data costs.
- Creator fees, rights and disclosures follow [instagram-format-discovery-and-sales-method.md § Discovery and creator partnerships](instagram-format-discovery-and-sales-method.md).

### Community engagement

- Proactive engagement on 5–10 larger same-niche accounts (10,000–100,000 followers): substantive comments within 30 minutes of their posts; nothing generic ("Great post!", "Love this"); the aim is to put the account's name before their audience.
- Stories stickers: polls with two options ("Which do you prefer? A or B"); questions to source FAQs, ideas and testimonials; quizzes for educational accounts; answer every Story reply by DM.

### Broadcast channels

The source describes Instagram broadcast channels as one-to-many messages (text, photo, video, poll) delivered to subscribers as DMs, outside feed ranking. The parent skill requires this delivery and notification behaviour to be verified as current, not presented as a guaranteed bypass of ranking. Legacy rationale: retained, engaged subscribers signal content quality; a subscriber who has had 3–4 channel messages arrives at a feed post warm; the relationship depends less on algorithm changes. Use:

- Phase 2–3 (500–10,000): launch the channel, announce it in 3 consecutive Stories with a countdown sticker, and give a specific reason to join ("I'll share my weekly content schedule and post templates here only").
- Phase 4+ (10,000+): make it the main channel for launches, exclusive previews and time-sensitive offers.
- Promote from the profile ("Join Channel" button) and in the call to action of every high-performing Reel.

## Release checklist

- [ ] Current growth phase identified from the ladder and every recommendation tailored to it.
- [ ] Concrete weekly schedule with a format mix (Reels, carousels, Stories, static) matched to stated capacity.
- [ ] A researched hashtag shortlist across the three tiers for the client's niche and Uganda/EA geography, rotated in sets of at most five tags per post (INSTAGRAM-HASHTAG-LIMIT-2025, partial; confirm live), with counts verified.
- [ ] Every tactic justified by its expected effect on reach, completion, saves or shares, not by aesthetic preference alone, and each carried number marked as a hypothesis or verified.
- [ ] Phase-appropriate growth benchmarks drawn from the account baseline, not aspirational projections.
- [ ] 30-day engagement calendar with community actions as tasks distinct from publishing.
- [ ] Uganda/EA context: 15–30-second Reels, WhatsApp call to action in bio, English and local vernacular mix in caption tone, data-cost awareness in video length.
- [ ] 2–3 named collaboration targets (handles or account-type descriptions) for Collabs or micro-influencer exchange.
- [ ] Experiments carry hypothesis, window, stop rule, owner and any spend or messaging authority.
