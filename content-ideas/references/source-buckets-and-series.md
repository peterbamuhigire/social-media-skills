# Source Buckets And Series

Use this reference when generating platform ideas that must feel fresh without becoming random.

Primary sources:

- Danny Iny et al., *Blog Post Ideas*
- Ann Handley, *Everybody Writes*

## Core rule

Generate ideas from source buckets and arrange them into repeatable series.

## Strong source buckets

- frequently asked questions
- objections
- proof and testimonials
- process and behind-the-scenes
- timely events and seasonal hooks
- audience mistakes
- community stories
- product or service use-cases

## Series logic

Every quarterly idea bank should include a few repeatable formats such as:

- ask us anything
- myth of the week
- customer spotlight
- behind-the-scenes diary
- one useful tip

## Quality standard

Each idea should imply:

- who it is for
- why it matters
- which platform fits it best
- what next action it supports
