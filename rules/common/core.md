# Core Rules — Social Media Engine

> Distilled from this engine's own `CLAUDE.md`.

## Every article or thought-leadership piece is researched first — never from assumed knowledge

Same standard as the website engine: a live research wave via
`digital-research-engine`, one research agent per cohort/region, synthesis done
by the orchestrator. Social cut-downs inherit the article's verified intent and
source map — they do not get a lighter evidence bar just because they are short.

## Attribution is mandatory; never fabricate a citation

Cite real, locatable sources. Name the researchers, institutions, and
regulators whose work is drawn on. Mark anything unverifiable as **UNVERIFIED**.

## `SKILL.md` files stay under 500 lines; detail moves to `references/`

A skill file over 500 lines is a sign the deep material belongs in a linked
reference file, not that the limit should be waived.

## British English throughout — never American spellings

organisation, colour, programme, behaviour, analyse, strategise, recognise,
centre, enquiry — consistently, across every deliverable.

## Imperative language in skill instructions

"Ask for…", "Generate…", "Apply…", "Include…" — not "you should" or "Claude
will". This keeps skill files usable as direct instructions rather than
descriptions of behaviour.
