## Section 1 — Persona discipline

Build personas as people in situations — a fictional name, a setting and a problem in their own words — from evidence, not wishes. Pick one primary persona whose solution still works for the others, never blend users into an average, use the persona to say no to scope creep, keep it in view on every brief, check it is not flatteringly perfect, and test new-offer personas with problem interviews.

Canonical reference: [`skills/pipeline/03-audience-personas/references/persona-discipline.md`](../skills/pipeline/03-audience-personas/references/persona-discipline.md).

## Section 2 — Brief screening: is this a strategy yet?

Before accepting a digital-product or experience brief, check that it has a fundable business result, behavioural evidence from real customers, a reason buyers would switch, the capability to deliver, a way to learn and adjust after launch, and coverage of every touchpoint. A brief that is only an exciting idea, a feature list, fashionable wording, an expert's say-so, an untested assumption or a slogan returns to intake with questions. Keep strategy (what to make and why) and design (making it) as separate jobs with separate owners.

Canonical reference: [`skills/pipeline/01-client-brief/references/ux-strategy-and-product-lenses.md`](../skills/pipeline/01-client-brief/references/ux-strategy-and-product-lenses.md) §1.

## Section 3 — Five outcomes applied to campaigns

A premium campaign or destination must pass all five; one "no" holds the launch:

| # | Outcome | Campaign check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Useful | Addresses the persona's stated goal, not a vanity metric |
| 2 | Easy | Understood at a glance; one clear CTA per asset |
| 3 | Efficient | Scannable copy; the image carries the message before text loads |
| 4 | Pleasing | Quality matches the brand's positioning (visual execution via `design-system-skills`) |
| 5 | Accessible | Alt text, captions, sufficient contrast, plain language; web targets per `website-skills` (WCAG 2.2 AA is the current W3C Recommendation, register CW-05, checked 2026-09-23) |
