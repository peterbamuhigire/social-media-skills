# First-wave campaign unit fixture

Status: IMPLEMENTED FIXTURE SPECIFICATION, not a published campaign. Date: 7 September 2026.

The fixture operationalises S01–S05. It requires a labelled brief, one audience, one channel job, one honest action, source and rights register, rendered asset, approval owner, response path, metric definition and reviewer.

## Normal and failure cases

Normal: the unit matches the channel job, uses approved evidence and rights, remains readable in the target treatment, receives human editorial approval and has a defined post-publication decision. Failure: unsupported claim, missing release, generic copy, stale platform rule, inaccessible render, denied account, complaint or conflicting metric. Failure remains visible as `NOT_ASSESSED`, blocked or rejected and routes to its owner.

`skills/ai-marketing/anti-ai-slop/SKILL.md` is necessary but insufficient: a detector result cannot establish audience truth, cultural fit, rights, usefulness or outcome. Human review records the concrete editorial decision and reason for adopt, iterate, pause or reject.
