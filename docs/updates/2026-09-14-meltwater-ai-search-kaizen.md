# Meltwater AI-search Kaizen — 2026-09-14

The social-media engine now uses a qualified implementation synthesis of the
Meltwater/LinkedIn AI-search report. The synthesis routes AI-search-aware work
through generative-search, LinkedIn presence, Company Page, and social strategy
skills; it treats the report's B2B citation findings as sample-bound hypotheses.

The engine now requires authors to preserve source scope, denominator, date,
model/product, locale, and attribution limits; to separate observed AI citation
from engagement, referral, and conversion; and to use credible expert and
Company Page distribution without manufacturing authority, UGC, reviews, or
citations. Legacy LinkedIn platform claims are explicitly volatile and require
current official verification before client-facing use.

The report and supporting URLs are recorded in the source register with tier,
freshness, uncertainty, and review dates. No raw DOCX extraction or universal
ranking claim was added.
