# Social Media Skills — Kaizen Adoption Plan

## Product scope

Social strategies, platform plans, campaigns, calendars, content, AI-assisted marketing, community playbooks, reports, training, decks, and policy assets.

## P0 changes

- Add the Kaizen skill to the engine router and pre-ship workflow.
- Add product/campaign audits that score audience value, evidence, cultural fit, accessibility, rights/legal, platform execution, measurement, handoff, and learning instrumentation.
- Strengthen `meta-testing-framework`, `meta-reporting`, and evergreen-content guidance with hypothesis, guardrail, decision rule, and standardisation fields.
- Add source-register review dates for platform, policy, market, AI, and legal claims.

## P1 changes

- Add a sanitised campaign fixture with baseline, hypothesis, creative variants, measurement, review, and next-cycle decision.
- Use Digital Storytelling/Dynamic Characters for visual narrative and character/brand expression; route visual production to design-system-skills.
- Use Paid for Your Perspective for expert positioning, evidence-bounded thought leadership, screening/compliance, and knowledge-product development.
- Use Nonprofit Guide and Facility Move patterns for mission-led campaigns, stakeholder communication, operational change, and continuity.

## Acceptance evidence

Run `validate_skill_engine.py`, routing smoke test, source-freshness checks, creative/legal-market gates, anti-slop audit, and a measurement-proof review. Keep an explicit learn/standardise/reject record.
