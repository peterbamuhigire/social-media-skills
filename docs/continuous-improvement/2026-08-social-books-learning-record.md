# Kaizen learning record: social-media book batch

**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Cycle:** Observe -> baseline -> select -> experiment -> check -> standardise -> re-measure

## Observe and baseline

Nine locally supplied books were reviewed against the current engine. The engine already had strong Kaizen, healthcare, listening, review, policy and measurement routes. The main cross-cutting gaps were a reusable audience-affordance card, pragmatic conversation repair, explicit attention/trust guardrails, and stronger screening of social-media job and income claims.

The book-by-book synthesis that was stored in `book-extractions/` was retired on 2026-09-23 under the no-stored-extractions rule. Its durable lessons now live in [the social operating system and pragmatics reference](../../skills/pipeline/06-digital-marketing-strategy/references/social-operating-system-and-pragmatics.md), which cites the nine titles briefly.

## Select and experiment

The selected improvements are deliberately small:

1. Add one reusable operating reference for audience motive, platform affordance, participation, conversation repair and privacy boundaries.
2. Require the reference in social strategy and channel architecture decisions.
3. Add conversation classification and public-to-private handoff controls to community and policy work.
4. Add at least one trust, attention or safety guardrail to measurement decisions.
5. Add a source-and-proof ladder for creator jobs, monetisation and earnings claims.

## Check

The experiment runs from 2026-08-06 to 2026-09-05. It will be checked on 2026-09-07 using two strategy artefacts, three community cases, two reports and three creator recommendations. The acceptance evidence (named cards, cases and reports) was specified in the retired synthesis and is carried by the operating reference above. No change is promoted to a universal template without this check.

## Standardise or reject

The engine maintainer will standardise only changes that improve decision quality, safety or traceability while remaining feasible for the relevant skill. A rejected change remains documented with its reason; rejection is a valid Kaizen outcome when the intervention does not earn its operating cost.
