# Hospitality, hotel and restaurant Kaizen - 2026-09-14

## Change

Added a hospitality social route for accommodation, dining, bars, events,
catering and food businesses. It connects discover, trust, choose, act,
experience and return content to an owned booking/reservation/enquiry path.
It adds UGC rights, review response, food/privacy/safeguarding escalation,
offer conditions, canonical website facts and qualified-action measurement.

## Evidence discipline

The supplied books informed durable principles around concept, people, service,
quality, culture, operations, financial realism and continuous improvement.
Social posts, reviews, creator claims and platform mechanics are not treated as
independent proof. Current platform/legal/market claims route through the social
source register and currentness gate. No fake reviews, synthetic mentions,
unpermissioned guest content or AI-citation promise is allowed.

## Experiment and gate

Hypothesis: content mapped to a guest occasion and staffed conversion path will
produce better qualified actions than reach-only planning. Measure qualified
enquiries/bookings, response time, fulfilment, review themes and attribution;
keep reach as a diagnostic. Rollback by unlinking the route. Next review:
2026-10-14; live account and conversion evidence are `NOT_ASSESSED`.
