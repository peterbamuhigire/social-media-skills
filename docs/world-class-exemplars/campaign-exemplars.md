# Finished campaign exemplars

These five complete, fictional examples demonstrate the engine’s campaign contract across common sectors. Names, budgets, dates, targets and results are illustrative—not benchmarks, observed outcomes or claims about real organisations. Replace every assumption with an approved brief and current evidence before use.

Each example ends at an approval-ready campaign pack. Live spend, publishing, legal approval and certification require separate authority.

## 1. B2B: Kampala facilities-management lead campaign

### Brief and strategic choice

**Scenario:** “Lakeview Facilities”, a fictional Kampala provider, needs qualified meetings with operations managers at organisations occupying multi-site premises. Illustrative six-week media budget: UGX 9,000,000.

**Choice:** Lead with the cost and disruption of reactive maintenance, not a generic “complete solutions” promise. LinkedIn builds professional recognition; a practical checklist captures intent; email and opted-in WhatsApp handle follow-up. Exclude consumer audiences and unverified job-title precision.

**Objective:** Produce 30 sales-accepted leads and 12 held discovery meetings within six weeks, subject to agreed CRM definitions.

**Audience:** Operations, administration and procurement decision-makers in named Kampala-area organisations. Verify role and organisation at qualification; do not infer sensitive attributes.

**Proposition and proof needed:** “Find preventable maintenance gaps before they become closures.” Proof: documented inspection method, named service coverage, response SLA approved by operations, and an attributable client reference or no testimonial.

### Campaign sequence

| Week | Asset and message | Channel/job | CTA | Evidence captured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Document post: seven signs maintenance is becoming reactive | LinkedIn awareness | Save checklist | Post ID, impressions, saves |
| 2 | 45-second technician walkthrough with captions | LinkedIn credibility | Download site-check sheet | View definition, clicks, asset version |
| 3 | Lead form offering a two-page inspection checklist | LinkedIn conversion | Request checklist | Form consent, valid lead, UTM |
| 4 | Case-method post: issue → inspection → action; no invented result | LinkedIn proof | Book a scoping call | Claim evidence, landing event |
| 5 | Email to consented leads with three qualification questions | Owned follow-up | Reply with site count | Delivery, reply, CRM status |
| 6 | WhatsApp reminder only for channel-specific opt-ins | Assisted conversion | Confirm meeting slot | Opt-in ID, reply, meeting status |

**Sample lead copy:** “A leaking valve is rarely the costly part. The costly part is discovering it after a work area closes. Use this seven-point inspection sheet to review your sites before the next emergency call-out.”

### Measurement and release

Primary decision chain: spend → valid leads → sales-accepted leads → held meetings → qualified opportunities. Reconcile platform leads to CRM records weekly. Stop or revise if lead validity falls below the pre-agreed guardrail after a sufficient sample; do not optimise to form fills alone.

Creative gate: exact service coverage and SLA verified; technician consent and location clearance retained; document readable on mobile; no fabricated savings. Legal gate: lead notice, purpose, retention and processor access recorded. Handoff: final asset register, audience, exclusions, budget authority, UTM map, CRM definitions and proof pack owner.

## 2. NGO: regional maternal-health knowledge campaign

### Brief and strategic choice

**Scenario:** “Safe Start Network”, a fictional EAC NGO, must increase use of a verified antenatal-care information service in Uganda and Kenya. Illustrative eight-week production and distribution budget: UGX 18,000,000 equivalent.

**Choice:** Use trusted midwives to answer practical questions and route people to verified services. Do not use fear, diagnose individuals, collect health details in public comments or imply that social content replaces care.

**Objective:** Generate 6,000 completed visits to the verified service directory and 1,000 saves/shares of approved guidance, while maintaining zero uncorrected medical misinformation incidents.

**Audience:** Adults seeking maternal-health information and family supporters. Language and location variants require local clinical and language review.

**Evidence needed:** Current ministry/clinical guidance, service directory owners, emergency wording, source date, reviewer name and referral limitations.

### Campaign sequence

| Phase | Asset and message | Channel/job | CTA | Safeguard/evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare | Question log from field teams, stripped of identifiers | Internal insight | Approve themes | Data-minimisation review |
| Explain | Midwife vertical videos: one question, one bounded answer | Facebook/Instagram reach | View verified guide | Clinical approval, captions |
| Equip | Kiswahili/English carousel: what to prepare for a visit | Social utility | Save/share | Native-language review |
| Refer | Location-aware directory posts without individual diagnosis | Social conversion | Find a service | Directory freshness check |
| Respond | Moderation protocol for emergencies and misinformation | Community safety | Private referral route | Escalation log |
| Learn | Anonymous question themes and directory completion | Reporting | Revise content | Aggregated evidence only |

**Sample copy:** “Not every pregnancy follows the same timetable. If you are unsure about a symptom, contact a qualified health worker. This guide explains what to carry to an antenatal visit and where to find verified services.”

### Measurement and release

Track completed directory visits—not link clicks alone—plus saves/shares, misinformation corrections within SLA, referrals and content-language variant. Never claim health outcomes from social metrics. Stop publication if clinical guidance, directory entries, emergency wording or language review is unavailable.

Creative gate: dignity, non-stigmatising imagery, subtitle accuracy, low-bandwidth version and no graphic fear device. Legal gate: health-data collection avoided unless a separately approved service requires it; identifiable stories need specific releases. Handoff includes approval matrix, source register, moderator scripts, incident log and measurement proof pack.

## 3. Retail: WhatsApp-first Kampala homeware launch

### Brief and strategic choice

**Scenario:** “Kato Home”, a fictional Kampala homeware retailer, launches a limited tableware range. Illustrative four-week paid budget: UGX 6,000,000; inventory, prices and delivery zones must come from the retailer’s live records.

**Choice:** Show the product in ordinary Kampala hosting situations, state confirmed prices, and route opted-in buyers to a structured WhatsApp order flow. Do not use fabricated scarcity, static payment fees or unsupported “best quality” claims.

**Objective:** Achieve 120 paid and fulfilled launch orders at an approved contribution threshold, with opt-out and complaint rates inside client guardrails.

**Audience:** Adults in verified delivery zones who have shown relevant shopping intent; existing contacts receive messages only where the recorded opt-in covers promotions.

**Proof needed:** SKU/stock export, price approval, material/care specifications, delivery fees and times, product image rights, payment account and returns terms.

### Campaign sequence

| Day | Asset | Channel/job | CTA | Operations evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −7 | Place-setting reveal with exact included pieces | Instagram/Facebook discovery | Join launch alerts | Consent source/scope |
| −3 | Short product demo and care instructions | Reels/TikTok consideration | View catalogue | SKU and claim check |
| 0 | Price, stock and delivery-zone launch card | Social conversion | WhatsApp to order | Timestamped stock/price |
| 0 | Approved WhatsApp template to opted-in segment | WhatsApp conversion | Reply with SKU/quantity | Template and cohort ID |
| +2 | Packing process and real dispatch cut-off | Stories trust | Order before cut-off | Operations approval |
| +7 | Consented customer image or product FAQ | Social proof/service | Ask a question | Release or no UGC use |

**Sample copy:** “The six-piece stoneware set is UGX [approved price], before delivery. Available colours and today’s stock are in the catalogue. Send the SKU and quantity on WhatsApp; we will confirm the total and delivery window before you pay.”

### Measurement and release

Reconcile ad spend, WhatsApp enquiries, valid orders, paid orders, fulfilment, refunds and contribution. A screenshot of a payment message is not finance reconciliation. Pause promotion when stock or fulfilment capacity is uncertain; recover with an updated inventory cut and revised creative.

Creative gate: actual product/pack size, legible price, no misleading crop, confirmed scarcity. Legal gate: WhatsApp opt-in provenance and opt-out, privacy notice, returns/consumer terms, UGC rights. Handoff includes SKU-to-asset map, catalogue version, order script, response SLA, stock owner and proof pack.

## 4. Public sector: municipal waste-service information campaign

### Brief and strategic choice

**Scenario:** “Kampala East Municipal Service”, fictional, changes collection days in four zones. The campaign is public information, not political persuasion.

**Choice:** Make the correct service action easy to find by zone, date and language. Use social posts to route residents to an authoritative schedule and staffed help channel; do not collect party preference or use public resources for partisan promotion.

**Objective:** Reach the affected zones before the change date, achieve 80% correct-answer performance in a small intercept comprehension check, and resolve schedule misinformation within the approved SLA.

**Audience:** Residents and businesses in the four zones, including people with limited data, literacy or accessibility. Offline notice and radio are complementary; social reach is not population coverage.

**Evidence needed:** Signed service schedule, zone map, effective date, public contact, language approvals, accessibility review and record owner.

### Campaign sequence

| Timing | Asset | Channel/job | CTA | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −14 days | Change notice: zone, old day, new day, effective date | Facebook/X awareness | Check your zone | Signed schedule |
| −10 | Low-data image and text-only version | WhatsApp/community distribution | Forward unchanged | Version hash/owner |
| −7 | Map carousel plus alt text | Facebook/Instagram explanation | Open accessible schedule | Map and accessibility QA |
| −3 | Reminder in approved local-language variants | Social/radio alignment | Save the date | Native review |
| 0 | Service-day status and help contact | Social operations | Report a missed collection | Moderation/SLA log |
| +7 | Correction and question summary | Public accountability | View updated FAQ | Change log |

**Sample copy:** “Collection in Zone B moves from Wednesday to Friday from 7 August. Put waste out by 7:00 a.m. The other zones are listed in the signed schedule: [authoritative URL]. For help, contact [approved service channel].”

### Measurement and release

Measure authoritative-page visits, help requests by issue, corrected misinformation, service complaints and comprehension sample. Do not treat impressions as people informed. Separate operational complaints from political sentiment and retain only necessary personal data.

Creative gate: date/zone legibility, language equivalence, alt text, no partisan marks or celebratory filler. Legal gate: public-information authority, records/retention, accessibility and privacy review; current WhatsApp government-use policy if used. Handoff includes signed source, version control, correction authority, escalation roster and public archive.

## 5. Creator: disclosed tourism partnership

### Brief and strategic choice

**Scenario:** “Rwenzori Weekends”, a fictional domestic-tourism operator, contracts a Ugandan creator for a hosted two-night itinerary. Illustrative creator and amplification budget: UGX 12,000,000.

**Choice:** Build the story around a real itinerary and clearly disclosed hosted experience. The creator keeps an authentic viewpoint but cannot invent availability, safety, environmental or “typical result” claims. Paid amplification is a separately licensed use.

**Objective:** Generate 400 attributable itinerary-page visits, 80 qualified enquiries and 20 confirmed bookings within the tracked window, subject to verified booking records.

**Audience:** Adults considering domestic leisure travel. Exclude unsafe targeting or messaging; confirm age suitability for any activity.

**Proof needed:** Contract, itinerary, included/excluded costs, live availability, operator licences/insurance representations reviewed by the client, location permissions, music/footage rights and disclosure plan.

### Campaign sequence

| Asset | Creator treatment | Brand control | CTA/evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teaser Story | “Hosted trip” stated on first commercial frame | Factual itinerary check | Tagged link/asset ID |
| 45–60 second video | Honest arrival-to-activity narrative; material relationship disclosed | Claims, safety and rights review | Tracked itinerary link |
| Carousel | Cost inclusions, exclusions and preparation notes | Price/date approval | Save/share + page visit |
| Live Q&A | Creator experience separated from operator policy | Escalation script for safety/refunds | Question log, no private data aired |
| Reminder Story | Confirmed availability only | Same-day inventory check | Enquiry link |
| Post-campaign reflection | What the creator genuinely liked and would prepare differently | No script that changes opinion | Retained disclosure/post snapshot |

**Sample copy:** “Hosted by Rwenzori Weekends. They covered this two-night itinerary; these are my own observations. The package includes [verified inclusions] and excludes [verified exclusions]. Current dates and prices are at [tracked link].”

### Measurement and release

Record asset-level link visits, valid enquiries, confirmed bookings, cancellations/refunds and paid-amplification spend. Compare creator-reported metrics with platform evidence and booking records. Do not infer destination-wide impact or causal lift without a credible counterfactual.

Creative gate: disclosure before truncation, accurate itinerary, genuine experience, captions, location/safety context and no prohibited music. Legal gate: contract, likeness/location/music rights, usage term/territory, takedown and disclosure evidence. Handoff includes contract summary, content/rights matrix, approval log, posted snapshots, platform export and booking reconciliation.

## Acceptance checklist for all exemplars

- The scenario is explicitly fictional and illustrative figures cannot be mistaken for benchmarks or observed results.
- Objective, audience, strategic choice, proof requirements, sequence, sample content, measurement and stop conditions are complete.
- Every material claim has a named evidence source or remains a placeholder that blocks release.
- The [creative review gate](../quality-gates/creative-review-gate.md), [legal/market gate](../quality-gates/legal-market-release-gate.md) and [measurement proof pack](../evidence-packs/measurement-proof-pack.md) are part of handoff.
- Missing tools, access, rendering, legal review, source evidence or live account data produces a qualified partial pack, never a fabricated pass.
