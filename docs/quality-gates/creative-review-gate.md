# Creative review gate

Apply this gate to campaign concepts, copy, creator briefs, image/video briefs and completed content before client approval or publication. Review the actual asset in context, including caption, crop, sound, subtitles, destination and disclosure. A brief alone cannot pass a finished-asset check.

## Evidence packet

Record: asset ID/version; campaign objective and audience; platform/placement; source brief; final text and visual/audio description; claim sources; rights releases; accessibility evidence; platform/legal gate; reviewer names; decision; corrections; approval timestamp.

## Scored review

Score each dimension 0–3: `0 absent/unsafe`, `1 major repair`, `2 acceptable with minor repair`, `3 release-ready`. Cite an observable feature for every score.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic job | No decision or audience link | Generic objective | Clear job, weak priority | One clear audience action tied to the objective |
| Message clarity | Meaning is missing/misleading | Competing messages | Main point is recoverable | Main point and next action are immediate |
| Distinctiveness | Interchangeable category copy | Mostly generic conventions | Some ownable cues | Recognisable voice, proof and brand memory cues |
| Evidence and honesty | Fabricated/unsupported material claim | Claim exceeds evidence | Qualified and mostly traced | All material claims, prices and scarcity traced |
| Cultural and market fit | Stereotype, exclusion or wrong market | Unchecked imported assumption | Locally intelligible | Specific, respectful and validated for the named audience |
| Platform execution | Prohibited or unusable | Wrong format/behaviour | Technically usable | Native format, hook, pacing, CTA and destination align |
| Craft | Errors or incoherent structure | Formulaic/overwritten | Clean with minor weakness | Precise language, purposeful detail and controlled rhythm |
| Accessibility | Critical content inaccessible | Major caption/contrast/reading gaps | Core meaning accessible | Captions/transcript, reading order, alt-text brief and legibility checked |
| Rights and safety | Missing permission or unsafe content | Material uncertainty | Evidence present with minor admin gap | Rights, privacy, safeguarding and disclosure evidence complete |
| Measurement readiness | No trackable action | Metric unrelated to objective | Metric and link/event named | Asset ID, UTM/event, hypothesis and decision rule recorded |

## Release rules

- Pass requires at least 24/30, no zero, and scores of 3 for evidence/honesty and rights/safety.
- Scores 18–23 require revision and rerun; a client preference cannot waive evidence, rights or safety.
- Below 18, any zero, or any unresolved legal/platform restriction is a fail.
- `Not assessed` is not a numeric score. If the actual asset, rendering, audio, destination, evidence or reviewer is unavailable, withhold approval and name the missing check.

## Red-team prompts

1. What would a sceptical audience reasonably infer that the copy never states?
2. Which word, crop, soundtrack, edit or testimonial changes the legal or factual claim?
3. Could another brand in the category publish this unchanged?
4. Does the work depend on a stereotype, imported idiom, stock “African” imagery or false local detail?
5. Can the intended person understand the offer, price, deadline, risk and next step on a mid-range phone?
6. What evidence would disprove the creative hypothesis, and is that signal collected?

## Common failure corrections

- “Professional-looking” with no observable criteria → score the actual placement against the table.
- Generic empowerment, innovation or community language → replace it with a concrete tension, action and proof.
- A visual implies results the caption qualifies away → make the whole asset match the evidence.
- Disclosure hidden after truncation or only in a bio → place an unambiguous disclosure with the endorsement.
- Engagement used as success for a conversion asset → attach the intended event and decision threshold.
- Accessibility reviewed from a script only → inspect the rendered asset, captions, timing and reading order.
