# Launch Channel Sequencing

Use this reference when channel architecture must support a timed campaign, launch, enrolment window, or event.

Primary source:

- Jeff Walker, *Launch*

## Core rule

Assign channels by sequence role, not only by platform popularity.

## Sequence roles

- teaser channel
- education channel
- proof channel
- conversion channel
- retention channel
- close-out channel

One platform can serve more than one stage across time, but its role at each stage must be explicit.

## Typical mapping

- discovery platforms: teaser and reach
- owned or direct channels: conversion and close
- community channels: proof and objection handling

## Output standard

A strong channel architecture should show:

- where the first attention comes from
- where trust is built
- where the actual ask happens
- where reminders and follow-up happen
