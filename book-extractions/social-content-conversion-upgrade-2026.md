Internal synthesis derived from the previously reviewed book set and adapted for the `social-media-skills` repository.

## What matters most for this repository

### 1. Voice-of-customer should drive social and email copy

- Captions, campaign hooks, email subject lines, and WhatsApp broadcasts should mine customer language, objections, aspirations, and proof.
- Internal brainstorming is not enough for strong conversion copy.

### 2. CTA wording must match channel and commitment level

- A caption CTA, email CTA, and WhatsApp CTA are not interchangeable.
- Each should name the reward, reduce friction, and fit the audience's readiness.

### 3. Content systems should produce series and sequences

- Good social and email programmes are not random post banks.
- They are built from source buckets, repeatable series, and timed sequences.

### 4. Repurposing should support a journey, not just output volume

- One source asset should feed discovery, warming, proof, and conversion assets.
- Repurposing is strongest when each derivative points to the right next step.

### 5. Launches need stage-aware orchestration

- The durable lesson from *Launch* is sequencing:
  - tease
  - teach
  - prove
  - reveal
  - follow up
  - close
- This repo already has campaign and funnel skills, so the right move is to harden those with sequence logic, not add redundant skills.

## What to reject

- fake scarcity
- overhyped info-product tone for trust-sensitive brands
- generic hooks that could belong to any brand
- identical cross-posting across platforms
- repurposing that ignores channel role or audience intent
