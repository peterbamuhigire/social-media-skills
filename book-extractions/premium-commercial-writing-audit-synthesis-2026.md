## Synthesis

The existing repository already covers strong pieces of the writing system:

- `content-writing` handles readability, headlines, ledes, benefits, and general editorial quality.
- `caption-writer` handles platform conventions, caption variants, and hashtag discipline.
- `blog-writer` handles long-form article structure, SEO basics, and human voice standards.
- `direct-response-funnel-copy` handles offer sequences, sales letters, launch mechanics, and conversion architecture.
- `seo-geo-optimisation` handles AI-search citation structure and EEAT-style credibility.
- `premium-social-selling` handles high-ticket buyers, executive outreach, and premium social lead generation.

The gap was not another format-specific writer. The gap was a cross-cutting commercial quality layer that raises any asset from "correct content" to "premium professional writing".

## What The New Layer Adds

The `premium-commercial-writing` skill adds:

- message spine before drafting: reader, moment, pain, outcome, point of view, mechanism, proof, objection, next step
- format-specific gates for social posts, captions, campaigns, blogs, documents, sales copy, email, WhatsApp, and collateral
- search and authority guidance that connects premium writing with SEO/GEO and AI-search extractability
- value, proof, and offer architecture for premium fees, high-ticket conversion, and price integrity
- clear anti-patterns for generic, cheap-sounding, discount-led, or unsupported copy

## Commercial Writing Principles To Preserve

- Start from buyer reality, not brand adjectives.
- Use voice-of-customer language where available.
- Make the offer's mechanism visible.
- Prove claims with evidence, examples, process, or cited sources.
- Sequence attention, education, proof, objection handling, and CTA.
- Protect premium positioning by avoiding fake scarcity and casual discounting.
- Structure long-form content so humans and AI systems can extract the answer quickly.
- Treat social content as part of a journey, not isolated posts.

## Rejected Approaches

- copying book frameworks verbatim into skills
- importing proprietary templates from EPUBs
- adding a large theory-heavy skill that duplicates existing writers
- using "premium" as a tone adjective instead of a measurable quality gate
- replacing format-specific skills with one universal writer
