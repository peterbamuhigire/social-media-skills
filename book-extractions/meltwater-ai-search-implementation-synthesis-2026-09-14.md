# Meltwater AI-search claim implementation synthesis

Status: adopted on 2026-09-14 as qualified, directional evidence for social
and cross-channel content planning.

## Evidence disposition

The Meltwater and LinkedIn material reports an observational analysis of 9.5
million citations from B2B prompts across 16 categories and six named AI
products. It reports LinkedIn as a leading source in that sample, with
individual member profiles contributing more citations than Company Pages and
structured, question-led content appearing frequently among highly cited
examples. These findings are useful for hypotheses, not universal ranking
rules: the study is vendor-produced, sample-bound, and does not establish
causation, traffic, lead quality, or sales impact.

The reported figures must always retain their sample, denominator, date, and
source. The study does not justify claims that LinkedIn always outranks other
sources, that follower count is ignored, or that headings, lists, posting
frequency, freshness, or format guarantee AI citation.

## Social implementation rules

- Build a bench of credible topic owners. Use the person's real role,
  organisation, geography, and experience; never manufacture authority.
- Answer a real buyer question near the beginning. Prefer decision support,
  comparisons, how-to guidance, definitions, risks, costs, and implementation
  lessons where the audience needs them.
- Use clear native structure: a useful opening, descriptive sections or
  numbered steps, named entities, sourced data, caveats, and one honest next
  action. Structure serves readers first; it is not a ranking hack.
- Coordinate employee or expert posts with Company Page support, while keeping
  each post useful on its own and linking to the canonical website resource
  when the full explanation belongs there.
- Prefer original, experience-based material and labelled analysis over copied
  or lightly remixed filler. Record source, rights, approval, and claim status.
- Treat two to three LinkedIn posts per week, article frequency, and format
  mix as test variables from the report, not standing platform laws. Protect
  quality, reviewer capacity, and audience trust.
- Use independent discussion, reviews, and professional communities only
  through legitimate participation. Never manufacture UGC, reviews, comments,
  citations, or forum mentions.

## Measurement and experiment contract

Track content representation, observed AI citations, qualified engagement,
referral, and conversion separately. A prompt test should record the exact
prompt set, model/product, date, locale, answer, cited URLs, brand accuracy,
author/profile, content format, referral activity, and business outcome where
available. Compare like with like and do not change strategy from one cited
answer or one high-performing post.
