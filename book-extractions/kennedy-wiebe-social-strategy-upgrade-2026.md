# Kennedy And Wiebe Social Strategy Upgrade 2026

Internal synthesis of the reviewed EPUBs for `social-media-skills`.

## Sources reviewed

- Dan Kennedy, *Speak To Sell*
- Dan Kennedy, *Magnetic Marketing*
- Joanna Wiebe, *Where Stellar Messages Come From*
- Joanna Wiebe, *Headlines, Subheads & Value Propositions*
- Joanna Wiebe, *Buttons*
- Joanna Wiebe, *The Great Value Proposition Test*

## Durable principles worth encoding

### 1. Social strategy should start with market, message, media, and offer

- Pick the audience first.
- Then decide the repeated message.
- Then decide which channels can carry that message credibly.
- Then decide the offer and CTA at each stage.

### 2. Random posting is not a strategy

- Kennedy is strongest when he forces a lead-generation system.
- Social content should support attraction, conversion, retention, and referral instead of chasing reach alone.

### 3. Voice-of-customer should shape hooks, offers, and objections

- The best caption line, DM follow-up, and offer framing usually already exist in customer language.
- Mine comments, DMs, reviews, calls, and testimonials continuously.

### 4. CTAs should sell the value of the next step

- `Comment`, `DM`, and `click` are not enough by themselves.
- The audience should know what they get, how much effort it takes, and what happens next.

### 5. Proof and objection handling belong inside the content sequence

- Social proof should not be an occasional nice-to-have.
- It should be placed deliberately between education and offer posts, and repeated in follow-up.

### 6. Authority grows through teaching and diagnosis

- *Speak To Sell* reinforces a durable social lesson: authority comes from naming the problem better than the market, not from repeatedly declaring expertise.

## What to reject or qualify

- fake scarcity
- identical CTAs across all platforms
- campaigns where every channel repeats the same post with no role difference
- content calendars with no conversion or follow-up path

## Repository changes driven by this synthesis

- harden strategy docs around system design, not just posting cadence
- harden caption and CTA guidance around value and friction reduction
- strengthen channel sequencing with retention and referral steps
- strengthen social selling around message mining and objection capture
