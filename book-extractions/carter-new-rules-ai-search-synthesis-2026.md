## Durable operating ideas

- Use three response modes—remembered brand knowledge, retrieved/current
  information, and deeper reasoning—as a planning lens only. Do not present
  them as a fixed cross-platform taxonomy.
- Separate representation, retrieval, citation or answer contribution, referral,
  qualified action, and revenue. A social mention or AI answer is not proof of
  reach, traffic, trust, or sales.
- Make the brand legible: state who it is, what it does, for whom, where, and
  under what limits. Use consistent names, plain relationship sentences,
  evidence, dates, and authentic profiles. Never manufacture mentions, reviews,
  UGC, or “authority”.
- Let the content unit match the job: evergreen profile and offer facts;
  timely, sourced updates; deep expert explainers; and real experience from
  social/community surfaces with rights and moderation review.
- Baseline with customer language, comments/DMs, search and social analytics,
  referral data, “How did you find us?” responses, prompt observations, and
  available logs. Each is partial and must retain its definition and limits.
- Run one reversible experiment at a time with a hypothesis, primary outcome,
  trust/cultural/accessibility guardrail, stop rule, and rollback. A content
  calendar is not an experiment record.
- Prepare for agent-mediated tasks only when a real customer task exists:
  accurate profile/catalogue data, accessible landing pages, current feeds, and
  consent-safe handoffs matter more than speculative files or protocols.

## Corrections applied to social guidance

- Remove fixed universal rules such as “50 words”, “FAQ on every page”, a
  universal monthly cadence, three-second performance claims, or promised GEO
  lifts. Use them only as a documented client experiment or local acceptance
  choice, never as platform fact.
- Do not report “AI rank”. Name the observation: brand mention, cited source,
  sampled grounding query, sentiment, referral, qualified enquiry, or sale.
- Do not repeat the EPUB's unverified traffic, adoption, gender, market-share,
  tool, or platform numbers. If a claim matters, verify it against the current
  platform, regulator, market, or research source; otherwise mark it
  `NOT_ASSESSED`.
- Treat social profiles, reviews, comments, creator work, and UGC as part of the
  discoverability surface, but apply rights, privacy, moderation, cultural, and
  correction gates. Authenticity is a requirement, not a growth hack.
- For Google Search, use the official foundation: core SEO, helpful content,
  crawlability, technical eligibility, and Search Console measurement. Google's
  guide does not require `llms.txt`, artificial chunking, special AI markup, or
  inauthentic mentions: [Google guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).
- Treat Bing AI Performance as a documented public-preview measurement surface,
  not a ranking, authority, or business-value score: [Bing announcement](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview).

## Social implementation map

| Job | Social practice | Proof and guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Brand representation | Profile identity, offer, audience, location, limits, links, and pinned explanation are consistent across channels | owner-approved fact sheet, profile audit, update date |
| Retrieval support | Publish useful, time-stamped answers and link to the canonical page; use native descriptions and accessible text | source/rights record, canonical URL, no citation promise |
| Reasoning support | Expert threads, explainers, case evidence, comparisons, and transparent trade-offs | named author, sources, counter-case, moderation review |
| Off-site reputation | Earn real customer evidence and respond to corrections; never seed praise or suppress criticism | consent/rights, response log, escalation owner |
| Measurement | Track prompt observations separately from platform analytics, referrals, self-report, and qualified actions | metric definition, sample, date, attribution limits |
| Agent handoff | Link to an accurate, accessible destination and a consent-safe WhatsApp/form flow when appropriate | task scenario, privacy/legal check, failure path |
