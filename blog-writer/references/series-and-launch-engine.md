# Series And Launch Engine

Use this reference when a blog article must support discovery, warming, proof, or a timed campaign.

Primary sources:

- Ann Handley, *Everybody Writes*
- Jeff Walker, *Launch*

## Core rule

Treat a strong article as one stage in a larger journey.

## Article roles

- attract: teach or frame the problem
- warm: deepen trust and handle objections
- convert: support an offer, event, or launch action

## Launch-aware article sequence

1. problem framing
2. solution explanation
3. proof story
4. objection handling
5. deadline or event reminder

## Repurposing rule

Each article should leave behind:

- social post angles
- one email angle
- one CTA path to the next asset
